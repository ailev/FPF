# Development DPF Suite

> Methods for constructing, realizing and continuing development, and for providing the work that makes it possible.

**Development DPF Suite, 12 October 2026.** The membership below identifies the product series in this collection; each linked publication retains its own edition and conditions of use.

This Suite is for people developing themselves, leaders and participants changing organizations, providers helping others develop, and people supporting a practice among independent carriers. It connects several disciplines. It is not one universal development Method, an organizational chart or a requirement to create an office.

Start with the [Reference](DEVELOPMENT-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md) when the missing contribution is unclear. Open a known DPF or pattern directly when it already answers the question. A MethodDescription helps you perform work; neither reading it nor producing a plan establishes that the intended development occurred.

## Scope and identity

The ecosystem purpose is to make useful development attainable across personal, organizational, provider and cultural work. Development can begin with a faint difficulty rather than a known target practice. Its scope includes constructing a worthwhile attainable change, obtaining the needed contributions, realizing the change, continuing from the changed position, and improving the means of development when that improvement is worth its whole burden.

Routine work, learning or creating a Method, and implementing a selected practice can contribute without themselves constituting development. They also have valuable independent uses. A successful transition does not make endless activity obligatory: the development practice retains the ability to identify and pursue the next worthwhile attainable problem, while stopping an exhausted inquiry or ending an unhelpful intervention can be correct.

The Suite is a continuing collection of DPF product series. An edition belongs to its own product series; that series may belong to this Suite and another Suite. Inclusion does not copy the DPF, transfer ownership of its subject or make all its Methods necessary for every use. FPF remains the common source for transdisciplinary ontology and Methods, outside this collection of DPF series.

### Inclusion, review and removal

A proposed member must supply a developed, independently usable contribution to this ecosystem purpose, have an identifiable product-series rule and an obtainable admitted edition with source return, and connect usefully with another contribution or a distinct practitioner entry here. Shared vocabulary or one convenient citation is insufficient. A supporting source may remain outside the Suite when the narrower direct return is enough.

An inclusion decision takes effect only after those grounds are established for an actual product series. A loss of qualification requires a visible warning and a decision to repair, replace or remove the affected contribution; it does not silently erase membership. Removal preserves previous citation and use facts. If a product series ends or is reidentified as another series, the old belonging ends and a new inclusion is required.

The same Suite can add or remove members and change its Reference while preserving its purpose, inclusion rule and identity conditions. A replacement of those anchors outside this evolution rule identifies another Suite. A temporary one-member or empty state preserves this Suite only under an explicit preservation decision naming a restoration, review or retirement condition; no current cross-DPF answer is claimed in that state. Explicit retirement ends current belonging occurrences and preserves their historical facts. A later active collection requires a new constitution.

These rules promise no future maintenance service or continuous availability. Source changes, failed practitioner use and defeated connections reopen the affected guidance. Such review is not a demand to reevaluate every unchanged member.

Human Capability Development, Problem Structuring and Decision Support, Research Method Practice, Method Engineering and Organization Change Engineering are members of both Development and Engineering. The primary publication home is Development for HCD, PSD and RMP, and Engineering for ME and OCE. Each DPF edition has one full publication, reached from both Suite entries where membership is shared. The other five series are Development-only members. Engineering has direct external entries to the useful contributions of Development and Self-Development Practice, Community Management and Strategy. Shared membership does not copy a Method or make every useful dependency a member of both Suites.

Corporate governance, operations, accounting, marketing and other disciplines remain direct suppliers where the actual work needs their results. This first selection does not claim a smallest necessary set. A recurring whole-use or source responsibility can justify a later inclusion.

## Reference product

The **Development DPF Suite Reference** is a separately identified series of question-led Reference editions for these readers. It selects practical questions about this ecosystem, explains what the returned Methods or results contribute, and returns to the actual member editions, Core sources and the Suite account.

Its first edition is admitted only after its complete answers, source returns, boundaries and reader use are qualified. Later editions require a supported source-edition relation and admission while preserving these readers, use and source-return rules. A change to another ecosystem or a materially different independent use identifies another Reference series. Lost access or defeated advice requires the relevant warning, repair or retirement; publication and continuing maintenance are separate facts. Reference inclusion is a separate Suite decision and is not a prerequisite to direct use of an adequate DPF.

## Use and attribution

Read [Using FPF and its DPF Suites](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/USING-FPF.md) with the available publications. Keep complete relevant patterns available when adapting them.
