## Management.Reference:1 - The work this Suite serves

Management concerns choosing and sustaining the direction, commitments and provision of a collective undertaking, and changing them when the results or conditions warrant it. The undertaking may be a company, public service, professional practice, temporary collaboration or voluntary community. Its participants can have different purposes and decision rights. A shared employer is unnecessary; an actual basis for making and relying on a contribution is necessary.

Use this Suite when a worthwhile direction, an accepted promise, available means and ongoing work do not yet fit together. It helps obtain a supported commitment and its workable continuation: what can be done, for whom, by which contributions, with which authority and resources, and what must be reconsidered if a consequential condition changes. Retaining a sufficient arrangement, narrowing a promise or declining it can be useful results.

When an organization or another established system is the target, management's engineering contribution concerns that system: practitioners arrange and change the Work, authority, resources and commitments through which it can function. A changed allocation that establishes effective access or a new work commitment changes the system's state even if its organization chart remains the same. A voluntary community or temporary collaboration requires its own account of the system and whole, if any, that is being changed. System-level design, realization and qualification therefore remain relevant to ongoing management as well as to reorganization. OCE develops the deliberate construction of organizational relations and capability; it does not replace the other management fields or make every management use an OCE project. This organizational specialization is described by the Management Suite. The broader Engineering edition connects to it without repeating OCE in its direct roster.

The intended readers are people and capable assisting agents who make, support or examine management decisions. Relevant professional knowledge and powers remain necessary for the acts that require them. A practitioner can obtain a specialist result instead of learning to produce it. Follow [Using FPF Library](../../USING-FPF.md) to find the needed contribution and read its full pattern, including its conditions and worked cases.

## Management.Reference:3 - What makes these fields a whole

The common subject is the management of an undertaking through compatible direction, commitments, provision and performance. Its constituent contributions constrain the same acts and consequences. A customer promise sets work requirements; an operating answer tests whether those requirements can be met; a resource account explains the demand and payments; a governing act supplies the required authority. A strategic comparison must retain those conditions when selecting the undertaking. The relationships run back as well: a failed service, changed resource condition or refused contribution can require a different promise or direction.

For example, [MKT.11](MARKETING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#mkt11---align-promises-partners-and-execution) carries an offer into a real supply answer and returns an unsupported promise for revision. [OPS.13](OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ops13---align-commitments-resources-and-service-outcomes) preserves existing obligations while comparing scope, time or resource changes. [MA.1](MANAGEMENT-ACCOUNTING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ma1---build-the-resource-consumption-and-cost-model) explains the resource consumption that both uses require; [FIN.2](CORPORATE-FINANCE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#fin2---assess-liquidity-and-funding-needs-by-date) establishes the corporation's dated funding position where that question is live. [CGOV.11](CORPORATE-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#cgov11---make-and-record-a-corporate-decision) makes the corporate decision when its conditions apply, while [CB.13](COMMUNITY-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#cb13---establish-a-workable-mandate-for-shared-decisions) supplies a community mandate. [LDR.2](LEADERSHIP-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ldr2---agree-feasible-joint-contributions) obtains responses to the same complete proposal and returns changed contributions to the parties affected by them. These are connections between complete Methods, with different results and sources of authority.

[Customization and Productization Management (CUST)](CUSTOMIZATION-AND-PRODUCTIZATION-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) connects differences encountered in client work to a maintainable local answer or repeatable offering. A successful local result can depend on exceptional expertise, restricted material or a configuration that cannot be supported elsewhere. CUST.1 explains the connected Method; CUST.2–CUST.8 are also directly usable when only their particular result is missing.

An offering family constructed through CUST.3 constrains which variations MKT.11 may promise. A qualified delivery arrangement from CUST.6 supplies OPS.13 with its demand on performers, partners and support. CUST.7 uses the full resource and economic comparison of MA.8 to examine growth in demand and variety, including adaptation and continuing maintenance. These consequences can require a different family or a narrower promise. CUST.8 connects a changed general offering to the transitions still owed to existing clients. A successful new configuration does not erase those obligations.


At the portfolio scale, [STR.16](STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str16---construct-a-feasible-portfolio-with-shared-or-branch-specific-support) constructs shared or separate support while preserving the purposes and evidence of each branch. [STR.8](STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str8---relate-capabilities-dependencies-business-models-and-initiatives) supplies result dependencies, [STR.14](STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str14---reconcile-simultaneous-strategic-work-across-horizons-and-scales) reconciles competing work across horizons, and [STR.11](STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str11---select-strategy-portfolio-commitments-and-assumptions) carries the supported comparison into an authorized commitment. For an asset-dependent undertaking, [EAM.12](ENGINEERING-ASSET-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#eam12---integrate-specialist-results-for-an-asset-recommendation-or-decision) receives the specialist results needed for the asset answer; it returns a missing engineering, operating or financial premise to its supplier.

These connected Methods help practitioners construct and revise **jointly supportable management commitments**. A good direction, a valid budget and an achievable local schedule can still be incompatible when they rely on the same unavailable resource, an unmade decision or a participant who has not agreed. Examining the whole means retaining these shared conditions and their consequences through the actual decision.

The whole also connects knowledge to practice. Knowledge of demand, resource consumption, authority, incentives and participation informs the construction and criticism of commitments. Results of practice can expose a bad assumption, an inappropriate measure or a missing condition. [MA.9](MANAGEMENT-ACCOUNTING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ma9---examine-the-behavioral-effects-of-management-accounting-information) develops that return for the use of accounting information; [OPS.22](OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ops22---interpret-operating-performance-and-decide-the-response) for operating performance; [STR.12](STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str12---observe-signals-to-continue-or-reconsider-strategic-direction-and-commitments) for strategic premises; and [CB.10](COMMUNITY-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#cb10---understand-the-value-of-participation-and-cultural-work) for participation. A changed observation first reopens the claim it bears on. A general causal conclusion needs the corresponding inquiry and evidence.

This Suite uses those existing full Methods for the connections it describes. This Architectural Reference helps a reader find and understand their joint use; the linked patterns supply the operations, cases and limits needed to perform it.

### Management.Reference:3.1 - Organizational construction and maintenance use

Management includes constructing and changing organizational arrangements as well as directing work through adequate existing ones. OCE develops this constituent subject and has its full home in the Management Suite. Its organizational target connects the construction to direction, commitments, authority, provision and performance. An asset-dependent undertaking also needs maintenance policy and programme work; it obtains the full MNT Methods through [Engineering (Engineering.Reference:0)](../Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/ENGINEERING-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md#engineeringreference0---engineering-specialization-and-maintenance). This use retains the maintenance field's own failure, condition and functioning questions.

**Organizational construction.** OCE connects knowledge of specialization, coordination, information, incentives and participation to the deliberate construction of organizational relations and capability. OCE.3 and OCE.4 construct interacting organization choices, rather than accepting an organization chart as the complete answer. OCE.5 and OCE.6 distinguish established positions from assignments and other enabling relations. OCE.9 realizes a bounded contribution; OCE.13 and OCE.14 return consequences to the choice of relations.

Those relations determine which management commitments can be made and sustained. A direction requiring independent acceptance needs an arrangement that supports independent judgement. A promise requiring the same specialist for incompatible work needs a different allocation, support or promise. Changed demand, authority or participation can reopen the organization design; the design's consequences can reopen direction and provision. Together with Strategy, Operations, Administration, Governance and Leadership, this contribution supports jointly feasible commitments rather than a set of separately plausible plans.

Engineering uses the same organizational knowledge and constructions to obtain functioning systems: OCE.7 coordinates organization and product or service architectures, while OCE.9 and OCE.11 connect realization to the conditions of use and continuing service. These contributions remain engineering through their organizational specialization in Management; this does not require a second direct inclusion of OCE in the broader Engineering edition. OCE does not cover all Organization Development, nor does a management task require organizational redesign whenever it uses this Suite.

**Maintenance.** MNT's subject is preserving or restoring required functioning, especially for physical and cyber-physical equipment in use. Maintenance engineering and maintenance management have distinct results within this connected field. The technical failure account constrains the policy and response; support, access and service conditions constrain which technically plausible response can be performed.

MNT.2 makes this connection within policy construction. A useful warning must leave time for detection, interpretation, support, access and intervention. MNT.5 and MNT.7 establish the support and operating arrangement needed by that response. MNT.8–MNT.11 retain separate protection, intervention, functioning and resumed-use results. MNT.12 and MNT.13 connect actual configuration and comparable failure history to choices about policy, support or programme. A recurring support delay calls for a different response from a failure mechanism that the task cannot address.

In management work, this field connects the operating promise to maintenance demands, obtainable provision and a programme that can change on evidence. It receives qualified capacity from Operations and relevant resource, funding and administrative results from their own practices. Its maintenance knowledge prevents an apparently affordable programme from promising unsupported availability. In Engineering, that same knowledge and practice constrain maintainability, realization and restored functioning; evidence from use can reopen the design. Neither relationship depends on every undertaking having equipment to maintain.

Maintenance does not absorb Engineering Asset Management. For a single pump, selecting a policy or intervention to preserve required functioning is a maintenance result. Comparing continued use, renewal, replacement, repurposing or withdrawal by value, costs, risks and service effects is an asset-management result. Conversely, coordinating a fleet maintenance programme remains MNT.13. Scale, the word replacement and the practitioner's job title do not decide the subject; one case can require both contributions.

OCE is a constituent because constructing organizational relations and capability participates in the same organizational subject as direction, commitments, authority and provision. MNT's whole subject instead connects failure and condition knowledge to maintaining required functioning. Keeping MNT in Engineering, with its full Methods available to management practitioners, preserves every policy, support and programme connection described here. The manager can perform a maintenance Method with the required capability; using an external discipline does not mean outsourcing the work. Dividing either complete repertoire into separate engineering and management products would obscure the interactions within OCE's organization choices and MNT's policy and response.

The cross-Suite arrangement requires users to preserve the conditions on both contributions. A changed organizational premise can affect both system realization and management commitments; a changed maintenance premise can affect both functioning and the service programme. Each Suite explains its own receiving connections. A user following a changed premise may therefore need both accounts, although the full Method remains in one description.

## Management.Reference:4 - Development, shared support and effective commitments

Management can support the development of an undertaking, its participants and the practices they use. Its contribution is to make the selected work supportable and keep its commitments connected. The development question remains what new problems become attainable and what changes make that possible; a sound allocation does not answer it. [FPF C.40.OD](../../FPF-Spec.md#c40od---construct-and-continue-open-ended-development) supplies the open-ended construction, [FPF C.40.JD](../../FPF-Spec.md#c40jd---develop-a-way-its-holder-and-its-provision-together) connects a way of obtaining a result with the development of its holder and provision, and [FPF C.40.NP](../../FPF-Spec.md#c40np---continue-from-a-result-to-new-attainable-problems) continues from a useful result to another worthwhile attainable problem. A bounded management decision can finish while that development continues.

### Management.Reference:4.1 - Shared support connects branches without merging their purposes

Research, product provision, human development, infrastructure work and community work can each be worth undertaking while competing for the same performers. Ranking each direction separately can promise incompatible work or charge the same common provision several times. The management whole needs both the reasons for pursuing the branches and a feasible account of their shared means.

[STR.16](STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str16---construct-a-feasible-portfolio-with-shared-or-branch-specific-support) constructs shared versus branch-specific support, joint demand and feasible combinations. It preserves scientific and editorial ends and decisions, and can use an adequate existing Strategy result directly. For a development provider, the comparison can include its own development, a shared Method library, independent scientific contributions, business-to-consumer and business-to-business provision, client projects and work involving people, AI and communities alongside current obligations. These are possible branches of one provider's portfolio, not a required organization of development.

A shared office is one possible realization of a common contribution. The common part must remain usable for each receiver; each branch still needs its subject interpretation, acceptance and any distinct support. Shared maintenance can save repeated work while adding coordination, waiting, adaptation and a common failure exposure. Provision within each branch can be preferable when specialist criteria, confidentiality, independent judgement or incompatible calendars make sharing unsuitable. Either arrangement can use distributed performers instead of an office.

The provider's remit is a separate choice: a service specializing in development can obtain other subject expertise externally and still supply its own contribution. C.40.JD:4.5 explains that distinction; STR.16:4.3 develops the sharing comparison. [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../FPF-Spec.md#c32mwapp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-provision-arrangements) supplies the performance and provision architecture when those arrangements still need construction; [OCE.4](ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#oce4---design-an-organizations-contribution-architecture) supplies an organizational contribution architecture when an organization boundary must be designed. Neither a portfolio nor an office name establishes the needed capability or authority.

Demands on the same performer must be reconciled against that performer's availability, even when that person fills several roles. A research branch's assigned cost share is also different from a payment avoided by cancelling it. STR.16:4.4 retains those distinctions and the actual time windows. A result dependency is another relation: a product needs new research only where a specific receiving operation cannot proceed with an adequate existing or alternative result. Independent inquiry can remain worth supporting on its own grounds.

Independent mathematical inquiry with no material shared dependency need not acquire a portfolio or office merely to use a development Method. The management comparison stops with the sufficient bounded decision and reopens the affected combination when a shared condition changes. When continued development is part of the purpose, its next problem comes from the development construction rather than from a requirement to perpetuate the management project.

### Management.Reference:4.2 - A proposal reaches work through judgement, decisions and contributions

An accepted strategy does not supply all the contributions needed to enact it. Telling people about the strategy can leave both a substantive objection and an incompatible promise unanswered. The connection is through the participants' actual work, authority and response to the same proposal.

[LDR.1](LEADERSHIP-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ldr1---obtain-and-continue-the-leadership-contribution-needed-for-joint-work) obtains and continues a missing leadership contribution. [LDR.2](LEADERSHIP-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ldr2---agree-feasible-joint-contributions) negotiates complete feasible contributions. [LDR.4](LEADERSHIP-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ldr4---prepare-a-proposal-for-decision-and-first-use) prepares a proposal through decision, commitments, a mutually understood start and first useful work. This last contribution includes what is sometimes called *propitka*: relevant trusted judgement and examination of consequences can change the proposal before commitment, and first use tests what the agreement actually provided. An adviser may help a participant judge the proposal without having authority to decide or commit on that participant's behalf.

When a subject question still needs a useful answer, [FPF B.5.SD](../../FPF-Spec.md#b5sd---construct-a-useful-answer-through-substantive-discussion) supplies its construction through substantive discussion. An unsupported inference needs subject reasoning; a real disadvantage can require another proposal; incompatible contributions need an agreement that can be performed. These returns do not make agreement a test of technical truth or make a technically sound proposal acceptable to every participant. Informed refusal and a decision under a valid non-consensual rule remain different results.

LDR.4 supplies the whole passage when that is missing. One of the other Methods can be used directly when only its contribution is needed. A voluntary community need not share an employer: [CB.13](COMMUNITY-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#cb13---establish-a-workable-mandate-for-shared-decisions) can establish the shared mandate, while participants retain powers they have not delegated. In a corporation, the applicable governance acts retain their own conditions. A person or AI may perform an authorized contribution; fluent analysis or a simulated preference does not supply another party's consent. Ordinary sufficient coordination needs no special preparation campaign.

The first useful handover supplies evidence at its actual scope. Continued help can remain part of the arrangement; success with that help does not show independent mastery. When the first use fails, LDR.4 returns the missing explanation, capability, means, access, authority or Method to its supplier instead of prescribing another announcement.

### Management.Reference:4.3 - Connected documents express compatible results

A provider may need a concept, strategy, project architecture and programmes for its own development and its capacity to help clients. Those documents describe different subjects and decisions. Their usefulness depends on the compatible contributions they describe, rather than on adopting the same layout or copying one framework's contents table.

[FPF C.40.OD:4.8](../../FPF-Spec.md#c40od48---make-the-way-reconstructible-in-further-use) identifies the decisions and Methods that must remain available at the point of use. ### Management.Reference:5.3 - An AI-assisted experimental research programme

A laboratory is considering AI-assisted research on an enzyme. It could seek a useful variant with a specified property, a better predictive model or an explanation of a mechanism. Those are different results: finding a good variant need not distinguish two explanations of why it works. RMP.2 constructs the inquiry appropriate to the intended claim; STR.7 asks whether obtaining that knowledge warrants the resources and delay. Management must preserve that distinction when agreeing the programme's result.

Suppose the immediate commitment is a bounded search for variants that retain a required activity after a specified treatment. The assay, admissible variation, uncertainty and independent confirmation must come from qualified research and engineering work. OCE.8 compares complete available arrangements: a scientist using existing tools, a specialist optimizer connected to laboratory automation, an LLM with scientific tools, and a mixed arrangement. It includes preparation, failed attempts, laboratory time, checking and interpretation. Adding AI to existing job descriptions is only one candidate. OCE.3 constructs other divisions of the work; LDR.2 obtains feasible contributions rather than assuming that an organization chart supplies them.

The distinction is supported by two different research lines. [Co-Scientist, Gottweis and colleagues, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10644-y), develops hypotheses through generation, criticism, ranking and revision, with scientists and separate experimental validation. A hypothesis's ranking is not experimental evidence for its truth. [SAMPLE, Rapp and colleagues, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44286-023-00002-4), instead demonstrates a specialized model–experiment loop for a bounded protein search. It models functionality as well as the property being optimized. These are candidates for different contributions, not two interchangeable demonstrations that an autonomous laboratory is generally superior.

Here is a constructed capacity example, not a report of either study. A week's provision permits twelve assay slots. The chosen protocol requires two control slots and reserves two for independent confirmation, leaving eight for exploratory trials. Raising candidate generation from twenty to eighty variants does not create another assay slot. MMP.16 supplies the comparison of obtainable observations and their value for the selected unknown; RMP.3 makes the chosen design executable. OPS.9 and OPS.18 relate that design to the shared bench and other commitments. The next useful commitment may be eight selected trials, a different experimental design, extra qualified capacity or a decision not to proceed. The counts alone do not select among them.

Different returns reopen different contributions. If a dispensing failure prevents one assay, RMP.4 preserves the failed execution and its conditions; the optimizer must not receive an invented zero-activity measurement. The bench arrangement is repaired or the run is repeated when warranted. If a valid assay falls below the activity threshold, it is evidence about that variant under those conditions, not by itself a refutation of a wider mechanism. If an attractive scalar score conflicts with the raw observations, RMP.6 and the measurement construction must resolve whether the observation is unusable, the transformation is inappropriate or the model needs revision. An unusual signal cannot be discarded merely because it disagrees with the current model.

That last distinction matters in autonomous search. [Harris and colleagues, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-024-01485-2), show how checking raw observations and their reduction to a scalar can change the next experiment selected by Bayesian optimization. Their synthetic and surrogate-data comparisons support a design alternative for oversight, not a demonstrated improvement of this laboratory. A qualified observer, its access to raw data and its consequences for the next proposal belong in the complete arrangement being compared.

The chosen workflow therefore connects hypothesis or candidate proposal, experiment selection, physically obtainable execution, interpretation of the actual return, model or claim revision, and separate confirmation. SYSE.47 constructs a tool-using procedure where automation is needed; SYSE.46 tests the complete configuration under representative conditions. A protocol that can call an instrument still needs the instrument's qualified behavior and the authority to use it. ADM.8 applies an adequate existing permission or obtains a missing one; it does not supply scientific validity. STR.7 and the responsible management decision then use the qualified research return to continue, change or stop the commitment.

This case explains why the Suite connects these management contributions and receives research, mathematical and engineering results without absorbing their disciplines. It does not provide an assay or a universal laboratory Method. A usable local arrangement still needs the complete supplying Methods, its domain models and explicit treatment of each consequential return. A successful variant, a better model and an explained mechanism remain separately warranted achievements.

## Management.Reference:7 - Continuity and coverage

Management retains its disciplinary identity while its theories, tools, techniques and institutional forms change, provided that its subject and essential knowledge–practice connections remain: direction and commitments are related to legitimate acts, obtainable means, actual performance and warranted reconsideration. An adequate replacement for a constituent method must preserve the contribution on which the remaining work relies. For example, a different forecasting method can replace one used in an operating promise if its population, capacity conditions, uncertainty and receiving use remain adequately supported.

For OCE, a replacement organization model or Method preserves its constituent contribution here when organizational knowledge remains connected to constructed relations, capability, commitments and their observed consequences. For an MNT contribution used here, a replacement policy or technology must preserve the connection between required functioning, supported technical reasoning, feasible provision and feedback to the programme. Both retain their engineering conditions. A change to one assignment, machine or service condition reopens the affected uses. Extending maintenance to all organizational change or to asset-value decisions would change its bounded subject.

An account that removes authority from the commitment question, separates resource knowledge from the work it explains, or replaces voluntary participation with an assumed right to assign people loses an essential connection of the management field described here. Extending coverage to another management branch can preserve that whole when its distinct subject, contributions and consequential connections are established.

This edition offers a connected selection of management fields. Marketing develops the customer-situation and feasible-offer branch; CUST develops the relation between client variation, repeatable offerings and their continuing obligations; Finance and Governance retain their corporate scope. The Suite does not provide complete coverage of organizational behavior, employment and human-resource management, public administration, international management or supply-chain management. Obtain the appropriate professional contribution when a task depends on those subjects. The boundaries of each included DPF remain applicable.

Each constituent retains its own sources, cases and conditions for reconsideration. New knowledge, a changed institutional condition or a revised Method reopens the other contributions that rely on it. Preserve independently adequate knowledge and practices while correcting the affected connection.

### Management.Reference:8.3 - What the comparison establishes

The synthesis favors comparing complete applicable arrangements over importing a fixed organization form. It preserves more than one scale of judgement: the quality and cost of a particular contribution, the supportable flow of work, the resulting commitment, and its value in use. New capabilities can change the work worth attempting; STR.17 reopens the strategic question when a local efficiency comparison no longer represents that change.

For example, when AI changes which tasks are attempted and several agents share reviewers or laboratory equipment, a time-per-task comparison is insufficient. A useful comparison must keep the original needs and declined work visible, distinguish comparable tasks from the changed undertaking, account for shared resources and retain checking and subsequent losses. Its design needs the relevant measurement and causal-inference expertise. An observed improvement, a locally useful arrangement and an established causal effect are different conclusions. Neither this Reference nor a positive study supplies that design for every undertaking.

The older source is retained where its construction remains adequate; a newer source reopens the affected decision when it supplies a better alternative or defeats a premise. Conversely, a new date, a leading company's name or institutional endorsement is not a sufficient selection rule. Reconsider the arrangement when its result, task mix, technology, limiting resource, authority or consequential horizon changes. These comparisons justify specific questions and alternatives; they do not assign a single state-of-the-art rank to every Method in the Suite.
