This account explains the [Human Capability Engineering DPF Suite](README.md) to people and capable assisting agents choosing or connecting its Methods. It describes the learning and somatic part supplied by HCD and SOM, the larger engineering subject to which that part contributes, and the boundaries that change a user's next move. A familiar Method can be used directly without reading the account in order.

The Suite contributes to the Library’s [canon for a shared human–AI culture of thinking and acting](../../FPF-LIBRARY-REFERENCE.md#libraryreference13---a-canon-for-shared-humanai-culture) by developing people’s capabilities for present work and open-ended development. Its receiving result can be a person’s better participation in a team whose AI agents perform other contributions. Specify the capability the person needs and the support that remains available. Human learning and somatic change retain their own methods; developing an AI agent or maintaining its attention and execution requires the appropriate engineering contribution. A successful joint performance and a person’s acquired capability are distinct results.

## HCE.Reference:1 - What is being changed, and for which result?

Human Capability Engineering concerns the deliberate formation, change, restoration and maintenance of a person's capabilities. Its knowledge concerns human mechanisms and conditions of change; its practice constructs and tests ways of obtaining the required capability. A sufficient result can be a supported programme comparison or a justified decision to keep the present arrangement. A claim that the person has changed requires evidence of that change.

The object being changed and the result sought are separate questions. Current regulation of work, capability change and open-ended growth can each concern a person, an organization or a robot. In this Suite, the human is the capability holder. Its first edition supplies learning and bodily-modeling Methods for that target. The person may also be the practitioner carrying out the change, but a teacher, AI assistant or provider organization can make other contributions.

| Distinction | Consequence for choosing and using a Method |
| --- | --- |
| Target of change | A person's capability, an organization's capability and a robot's capability require different accounts of what changes and how it can change. |
| Performer of the helping work | A person, AI or organization may provide a contribution. That choice changes the help available and its conditions; it need not change who is learning. |
| Result and recipient | The person may acquire an ability, a customer may receive a successful service, or a team may obtain a joint result. These results have different bearers and evidence. |
| Mechanism | Human perception, practice and bodily regulation differ from parameter training, controller design and actuator change. |

For example, a person can produce an adequate report with an expert's help while still lacking the expert's diagnostic ability. The joint arrangement may be sufficient for the job. If personal capability is needed, [HCD.11](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd11--assess-human-performance-in-representative-work) and [HCD.13](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd13--test-human-capability-retention-and-dependence-on-tools-or-other-support) keep performance and support dependence identifiable. Withdrawing useful help is a separate choice, not an automatic improvement.

Regulating today's attempt can obtain today's result without establishing a lasting capability change. Learning an existing Method can establish the requested capability and finish there. Open-ended development connects such gains to new worthwhile attainable problems and, where needed, the development of ways to solve them; [FPF C.40.OD](../../FPF-Spec.md#c40od---construct-and-continue-open-ended-development) supplies that larger construction. HCD.15 supplies a continuing human-capability arrangement when its conditions call for one.

## HCE.Reference:2 - Why combine HCD and SOM?

The practical difficulty is a gap between a planned human contribution and the mechanisms by which the person can acquire and sustain it. A general learning programme may leave the needed bodily action unspecified. Conversely, a useful bodily distinction can remain an isolated achievement that does not enter the person's work. The two fields connect those questions while retaining their distinct knowledge and Methods.

[Human Capability Development (HCD)](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) relates required later work, the person's starting conditions, feasible development arrangements and evidence of performance. [HCD.2](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd2---compose-and-compare-human-capability-development-programmes-for-later-work) compares programme candidates with their complete contributions, burdens and support. HCD.5 selects a suitable development Method; HCD.6 and HCD.10 address representative practice and its variation. HCD.11–HCD.13 examine performance, transfer and retention under the conditions required by the intended use. HCD.14 uses the resulting observations to reconsider the relevant assumptions and revise the affected model or development arrangement. The full language also includes learning products and shared learning, whose results remain distinct from one person's acquired capability.

[Somatic Movement Modeling (SOM)](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) supplies a specialized human contribution: relating bodily experience, voluntary regulation and observed movement within an already known practice. [SOM.1](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#som1---model-the-bodily-contribution-to-a-movement) develops that relation; SOM.2–SOM.5 supply distinguishable changes, effort regulation, coordinated action and adaptation to support or load. Technique and suitable physical demands come from the movement practice and competent instruction.

The intended whole action constrains which local distinction is useful. What the learner can actually distinguish and regulate constrains the programme's feasible practice task and support. The task's other demands constrain whether a correction can be used at the same time. Observed action then returns to both the bodily account and the programme comparison. A failure under changed support can require another somatic construction; a failure caused by unavailable instruction calls for another support arrangement.

This knowledge–practice connection also permits improvement of the Methods. Knowledge about learning, attention and bodily control constrains the proposed attempt. Its interpretable result can expose a mistaken premise or an unsuitable cue. The observation bears first on that particular claim; a general effectiveness claim requires evidence at that broader scope.

The useful property of the combined knowledge and Methods is **how well they guide the practitioner in designing and revising a feasible capability change for the person's intended action**. Judging that property requires considering the combined use: a feasible timetable, a recognized sensation and a completed exercise can coexist with an unavailable whole action. Burden, concurrent demands, support dependence and retention have to remain visible where they affect that use. A capability question that one DPF can settle need not use both.

## HCE.Reference:3 - How do the contributions meet in a whole action?

Consider the dancer in [HCD.Reference:4](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcdreference4---several-contributions-can-be-simultaneous): strength is adequate, the stationary axis is available and the figure is remembered, but balance fails during the required rotation. [SOM.Reference:4](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#somreference4---the-simultaneous-vertical-of-movement) explains the simultaneous figure, rhythmic steps, changing support and bodily regulation. This is a constructed use of those full explanations, not an observed learning result.

The first useful answer is the contribution that limits the action. [HCD.3](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd3--diagnose-whether-a-capability-misconception-or-behaviour-limits-work) can locate a supported coordination target while preserving the already adequate contributions. If the bodily relation remains unclear, SOM.1 connects what the dancer experiences, can deliberately change and actually does. A label such as poor balance does not yet supply that relation or its correction.

SOM's full Methods provide different available constructions. [SOM.2](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#som2---make-a-bodily-change-distinguishable) is relevant when a needed change cannot be distinguished; [SOM.3](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#som3---regulate-bodily-effort-in-discernible-steps) when the increase or release of effort cannot yet be regulated; [SOM.4](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#som4---build-and-recall-a-coordinated-bodily-change) when available changes do not work together; and [SOM.5](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#som5---adapt-bodily-coordination-to-changing-support-and-load) when support or load changes defeat the coordination. These are alternatives and returns within the repertoire, not four compulsory exercises.

HCD.2 includes the selected development contribution together with the teacher, practice conditions and burden. HCD.6 and HCD.10 connect preparation to the whole figure and the variation its later use needs. A reduced task may temporarily remove the partner or timing demand; the later combination still has to become possible. A local correction that displaces the steps or prevents the partner's continuation fails the intended use.

[HCD.11](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd11--assess-human-performance-in-representative-work) supplies the performance judgement. [HCD.12](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd12--test-transfer-to-unfamiliar-work-or-learning-from-a-new-source) and HCD.13 answer material transfer or retention questions when those are needed. A failed attempt at the whole action returns to the particular bodily, practice or support premise through HCD.14 and the relevant SOM Method. There is no need to complete all these evaluations when a smaller supported answer settles the current decision.

A different cause changes the route. Missing technique requires instruction; insufficient strength is not supplied by refining a sensation. A tool or surface change can make the action available without new bodily learning, as the writing case in [SOM.Reference:3](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#somreference3---a-connected-worked-use) shows. Once the needed action works under its required conditions, further detail needs a reason. The teaching and safety conditions of the full SOM Methods continue to apply.

## HCE.Reference:4 - What changes when a teacher, learner, instrument or listener changes?

Replacing one participant can leave the target of capability change intact or alter the subject enough to require another Method. The following comparisons show which distinctions affect the choice.

| Changed participant or condition | What remains and what must be reconsidered |
| --- | --- |
| A teacher is replaced by an AI assistant | The human can remain the learner. [HCD.7](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd7---arrange-providers-access-tools-and-ai-support-for-human-capability-development) addresses the needed provider, observation, feedback, human-check and fallback contributions. The assistant's ability to discuss a movement does not supply physical observation or teaching competence it lacks. |
| The learner is replaced by a robot | A movement criterion may remain useful, but the changed holder needs its own sensing, controller, learning and actuator Methods. SOM's first-person bodily distinctions do not construct that mechanism. The human and robotic results can be compared only on the criteria that actually apply to both. |
| An acoustic instrument is replaced by a sampler or DJ system | The performing configuration and its control demands change. [MDPE.3](../MUSIC-AND-DANCE-PRACTICE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#mdpe3---configure-and-coordinate-a-music-or-dance-performing-whole) supplies the configuration question. HCD is needed only for a selected change in a person's contribution; different motor demands can remove one learning need and create another. |
| A listener or other recipient changes | Recognizable or valued results may change even when the performer can still execute the material. The receiving judgement belongs to the artistic practice; a personal capability change is a separate question. |
| The learning mechanism changes | Comparable performance can be pursued through human practice and biological adaptation, statistical model training or controller redesign. Different mechanisms require different operations and evidence. |

Ease of listening and ease of performing can therefore favor different variants. Changed instruments, learning conditions or participant populations can change later cultural selection. [MDPE.20](../MUSIC-AND-DANCE-PRACTICE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#mdpe20---forecast-how-a-music-or-dance-practice-may-develop), especially section 3.4, distinguishes perceptual and reconstructive, bodily and robotic, capability-development, recognition and transmission mechanisms. That complete Method supplies the cultural-trajectory question. One person's learning result alone does not establish the trajectory of a practice population.

### HCE.Reference:4.1 - Distinguish the professional targets in human development

Professional names in learning work can refer to different targets. The *Person Engineering* source uses an engineering vocabulary to distinguish contributions to developing a person, designing learning and providing the work. Its useful question is what changes, through which method, and with which resulting capability. Apply that question before equating similarly named architects, designers, methodologists or technologists.

The person remains an agent with their own purposes, choices and contribution to learning. A provider can offer a route, establish conditions within its mandate and supply help. An employer can specify an organizational need. These contributions meet in an arrangement that the actual participants can undertake. A promise to develop a particular person's capability requires their learning and evidence of their performance under the intended conditions. An already adequate capability can be used directly.

Choosing a worthwhile educational direction is itself a professional contribution. Its object is the proposed connection between a future activity or possibility and the mastery worth developing for it. In the source, the cultural-promoter contribution brings prospective mastery into educational strategy. This calls for knowledge of the practice and its possible changes, the people who could use the mastery, their alternatives, and the effort and provision its development would require. Forecasts remain qualified by their grounds and horizon. A popular course topic can be a promising lead without yet establishing that connection.

Recover whose purpose makes the direction worthwhile. A person may want a different profession, research capability or a personally meaningful practice. An employer may need a contribution to its future work. A provider may seek a sustainable educational offer. These purposes can support one undertaking while remaining different. An employer's requirement does not establish the person's wider purpose; interest in a provider's offer does not establish its educational benefit. [Management.Reference:4.4](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/MANAGEMENT-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md#managementreference44---personal-and-corporate-development) explains this connection between personal and corporate development.

Obtain a reasoned choice or a precise question that prevents one. [C.40.PC](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c40pc---construct-a-worthwhile-problem-and-proposed-contribution) separates the worthwhile difference from a preferred course or technology and gives the proposed contribution an inspectable receiving use. Strategy supplies comparison of future directions and their support; marketing inquiry can establish which people want or could use an offer and on what terms. Subject-method expertise must still establish what the proposed mastery enables. Compare learning with adequate existing capability, useful tools or expert support, and a changed aim where those alternatives could answer the actual purpose. Inquiry can reject a direction, retain it as a hypothesis or support proceeding. An individually worthwhile purpose need not be justified by an employer's return or a course market.

The result is received by the person choosing their development, by those agreeing a joint undertaking, and by the professionals designing or offering learning. It explains which prospective contribution matters, for whom, under which conditions and uncertainties, and why its development is worth pursuing. HCD.1 can then derive the human demand from representative later work; HCD.28 can construct a learning product for the intended audience. Subject-method work supplies the practice's content and criteria, learning design makes its acquisition feasible, and provision establishes the resources and access. These contributions can refine the direction together: discovering that a supposed benefit depends on an unavailable method or exceeds an acceptable burden reopens its basis before further course construction.

Three targets make the professional differences concrete:

| Target being designed or changed | Professional result | Knowledge and practical consequences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A person's connected capabilities | An account of the contributions that person needs, how they depend on one another, and feasible ways to develop or support them. | HCD.1 derives the demand from later work; HCD.2 compares programmes. The person's preparation, purposes, permitted help and ability to participate constrain the design. |
| A learning programme or product | A coherent route through explanations, practice, feedback and assessment that can support the selected learning. | Subject knowledge determines what is to be learned and what counts as adequate performance; learning and explanation methods determine how to make it learnable under the actual conditions. |
| The provider organization and its platform | An obtainable teaching and support service with competent participants, access, means, time and workable exceptions. | OCE, operations and HCD.7 connect the provider arrangement to the learning work. A well-designed course can remain unavailable if these contributions are missing. |

An architect working on a person's capability structure can ask how diagnostic judgement, communication and permitted tool use must work together in later practice. A programme architect can ask how the learning experiences and assessments develop those contributions within a feasible route. A provider architect can ask how subject specialists, teachers, facilities and support make that route available. One person may perform all three contributions. The objects, relevant knowledge and results remain different.

The broader phrase architecture of a person or personality may include aspirations, identity, values or other changes beyond this Suite's learning and somatic methods. Recover the intended target and obtain its appropriate grounds. HCD can contribute to a specified capability demand without claiming a complete method for changing a person's life or personality. The person's own choices can also change the target: an adequate joint work result does not remove their reason to pursue an individually meaningful capability.

### HCE.Reference:4.2 - Connect subject expertise, learning design and teaching

The source's methodological vocabulary contains several possible contributions. Developing a subject method asks how the target practice obtains its result. Synthesizing the functional behavior of a selected engineered system is the narrower applied-methodologist contribution explained in Engineering.Reference:0.8.1. Developing a learning method asks how a person can acquire and adapt a needed contribution. Designing a programme, producing its materials, arranging its delivery and conducting a teaching interaction add further work. Recover the object and result when a local title such as methodologist, instructional designer or learning technologist could refer to more than one of them.

Subject expertise determines the important distinctions, valid operations, typical errors and meaningful variation in the target work. A teaching method uses that content to arrange attempts, feedback and correction that can change the learner's capability. Explanation design helps the learner recover the meaning and reasons for an operation. Production and technical provision make the materials and interaction available. None can be inferred from the quality of another contribution.

The teaching team therefore needs sufficient current subject understanding as well as learning expertise. These can be combined in one person or obtained through closely connected specialists. The *Person Engineering* source explicitly allows a subject expert and an instructional specialist to work together. It also distinguishes deep knowledge of a practice from personal performance of every action: a coach can understand and develop a skill without demonstrating every difficult movement. The important requirement is a reliable professional basis for selecting tasks, recognizing performance and returning a useful correction.

Obtaining and sustaining actual participation is another professional contribution. Its object is the person's engagement in the learning actions the arrangement requires. Agreeing an enrolment or explaining an exercise leaves open whether the learner can and will undertake those actions. The teacher's leadership contribution joins knowledge of the intended learning with an understanding of participants' purposes, commitments, workload, available help and experience of the work. An adviser, teacher, facilitator or qualified assistant can supply parts of this contribution; one occupational title is not required.

Make the next learning action and its reason recoverable to the person. Establish a feasible occasion, the needed material and help, and how the learner can raise a difficulty, ask to change the arrangement or decline it. Observe the contribution that the action needs: an attempted diagnosis, a comparison of grounds or a requested clarification. Attendance, enthusiasm and compliance with a reminder establish different things. The useful result is an actual, supported continuation that the learner and teacher can use, or a justified change, pause or refusal with its consequences made explicit. It is not yet evidence of acquired mastery.

When the action is absent, distinguish the causes that would change the remedy. The person may reject the purpose, lack protected time or access, not understand the request, lack a prerequisite, or find the available help unusable. [HCD.3](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd3--diagnose-whether-a-capability-misconception-or-behaviour-limits-work) distinguishes a supported human limitation from demand, method, provision and organizational alternatives. Obtain the relevant explanation or arrangement change, then inspect whether the needed action becomes possible. A diagnosis of low motivation cannot be inferred from an empty submission.

For shared learning, [HCD.26](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd26---regulate-shared-learning) compares intended and actual work, obtains a feasible regulation move and inspects its uptake. HCD.7 supplies missing access and help; subject instruction addresses a demonstrated misunderstanding. When a person's self-regulation is itself a development target, obtain its supported demand and learning method rather than treating every interruption as that deficit. A changed purpose or refusal returns to the participants' decision; a teacher's wish to finish the programme supplies no substitute for it. Agreed work commitments and their consequences remain matters for the authorized participants.

Participation and subject teaching constrain each other. An inaccessible or meaningless task can prevent an attempt; an intelligible feasible task can expose a genuine subject difficulty. A teacher may both restore the learning occasion and give feedback on what happens in it, while an external specialist supplies the subject criterion. Where these contributions are divided, their results must meet in time for the learner's next action. The provider needs to make that connection obtainable, including changes to arrangements that the teacher cannot authorize.

Suppose a provider teaches equipment diagnosis. A subject specialist supplies representative fault situations and the reasons that distinguish a valid inference from an attractive guess. The instructional designer uses these distinctions to construct practice and variation; [HCD.6 and HCD.10](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) develop the relevant task construction. A teacher observes the learner's attempt and uses [HCD.9](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd9---perform-deliberate-practice-with-feedback) to connect feedback with the learner's response and a further attempt when correction requires it. HCD.11–HCD.13 supply performance, transfer and retention judgements when the promised use needs them.

A polished solution can supply a useful explanation while leaving the learning operation undone. Conversely, a difficult exercise with no interpretable feedback can consume effort without developing the missing contribution. A competent teaching arrangement makes the subject criterion, the learner's attempted action and the correction meet. Its result is assessed under the help that will actually be available later; independence from every tool is not a universal objective.

Tutoring and guidance concern another connection: the person chooses or revises a route, interprets difficulty and obtains suitable help. The recipient's perspective and the helper's perspective must remain recoverable. The same person can help others and work on their own development. An AI assistant can supply tutoring contributions where qualified. Changing the speaker of “I” in an instruction does not establish whose aims are being chosen or who acquires the capability.

[HCD.7](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd7---arrange-providers-access-tools-and-ai-support-for-human-capability-development) obtains the actual providers and means. C.32.MWA.PP compares the complete arrangement: integrated expertise, connected specialists, external teaching provision, AI-assisted participation or another suitable way. Include the learner's work and the time required for subject judgement, feedback and exceptions. A platform can share materials or preparation, while a person-specific attempt and response can still require individual work.

### HCE.Reference:4.3 - Revise the professional arrangement when the learning promise changes

The following constructed variations teach how to select the professional question that a changed condition reopens. A lost reason to pursue a mastery, an absent learning action and a lost tool lead to different returns. The local programme decisions make those differences inspectable; the reusable result is the ability to retain adequate contributions while obtaining the one whose grounds or realization failed.

Suppose a provider proposes an attractive new course in writing diagnostic software. Employers express interest, prospective learners register, and the provider can produce clear lessons. Inquiry into the proposed workplace use then finds that those employers already obtain maintained software from a specialist. Their technicians need to judge whether its recommendation fits the observed equipment and when to escalate. The proposed connection between software-writing mastery and that workplace benefit has failed. The educational direction must be reconsidered before better teaching of software writing could justify this course for that use.

The professionals recover the distinct purposes. The employer can consider a programme for supported diagnostic judgement; the subject specialist supplies the relevant decisions and criteria; learning designers and providers compare feasible ways to develop and support them. A learner who wants to become a software developer can retain that different direction with its own grounds and route. The provider can change its offer, investigate another audience or decline to offer a programme. This variation shows why demand for an offer, a learning objective and a person's reason to learn require different evidence and receiving decisions.

Consider a constructed programme for technicians who must diagnose equipment on site. Initially, they will have access to a maintained diagnostic assistant. The later-work demand requires them to recognize unsuitable inputs, supply the relevant observations, interpret the returned recommendation and stop or obtain help when its conditions fail. HCD.1 derives those human contributions; it need not require every technician to reproduce the assistant's entire method unaided.

The professional arrangement still needs the diagnostic method, a qualified assistant configuration, instructional work on the human contribution, accessible practice situations and a provider able to handle exceptions. Subject specialists qualify the diagnosis and its boundaries. Learning specialists develop the practice; technical contributors provide and maintain the assistant; the provider makes their work obtainable. A shared result depends on their connection. The learner performs the selected learning work.

Now suppose the technicians accept that purpose but the planned first practice produces no learner attempts. Interviews and the scheduled work show that service calls occupy the whole allocated window; one technician also cannot access the cases. The immediate missing contribution is an obtainable learning occasion. The provider restores access, and the employer and technicians agree a protected window or a different feasible route. Repeating the explanation or assigning harder cases would leave these conditions unchanged. If the window cannot be obtained, retain a supported service arrangement or revise the promise about when personal capability will be available.

In the restored session, a facilitator discovers that a shared assistant has been giving the diagnosis before the technicians compare the observations. The group has obtained answers while its intended learning action remains absent. The facilitator makes that discrepancy visible and asks the technicians to choose which observations the assistant needs and which conditions would require escalation. They can then obtain its recommendation and assess it. The facilitator examines whether the technicians now supply and discuss their required contributions. HCD.26 supplies this regulation of shared work; HCD.9 supplies feedback on the resulting attempt. Further subject help is selected from what the attempt reveals. A delivered prompt alone would establish neither participation nor learning.

If a technician instead disputes the usefulness of this direction or declines the proposed undertaking, return to that purpose and the available agreement. Additional exercises do not settle the disagreement. The variation separates obtaining participation from changing a person's capability: restoring a feasible occasion, revising an unhelpful prompt and reconsidering a disputed purpose obtain different results, each received by different next work.

Now the organization changes the field conditions: some sites cannot provide the assistant, while the service promise still requires diagnosis there. The changed support has changed the human demand for those sites. Compare an offline qualified tool, different qualified help, a narrower field-service promise and development of the technician's own diagnostic contribution. HCD.2 and C.32.MWA.PP compare these complete alternatives. The chosen option determines what learning, support and qualification are actually needed.

If the choice requires personal diagnostic capability that the technicians do not yet have, the programme must now develop and examine that contribution. Merely removing the assistant from the old final test can reveal failure; it supplies no learning route. The subject specialist must expose the distinctions the learner needs, the instructional designer must arrange suitable practice and feedback, and the provider must make it feasible. Adequate earlier learning can be retained.

A different change produces a different return. If the diagnostic method itself changes while support remains available, subject work first determines which inferences and criteria changed. Then the explanatory materials, assistant configuration, teacher preparation and affected learner contributions must be reconsidered. If only the platform's access fails, repair or replace provision before assuming every participant needs retraining. If a better AI configuration can supply a previously scarce teaching contribution, qualify it for the required observations and feedback and compare the new whole arrangement.

The result holder remains decisive. A good joint diagnosis establishes the service result under its conditions. A person's acquired capability requires evidence of that person's contribution. A reliable provider arrangement requires evidence about the joined work and support. A revised model, prompt, memory or tool changes a technical configuration by its own mechanism; it does not establish human learning. The shared professional account connects these results while preserving their different methods of change.

This is why transferring the engineering map to education requires subject reconstruction. The broad questions of use, architecture, realization, operation and provision remain useful. Human learning, agency and the professional interpretation of performance determine what those questions mean here. The provider's organization, the programme's design and the person's capability can be developed together, with changes flowing among them through their actual dependencies.

## HCE.Reference:5 - Why a specialized Suite rather than one larger DPF?

Human capability engineering is a specialization of engineering by its target and its human mechanisms. [Engineering (Engineering DPF Suite)](../Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/README.md) connects knowledge to constructing, changing and sustaining functioning systems. This Suite explains the human learning and somatic contributions at the level where their special conditions can be understood together. Separate publication coverage does not remove that engineering relation.

Keeping HCD and SOM as independent publications linked only at individual uses would preserve their Methods, but leave their shared boundary and reciprocal constraints to be reconstructed repeatedly. Folding SOM into HCD would put the specialized bodily constructions inside the more general programme language and make direct somatic use harder to distinguish. The Suite retains both complete languages and makes their part–whole connection explicit. Their different generality is useful: SOM specializes one human contribution, while HCD also serves work without a bodily-learning difficulty.

FPF Core supplies shared meanings for capability holders, Methods, work, support and evidence. The human mechanisms described here require the domain languages. Explanation Design, Music and Dance Practice Engineering and Embodied Rhythmics have their own subjects beyond changing a human capability. Their relevant contributions remain accessible without importing their whole fields.

The cost of the two-language arrangement is that a question crossing programme and bodily-learning assumptions requires both accounts. Their direct entries and the connected example reduce that burden; a known local question still permits direct use. HCD and SOM each have one complete description in this Suite, rather than separately edited copies in different Suites.

This first edition covers only part of the larger capability-engineering subject. Its value is the explained and usable connection it supplies now, including the point where a missing human mechanism or specialist result prevents continuation. The field can include other substantiated contributions without requiring them all in each use. A new professional label or a longer list of topics alone would not extend the available Methods.

The distinction between target and task also matters within human work. Personal management can regulate current priorities and activity without pursuing capability change. Pedagogy, adult education and coaching can provide different learning, advising or regulating contributions. A profession, service label or learner's age does not alone select the mechanism or establish the required expertise. Use the actual result and conditions to identify the contribution; keep the unprovided result visible.

Further work may establish additional human-capability fields and their connections, or show that a needed result is better obtained externally. This edition's learning and somatic coverage does not define the boundary of the larger human subject.

## HCE.Reference:7 - What preserves the whole when its Methods change?

The discipline retains its identity while particular theories, teaching techniques, instruments and service arrangements change, provided knowledge of human change remains connected to deliberate capability construction, actual human performance and warranted reconsideration. The engineering target is still the person's capability; the person's participation, permitted interventions and applicable professional conditions constrain how it can be pursued.

A replacement contribution must preserve the relations on which its receiving use relies. A different movement-teaching Method can replace a SOM contribution for a particular programme when it supplies the required bodily action, retains the relevant whole-task conditions and supports the needed judgement. The words used to describe sensations can differ. HCD.5 and HCD.14 supply the corresponding Method choice and evidence-based revision.

Changes run in both directions. A new observation can revise the bodily explanation or the programme; a changed task can invalidate the former test; a changed helper can alter what the person must supply. Compatible results remain usable while the affected assumption is reconsidered.

This specialization can contribute to a larger engineering arrangement joining human, organizational and technical capabilities. Each holder's means of change and evidence remain identifiable. A successful service can require all three while still leaving a separate question about what one human can do. The broader system's purpose and acceptance therefore do not replace the human result specified here.

If an additional field supplies a human mechanism and consequential connections that the current pair lacks, the assembly can expand. If a proposed addition only shares an audience or supports an occasional use, an external return may be sufficient. The same comparison can justify keeping a contribution outside, changing a boundary or using a better independent source.

## HCE.Reference:8 - What grounds the subject and the name?

The two constituent frameworks carry their full explanations, cases and source comparisons. [HCD's source account (HCD.Sources:1)](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcdsources1---source-use-and-currentness) distinguishes human-learning evidence, specialist criteria and the broader programme construction. [SOM's rationale (SOM.Reference:6)](SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#somreference6---rationale-source-use-and-alternatives) explains the relation between bodily modeling and learning, its comparison with direct instruction, and the limits of its teaching claims. Their sources can support particular mechanisms without establishing every proposed combination's effectiveness.

The distinction between the human whose capability changes and the person or AI providing help has empirical consequences. In [Bastani and colleagues’ 2025 mathematics trial](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122), both AI tutors improved assisted practice. On the subsequent unassisted exam, the standard chatbot group performed worse than the control group; safeguards largely removed that loss without establishing an improvement over the control. HCD.2, HCD.10 and HCD.12 therefore keep supported performance, independent performance and transfer separately assessable. A useful service result can be enough for a work task, while a capability-development claim needs evidence about the learner.

A serious alternative to treating AI merely as an answer provider is a deliberately designed tutor. [Kestin and colleagues’ 2025 randomized study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6) found higher immediate post-test performance with a structured AI tutor than with the compared active-learning classes in two college-physics lessons. The arrangement included expert-prepared material and controlled progression. This is evidence for that instructional alternative, not for long retention, workplace transfer or every AI tutor. Together the studies favor selecting and testing the actual teaching arrangement over choosing or rejecting it by the provider’s label. They do not rank SOM’s bodily mechanisms or establish the effectiveness of every HCD–SOM combination. Those questions retain their own sources and tests.

Related fields help locate this assembly. [IEEE ICICLE's Learning Engineering account](https://sagroups.ieee.org/icicle/learning-engineering-process/) connects learning sciences, human-centered engineering design and data-informed decisions to support learners. This informs the learning branch; the bodily content and its conditions still need their own explanation. [ISMETA's professional account](https://ismeta.org/about-the-profession) describes somatic movement education and its professional boundaries. It distinguishes that practice from clinical diagnosis and treatment.

The name Human Capability Engineering identifies the bounded synthesis explained here. Human denotes the person whose capability is to change, Capability limits the intended subject, and Engineering names the connection between knowledge, deliberate construction and qualified use. The title does not claim that all these Methods belong to one established profession.

Two nearby names would suggest a different principal subject. [Human factors and ergonomics](https://iea.cc/about/what-is-ergonomics/) concerns interactions between humans and other elements of a system and the design of those interactions. [Human Development in the lifespan sense](https://human.cornell.edu/degrees/human-development) studies broader biological, psychological, social and cultural change. Those bodies of knowledge can supply relevant constraints, but neither name identifies the deliberate capability construction and present coverage of this Suite as clearly.

Reconsider a relied-on mechanism when better evidence changes its conditions or effect; reconsider the assembly when a changed contribution alters the connections explained above. The complete Methods remain the source for performing and adapting the work. [Using FPF Library](../../USING-FPF.md) supplies connected-reading guidance, and the [FPF Library Architectural Reference](../../FPF-LIBRARY-REFERENCE.md) places this Suite among the Library's other languages.
