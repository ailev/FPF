# FPF Library

**Methods for solving difficult problems, expanding what you can do, and continuing development.**

FPF Library brings together the **First Principles Framework (FPF Core)** and domain frameworks for people, AI agents, teams and organizations. Its methods connect reasoning, research, design, management and learning. Each is explained as a pattern: a recognizable difficulty, the conditions in which it arises, a way to obtain a useful result, worked cases and limits.

**Use FPF with the AI assistant and workflow you already have.** Start with one method when it fits. When the job calls for several kinds of expertise, work out what each practice can contribute and where the contributions must fit together. Ask what a difference in their assumptions would change for the people who use the result. Include learning when the team needs new skills for later work. You can use one method for one calculation, design, decision or learning need and stop there.

Obtaining a result, acquiring the capability to produce it, and improving how you develop further capabilities call for different work and evidence. FPF helps connect them: engineering can establish what will work, management can organize its delivery, and learning methods can help the intended performers acquire what they need. Choose the contributions your work requires.

## Try one method

Open **[Develop a way, its holder and its provision together (C.40.JD)](https://fpf.tools/C.40.JD)** when you need to connect a working method, the capability to perform it, and the resources and authority for its use. Read it yourself, or give your assistant the text and a situation such as this:

> Read [Using FPF Library](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/USING-FPF.md) and [C.40.JD](https://fpf.tools/C.40.JD). Our team cannot yet fulfil this order. A contractor can deliver a result that passes acceptance now, but next month our team must fulfil a changed order using its own staff and tools. Help compare ways to meet both needs. Identify the work to arrange now, what to check on delivery, and what evidence would justify entrusting the changed order to our team. State which missing facts could change the recommendation.

If your assistant cannot open a link, give it the linked text. For ongoing use, [connect MCP or keep local copies](#read-online-or-use-your-own-copies).

A useful answer tells you what to accept from the contractor and what would justify giving the next order to your team. The team's earlier work may already show that it can perform the operations the changed order requires, with the staff and tools it will have. Where the change puts a necessary capability in doubt, identify the cause and arrange what the team needs before the deadline. That might mean practice with feedback, access to equipment or a trial of the unfamiliar operation. The changed order itself is still due next month. Its acceptance depends on what the team delivers then.

Change the aim and the decision can change: if only this delivery is needed and a qualified contractor can supply it, buying the result may suffice. If the team must keep adapting to unfamiliar orders, also examine how it will discover missing methods, develop or obtain them, and organize the next acquisition. **[C.40.OD](https://fpf.tools/C.40.OD)** develops that continuing undertaking.

## Keep connected reasoning in view

**Long mantras** recall how answers to related questions depend on one another. [B.5.PI](https://fpf.tools/B.5.PI) explains how a suitable mantra can help find a consequential problem before it is felt: examine its connected questions in a case and investigate a consequential gap when one appears. For a systemic project question, **[A.1.STM](https://fpf.tools/A.1.STM)** connects what should change in use to the system’s contribution. Examine how the parts of a larger whole function together when that relation determines the contribution. Direct evidence can settle a question confined to a particular use. Follow the dependencies the project actually needs into the system’s structure, the methods for obtaining it and who can perform them. Keep the product's intended use in view when improving the people, AI systems and organizations that create it. Those improvements can have their own finite projects and acceptance conditions. Independent suppliers connect their product purposes through the receiving use and obligations. The full account explains how a noticed difficulty reaches those same grounds and what to revise when a premise changes.

The **[control mantra (FPF C.43)](https://fpf.tools/C.43)** connects assessment of the situation, possible continuations, comparison and choice, realization, examination of what happened and revision. Its full Method explains how observations can change later choices, how means and timing constrain them, and how an intervention can develop the capability to address further problems. Technical control, economic choices and developmental recommendation retain their particular mechanisms.

A mantra presents logical dependencies in an order useful for explanation and recall. The present problem determines the order of thinking and work. The [Library's account of connected reasoning (Library.Reference:1.1)](FPF-LIBRARY-REFERENCE.md#libraryreference11---long-mantras-and-connected-reasoning) leads to developed connections in several fields. Read the full account to understand and adapt the method; use the short formula to recall it.

## From a useful result to further development

The Library is developing as a **methodology of open-ended development**. A result can make a previously unattainable problem approachable; working on that problem can expand both your capabilities and your ways of developing them. The aim is to keep that continuation possible, without a predetermined final level. This includes noticing limitations that have not yet become explicit problems, using existing knowledge, constructing missing methods, and passing useful capabilities and knowledge to others.

It can serve as a **canon for a shared culture of thinking and acting among people and AI agents**, oriented toward their open-ended development. Here, a canon is a common, revisable body of explanations and methods through which participants can organize work, learn from results and improve their practice. People and AI can contribute as assistants or teammates according to their capabilities and roles. The culture takes shape in how they work and develop together; using the Library does not require every participant to master the entire Library.

A person working with an AI, several collaborating teams and a company with a development office need different domain methods and allocations of work, resources and authority. Core supplies the general connections: [continue development (C.40.OD)](https://fpf.tools/C.40.OD), [develop a method, its performer and its provision together (C.40.JD)](https://fpf.tools/C.40.JD), and [find new attainable problems (C.40.NP)](https://fpf.tools/C.40.NP). The DPFs supply the particular mechanisms of learning, research, engineering, management and other practices. Developing a method, changing an agent's capability and performing work remain distinct contributions.

## Check whether it helps your work

Choose a task for which you can judge whether the answer lets someone do the work they asked for. Compare your usual workflow with and without the selected FPF methods. Keep the agent, tools, starting information and task conditions comparable and account for what the first attempt could teach the second. Before reading the answers, decide which mistakes would prevent the recipient from doing the commissioned work or change the decision. Count those still present and record your time spent selecting, reading, supplying and applying the methods, checking the answers and making corrections. When judging the team’s total effort, include the other participants’ work.

To judge the effect on the same tasks, compare results under conditions that matter and account for how often those tasks occur and what their errors and corrections cost. To decide whether to adopt the workflow, include the work each arrangement would create or remove, such as review, training or repeated corrections. A result on one task may already expose a defect or settle that task. To make a claim across the workload, use evidence or justified bounds covering the differences that could change the decision. [Design a comparison for its intended use](https://fpf.tools/G.9:4.2).

## What is available

**FPF Core** contains 408 patterns in 19.338 million characters. It supplies transdisciplinary concepts and methods, including methodological work, architecture construction and comparison, and open-ended development. **36 Domain Principle Frameworks (DPFs)** supply subject-specific methods: 26 belong to four Suites, and ten have their own files directly in the DPF catalogue.

A DPF describes one field through a connected pattern language. A **Suite** describes a composite discipline through its constituent fields and their relations. An **Architectural Reference** explains how the relevant language is arranged and why: within a framework, across a Suite, or across the Library.

| DPF Suite | Working subject | DPFs | Patterns | Text, M characters |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [Engineering](DPFs/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/) | Designing, realizing, changing and maintaining systems; knowledge access, semantic integration and control architecture design. | 5 | 99 | 2.999 |
| [Management](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/) | Direction, goals, commitments, operations, resources, markets, communities and organizational change. | 14 | 201 | 5.799 |

The [DPF catalogue](DPFs/) lists the ten further frameworks and opens each Suite's files. Suite totals sum their constituent framework texts; separate Suite References and README pages are excluded. Character counts include spaces and original line breaks.

The Library covers its disciplines selectively. For example, the current Human Capability Engineering Suite develops learning and somatic contributions.

### Work with an AI assistant

Give the assistant access to the full relevant texts, your situation, constraints and observations. Put this instruction in the project settings or instruction file the agent reads, replacing `fpf/` with your location:

> Use FPF Library from `fpf/`. Before applying its methods, read `fpf/USING-FPF.md` and follow its guidance. Help obtain the result our work needs. Explain the reasoning in our working language, identify the patterns used, and distinguish source claims, assumptions and missing evidence.

Then ask about your own work. The [first-use example](#try-one-method) shows how to supply a consequential condition and ask for an answer you can check.

An assistant can retrieve methods, calculate, compare alternatives, draft and challenge reasoning. Its fluent answer does not establish an observed fact, a new capability, permission, a commitment or an implemented system. Check the output against the actual work, evidence and applicable decision authority. FPF Library is intended for human use as well as AI-assisted work, across physical, organizational, intellectual and embodied practices.

## Understand, check or extend a contribution

The README pages help you locate a method. Its full pattern gives the grounds for using it. A maintained comparison can also explain competing approaches, failures and limits.

When you write a guide or a pattern, start with the work its reader should be able to do. Someone asked to design a way of working needs grounds for choosing an operation or arrangement. If several contributions must work together, explain what connects them and supports the result. The same decision can require different information for the person making it and for the people who must carry it out. Choose each account from the work its reader has to do. An example can show how one author made those choices. Give the reader the means to make them in their own work, and preserve that opportunity in any assignment issued separately. [Explanation Design](https://fpf.tools/EXD.Reference:3) · [FPF pattern authoring](https://fpf.tools/E.8) · [Developing a domain framework](https://fpf.tools/E.4.DPF) · [Choosing viewpoints](https://fpf.tools/E.17.0:4.8)

For a precise reference, retain the complete PatternID or section address and enough context to identify the publication. [Using FPF Library: resolve a portable reference](USING-FPF.md#resolve-a-portable-reference) explains how this works across files, search and MCP. You need not learn document codes before choosing what to read.

For an [issue or proposed improvement](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/issues), identify the publication and passage, the working situation, what failed or remains unavailable, and the change you propose. Use the relevant Architectural Reference to understand the current design. The broader FPF ecosystem can include independently authored DPFs, local frameworks (LPFs), guides, tools and services; this repository publishes FPF Library.
