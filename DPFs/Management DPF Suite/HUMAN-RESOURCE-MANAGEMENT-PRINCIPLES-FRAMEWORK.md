# Human Resource Management Readme

## Practical entries

These practical examples illustrate ways to enter the framework. If your question does not fit an example, use the [Table of Contents](#table-of-contents), search the complete text, or follow [Using FPF Library](../../USING-FPF.md) to find the relevant contribution.

### Connected conditions — One personnel change defeats another commitment

**Situation.** A new service needs different work, but selection still tests the old job, reward omits the helping work, or the hours needed to meet the promise conflict with a protection requirement.

**Question.** Which complete work and participation arrangement can actually be sustained?

**First useful result.** A feasible combination, or the precise incompatible commitment or missing professional result that prevents one.

**Start with.** [HRM.1](#hrm1---construct-and-revise-a-coherent-hr-arrangement). The [Reference](#human-resource-management-principles-framework-architectural-reference) explains the whole and the reasons to select its parts.

**Stop or return.** Use an adequate existing instruction directly for an ordinary case. A routine pay correction does not require a workforce strategy.

### Selection — An assessment must support a consequential choice

**Situation.** You must recruit, promote or allocate a person, and a course result, work history, interview or AI score is offered as evidence.

**Question.** What does that evidence warrant for the actual work and decision?

**First useful result.** A supported comparison and recommendation, or a specific evidence gap.

**Start with.** [HRM.2](#hrm2---substantiate-a-personnel-assessment-and-selection).

**Stop or return.** A learning discussion can remain formative. An appointment needs its own authority and effective act.

### Reward — The basis of a pay difference is unclear

**Situation.** Jobs change, market rates diverge, a progression promise is disputed, or individual targets penalize the help the organization requests.

**Question.** Which job, market, contribution and obligation grounds should determine the terms?

**First useful result.** An explained reward arrangement with an implementable transition.

**Start with.** [HRM.3](#hrm3---design-and-revise-remuneration-and-progression).

**Stop or return.** If the existing rule and entitlement are clear, use Organization Administration to correct provision. A contested rule or valuation can need HRM.4.

### Voice — A person needs influence or a decision they can challenge

**Situation.** A change affects employees, a concern is being dismissed as resistance, or a personnel decision needs correction, determination or appeal.

**Question.** Which process can hear the issue and produce the required response with the right authority?

**First useful result.** Obtainable participation in the decision, or a properly determined issue and remedy.

**Start with.** [HRM.4](#hrm4---obtain-employee-voice-and-handle-a-contested-personnel-decision).

**Stop or return.** Use a sufficient ordinary correction without manufacturing a dispute. Preserve direct external and urgent protective routes where applicable.

### Participation — The present arrangement no longer fits the person

**Situation.** A person wants a new direction after success, reports severe exhaustion, needs changed conditions or a return after absence, considers leaving, or must end the relationship.

**Question.** What continuation, absence, return, movement or exit is actually warranted and obtainable?

**First useful result.** An arrangement connecting real work, support and terms to the person's response and applicable constraints.

**Start with.** [HRM.5](#hrm5---arrange-continuing-participation-and-work-transitions). Serious functional difficulty calls for access to medical assessment and the resulting work response even when output remains excellent.

**Stop or return.** A manager or agent does not diagnose the cause. Obtain qualified clinical or safety help for its own result, and implement the resulting work restrictions through the actual work and personnel arrangements.

# Human Resource Management Principles Framework Architectural Reference

## HRM.Reference:1 - The field and the result it serves

Human resource management concerns the arrangements through which people enter organizational work, contribute, receive returns, influence conditions, continue or resume participation, move to other work and leave. This framework develops the recurring personnel decisions and their connections. It serves a manager, HR practitioner, employee representative, person considering a work change, or capable assisting agent who must construct or question such an arrangement. It does not assume one party's interests exhaust the problem.

Employment is a major case. A contractor, volunteer or member can have a different relation, with different rights, obligations and possibilities of exit. Establish the actual relation before using an employment procedure. The name of this field does not mean that an organization owns a person. A capability, a commitment to contribute, permission to act, willingness, health and access to another opportunity remain different facts.

The broad people-and-culture question exceeds this personnel branch. Independent learning, a customer's experience and a voluntary practice community do not become HR cases merely because people participate. They enter this framework when personnel choices or workforce terms become material: paid practice time, eligibility for a role, the use of community activity in promotion, a work restriction, or an employment obligation. Human Capability Development, Community Management and the particular professional practice retain their subjects.

The practical result is a warranted, obtainable arrangement and usable personnel decisions. Sometimes this means a new appointment. Sometimes it means changing the workload, offering an alternative role, granting an applicable absence, correcting pay, obtaining a remedy, declining a new promise or supporting an exit. Retention, engagement and productivity are possible outcomes, not universal definitions of success.

## HRM.Reference:2 - Why the practices belong together

### HRM.Reference:2.1 - The common relationship and its different grounds

An organization needs work under actual conditions. People participate under terms, obligations, expectations and alternatives of their own. The work changes those terms, while the terms influence who can and will perform the work. A technically possible service may remain unobtainable because its recruitment offer is implausible, its hours are harmful, its required helping work is unpaid, or no participant has accepted its contribution. A satisfactory service result can conceal these failures for a time.

HR practice acts on this continuing relationship through different professional operations. Assessment supports an inference about a person's contribution under stated conditions. Reward establishes the terms and grounds of exchange. Voice allows affected people to influence decisions and contest their application. A participation arrangement joins a person's circumstances and qualified constraints to available work and provision. The whole-configuration Method explains how these operations fit together and what a change requires one to reconsider.

Their unity therefore comes from conditional dependence, not a shared department or database. The inference from a work sample to selection depends on the work actually offered. The fairness and affordability of progression depend on the applicable rule, job demands, comparison population and funding. An accommodation depends on obtainable work and on the professional restriction it must meet. A record can connect the same person's data while leaving every one of these inferences unsupported.

### HRM.Reference:2.2 - Knowledge guides practice, and practice can change the account

The field draws on knowledge of work requirements, selection and measurement, employment relations, labor markets, motivation, job value, reward and institutional effects. Occupational health and safety provide qualified grounds concerning conditions, exposure and supported participation. These contributions answer different questions. A psychological account of motivation supplies no wage entitlement; an authorized appointment supplies no evidence of mastery; a high performance score supplies no clinical assessment.

Practice makes those distinctions consequential. A recruitment comparison can reveal that the supposed entry requirement is learnable after appointment. A pay challenge can expose an omitted responsibility in job evaluation. A supported return can show that a technically possible schedule was not actually provided. A consistently successful AI service can permit a different job or reveal a damaging pace of work. Each result can reopen the relevant knowledge, rule, work design or provision, without requiring every previous conclusion to be discarded.

This is a plural professional discipline. No single theory of motivation, economic optimization or institutional compliance replaces its other grounds. The relations are often conditional and sometimes contested. A bundle that improves organizational results can burden participants differently; agreement cannot be presumed. Use the actual protection requirements, representations, alternatives and decision powers to delimit acceptable options, then compare their remaining consequences. More aggregate benefit does not purchase an exception from a binding condition.

### HRM.Reference:2.3 - Five separately usable Methods

| Method | Its own result | What it supplies to another Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HRM.1, coherent arrangement | A compatible combination of workforce practices, work, terms and provision, or an identified unresolved dependency | Relevant work and participation conditions for HRM.2–HRM.5; receives their findings when a combination must change |
| HRM.2, assessment and selection | A justified personnel comparison and recommendation for a particular use | The supported capability and suitability inference; not an appointment, entitlement or clinical conclusion |
| HRM.3, remuneration and progression | Explained reward terms and their transition | Obligations and incentives affecting selection, participation and helping work; receives changed job demands and qualified contribution findings |
| HRM.4, voice and contested decisions | Obtainable influence, a qualified determination or a remedy | Participant knowledge, negotiated terms or a decision that can require any affected practice to change |
| HRM.5, participation and transitions | A particular continuation, absence, return, movement or exit with actual work, support and effective terms | Individual conditions and consequences; returns recurring incompatibilities to HRM.1 |

These are not stages that every employee must traverse. HRM.1 works across practices and participants; HRM.5 constructs a particular person's arrangement. HRM.2–HRM.4 can be used directly. An existing sufficient rule can settle a case without invoking a new configuration exercise. Conversely, running all five separately does not prove that their commitments can coexist.

## HRM.Reference:3 - Professional contributions and effective acts

### HRM.Reference:3.1 - Keep the result of each act recognizable

A person can be capable but ineligible under an applicable rule, eligible but not selected, selected but not appointed, appointed but unable to obtain the promised access, correctly graded but incorrectly paid, or willing to continue under conditions that cannot currently be supplied. These differences select different work.

| Needed result | Obtain it through | Return to HR when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A worthwhile direction or revised commitment | Strategy and the responsible undertaking owner | Work, staffing or terms must change to make it obtainable |
| Feasible operating work and a warranted interpretation of results | Operations Management, including OPS.22 for operating assessment | The result is proposed for selection, reward or a participation decision; that further inference needs its own grounds |
| A changed organization or way to obtain a contribution | Organization Change Engineering, including OCE.8 for whole obtaining alternatives | Assignments, authority, opportunities and participation terms must fit the selected arrangement |
| Effective permissions, dates, provision and obligations | Organization Administration under the applicable personnel instruction | A rule, professional judgement or contested entitlement is missing; administration cannot invent it |
| A human capability selected for later work | Human Capability Development, with C.40.JD when the way, holder and provision need joint development | Learning access, use opportunities and personnel consequences need an HR arrangement |
| A clinical, occupational-health, safety, legal or qualified assessment result | The competent professional supplier for the actual question and setting | Its conditions must change work, terms or the personnel inference |
| Actual participation in a shared practice | Community Management and the practice's own Methods | Employer commitments, reward or personnel uses become material |

A professional referral becomes useful when its output can enter the receiving decision. State the actual work question, relevant conditions and permitted information; obtain the supplier's conclusion, scope, uncertainty and reconsideration condition. Receive what the decision needs, not every underlying private record. Then identify the owner able to change the work, terms or decision. A recommendation sent to HR has not yet changed a schedule or authorized leave.

### HRM.Reference:3.2 - Protection before failure and medical help without managerial diagnosis

Prevention can begin with changed hours, staffing, pace, exposure or a credible concern while every production target is met. HRM.1 obtains the qualified protection result and connects it to operating and organizational choices. HRM.5 turns person-specific functional conditions into an attainable arrangement. These receiving operations do not replace the professional risk assessment or clinical work that supports them.

Severe exhaustion or an inability to carry out ordinary activity is a reason to facilitate qualified medical assessment and apply the relevant immediate support and absence arrangements. It is not evidence of poor motivation. Do not make falling output, an employee's diagnostic label or completion of a leadership course a condition for taking the difficulty seriously. The phrase “adrenal fatigue” is not an established medical diagnosis; actual adrenal insufficiency is a different condition requiring clinical diagnosis and treatment. The personnel response should follow the person's functional difficulty and qualified advice, rather than choose a cause or universal rest prescription. [Endocrine Society: adrenal fatigue](https://www.endocrine.org/patient-engagement/endocrine-library/adrenal-fatigue), [adrenal insufficiency](https://www.endocrine.org/patient-engagement/endocrine-library/adrenal-insufficiency).

Rest, leave, altered hours, workload limits or other adjustments can be part of a warranted arrangement. Their content and duration depend on the applicable grounds. Psychological help may be appropriate; it does not replace a needed medical inquiry into causes. Clinical information remains with its appropriate holder. A work owner generally needs the permitted functional conditions, provision and review point, not unrestricted access to a diagnosis or a personal assistant's conversations.

## HRM.Reference:4 - People, culture, offices and agents

### HRM.Reference:4.1 - Culture has several receiving questions

Culture can mean the ways of working that participants perform, explain, criticize, transmit and revise. Changing that culture requires changes in practice and its reproduction. Belonging and experienced support concern another aspect of participation. Voice, representation and remedy concern institutional influence. These can affect one another without becoming one measure. An enjoyable community event may leave work unchanged; a new reward rule may change willingness to help without changing anyone's capability.

An organization can give HR broad responsibility for obtaining cultural contributions. This neither makes HR the exclusive performer nor supplies unlimited powers. Line managers must provide suitable work and time; specialists supply subject Methods; communities provide access to practitioners and criticism; participants retain their purposes and responses. A shared practice need not be one organized agent. A company, professional network and customer community can share some practices while retaining different commitments and powers.

Development also begins after success. A mastered operation can make a new product, harder problem or better way of developing attainable. Core's development Methods, Strategy and the relevant domain practice supply that continuation. HRM examines the resulting work opportunities, terms, access and personnel uses. A deficit-only training intake would miss it. A person's independent trajectory may lead away from the employer; that difference calls for an actual participation decision, not a diagnosis of disloyalty.

### HRM.Reference:4.2 - An office is an obtaining arrangement

| Arrangement under consideration | Contribution to specify | Consequence for the HR question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PMO or development office | The selected advisory, project, method, coordination or development-help functions; their recipients and access | HR consequences follow from actual work and terms. A catalogue of office functions does not teach their professional Methods. |
| Improvement or DBS function | Selection and support of worthwhile improvement or innovation, including opportunities after success | Supply time, capability and receiving work. Do not reduce every initiative to employee training. |
| Enabling team | A bounded facilitation or learning contribution with a receiving team | Determine what the recipient should later obtain or perform and when this interaction can change or end. |
| Knowledge service | Continuing access, use-time improvement and stewardship of knowledge | A continuing service can remain useful after local learning; removing it is not automatically independence. |
| AI CoE or platform service | Particular common capabilities, advice, standards or infrastructure | Separate service provision from a workload owner's decisions and from personnel authority. |

Compare these with distributed provision, a competent colleague, an external professional and adequate agent work through OCE.8. Include the burden of obtaining and maintaining the help, not only the intervention. A title does not establish capability, availability, confidentiality or a useful result. An internal office may need its own Methods for client selection, intervention, preparation of helpers, capacity and continuing context. Those Methods are supplied by its own professional practice; the general Library connections do not create proprietary service know-how.

### HRM.Reference:4.3 - Allocate performance, authority and responsibility separately

An agent may research a career, construct an assessment, propose a work arrangement, perform a permitted administrative act or check another result when it is capable and authorized. A personal agent can serve a person's outside opportunity or challenge; a corporate agent can serve the employer's commitments. Sharing technology does not merge their mandates. Set the permitted data and act, the recipient, the relevant quality conditions and the route for correction or revoked authority.

AI can change the required capability and the work itself. Evaluate the actual human–AI or provider configuration. Retain an unaided human operation when the required result, credible operating conditions or an applicable rule require it; otherwise consider a changed operation or competent supply. A learning goal independently chosen by the person remains possible. Likewise, human review is selected for a needed capability, independence or institutional requirement, rather than appended to every agent act. Formal responsibility and powers still follow their actual rules; a high-quality generated recommendation does not grant them.

## HRM.Reference:5 - Construct and revise the connected arrangement

### HRM.Reference:5.1 - Successful automation opens new work

A service has reliable automated report preparation and proposes a new advisory offering. Strategy selects the worthwhile opportunity. Operations establishes the proposed demand and promises. OCE compares ways of supplying it, including agents, providers and differently organized work. HRM.1 then asks what work remains for people and under which terms they can supply it.

The old unaided reporting test no longer answers the advisory selection question. HRM.2 derives a relevant assessment from the proposed work and permitted support. HRM.3 evaluates changed job demands separately from individual performance and considers the resulting reward arrangement. If experienced advisers must mentor others, billable targets must leave attainable time for that commitment. HRM.4 obtains the required employee influence and handles a challenge to a proposed rule. HRM.5 constructs particular movements, learning arrangements or exits with those affected. ADM makes the authorized terms effective; OCE and OPS provide the actual work.

The whole reveals a contradiction that none of the local outputs alone resolves: a role can look properly assessed and paid while its promised mentoring time is impossible. Revise the work, provision, target or promise at its owner. An engagement campaign cannot supply the missing time.

Now change only a pay rule's effective date. Correct the affected entitlement and provision, retaining the supported work sample and acquired capability. If instead the AI arrangement loses a needed function, revisit the obtaining choice and the affected assessment. These different returns follow from the grounds of the original decisions.

### HRM.Reference:5.2 - Good output coexists with an unacceptable load

In another branch, report quality remains high but work repeatedly extends beyond the supported hours. HRM.1 obtains qualified occupational-health and safety grounds using the actual work and workers' knowledge. Suppose those grounds require a change in the exposure or conditions. OPS revises service windows or commitments; OCE compares coverage alternatives; HRM connects these to hours, reward and participation. The constraint remains meaningful even if more overtime would raise revenue.

One employee then reports profound exhaustion: “I have no strength; I can only lie down.” HRM.5 facilitates the applicable medical route and immediate support, rather than interpreting this as refusal of responsibility. The receiving clinical result may require absence or work limits. HRM obtains only the information needed for work, arranges leave or changed conditions under the proper authority, and checks that assignments and messages actually respect them. The case does not assume a diagnosis or prescribe a recovery duration.

For a subsequent return, suppose the qualified advice permits a limited schedule with a review point. The work owner must supply tasks and coverage that fit it; returning attendance alone is insufficient. If the advice later changes, reconsider those conditions and terms. Preserve the good reports, valid obligations and population-level prevention result unless their own premises change. Conversely, a changed shift pattern can reopen prevention while the person's functional restriction stays the same.

### HRM.Reference:5.3 - Ordinary payment and a voluntary community

An employee shows that payroll used the wrong rate under an unambiguous current appointment. ADM can correct the obligation and provision directly. If the dispute is instead about the job evaluation, HRM.3 obtains the valuation grounds and HRM.4 supplies the competent challenge process. Making the records agree cannot decide the valuation.

A voluntary practice group wants to generalize a successful Method. Core development, the practice's research, Community Management and selected learning may be sufficient. HRM becomes relevant if an employer promises paid time or uses participation as promotion evidence. The changed relation selects an additional operation without turning the whole community into an HR department.

## HRM.Reference:6 - Alternatives, limits and use

These Methods form one bounded HRM discipline because they repeatedly construct and revise the same participation relationships under interacting grounds. A Suite of separate talent, reward, relations and culture disciplines would need those independently developed wholes and justified constituent relations. Departmental specialization alone does not supply them. One DPF preserves independently usable Methods without multiplying boundaries. If a later field account establishes an autonomous whole, that partition can change.

A competent external HR service remains a serious alternative to using this framework. Compare whether it supplies the required professional result, its explanation and usable joins, at the actual cost of acquisition, interpretation, combination and revision. A capable practitioner or agent with professional sources may need no additional local publication. This framework's contribution is the reusable connected account and construction, not an assertion that other providers cannot perform the work.

Use the smallest sufficient Method. For unfamiliar or coupled work, understand the Reference and the selected Methods' complete Solutions and cases; the entry table and conformance questions do not replace them. Adapt the procedure to the actual work relationship, population, jurisdiction and consequences. Obtain local professional results where the act depends on them. The framework teaches the receiving operation and its connection to work; it does not supply occupational medicine, every psychometric technique or worldwide employment law.

The cases are constructed demonstrations. They establish neither a measured time saving nor a causal effect on culture, wellbeing or retention. The source-use account distinguishes professional prescriptions, historical theories, empirical observations and new construction. Check actual work and participant outcomes separately. A changed rule, work arrangement, agent capability, source conclusion or recurring failure reopens the affected premise and its consumers; preserve the results that remain adequate.

## HRM.1 - Construct and revise a coherent HR arrangement

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### HRM.1:1 - Problem frame

**Use this when** a workforce proposal or existing practice changes conditions on which another personnel practice relies: a new job changes selection and pay, individual targets obstruct mentoring, or a service succeeds through hours that require protective action. The primary object is the combination of work, participation terms and HR practices for the affected population. Begin with the actual proposed contribution or changed condition and the current commitments. The first useful result is a compatible arrangement or an exact incompatibility returned to its owner.

An ordinary case with adequate existing grounds can use its established instruction directly. Do not conduct a complete HR audit to correct a known payroll error.

### HRM.1:2 - Problem

Individually plausible decisions can jointly promise impossible work, exclude needed participants or impose unacceptable burdens. Training can be available while no practice opportunity exists. A recruitment offer can promise development while the operating target consumes all learning time. Output can remain good while working conditions become harmful. Combining policies in one document does not resolve these contradictions.

### HRM.1:3 - Forces

| Force | Consequence for construction |
| --- | --- |
| Work requirements and personal alternatives | Feasibility includes obtainable participation, not only technical capacity. |
| Different professional grounds | An assessment, pay rule and protection condition cannot be combined by averaging their scores. |
| Local sufficiency and shared consequences | Reuse adequate results, but examine the dependencies a changed practice can defeat. |
| Viability and binding constraints | Compare feasible alternatives without trading away an applicable protection or obligation. |

### HRM.1:4 - Solution

#### HRM.1:4.1 - Recover the work and the actual relation

Identify the contribution, its recipient and conditions: what work is needed, when, with what tools, assistance and uncertainty, and who may revise that need. Recover the affected participation relations, present terms, commitments and material alternatives. Ask affected people what they actually do and what the proposed change would demand. A job title and nominal hours can conceal coordination, interruption, emotional demands or helping work.

Determine which work is newly needed, remains necessary, has disappeared or can be obtained differently. Strategy can propose a new opportunity after success; Operations supplies current demand and promises; OCE supplies organization and obtaining alternatives. Do not freeze an old task list and classify every difference as a human deficit. Keep effective commitments until their valid change: an eliminated task does not automatically eliminate a wage obligation.

#### HRM.1:4.2 - Obtain the conditions that constrain feasible participation

Recover the applicable employment or other participation rules, agreements, decision powers, consultation requirements and data-use conditions. Where their interpretation is consequential and unresolved, obtain a qualified answer for the actual relation and jurisdiction. Ask what act is permitted or required, by whom and under what conditions. A generic policy or an agent's confident explanation cannot supply missing authority.

For a credible health or safety question, obtain competent assessment of the actual work, proposed change and relevant participant information. Request the conclusion needed for the work decision: conditions or exposure assessed, required protection or functional constraints, scope, uncertainty and reconsideration condition. The professional supplier chooses and qualifies its assessment. HR does not derive a diagnosis from performance, attendance or a personal agent's notes.

Prevention does not require a complaint or a missed target. Changed pace, hours or staffing can be sufficient to open the professional question. Where a necessary result is missing, apply the actual interim protective requirements and leave the dependent claim of acceptable work unresolved. Distinguish binding constraints, existing obligations and negotiable preferences; each changes the alternatives differently. Receive only information needed and permitted for the decision.

#### HRM.1:4.3 - Construct complete alternatives around the unresolved question

Develop plausible combinations of changed work, internal movement, recruitment, external provision, learning and agent performance. Use OCE.8 when the whole obtaining choice is live. For each serious option, state the work people would actually undertake and the corresponding terms: entry and assessment, workload and time, opportunity to develop, reward, voice, protection, support and likely transitions. These are questions to resolve where material, not a compulsory form for every case.

A proposed recruitment campaign, for example, needs a real offer and qualifying work, not merely a target headcount. An internal-development option needs attainable practice and later use. An external-provider option may reduce employee learning needs while creating supplier supervision or integration work. An agent option can change both job demands and the number of participants. Compare complete contributions, including the costs of obtaining, operating and revising them.

#### HRM.1:4.4 - Examine the dependencies that can defeat an option

Use the relevant HR Method to substantiate each unresolved professional result. Then follow its consequences:

| Dependency | Test and useful correction |
| --- | --- |
| Work → assessment | Does the selection evidence concern the actual job and permitted support? Revise the evidence or the proposed job. |
| Opportunity → capability evidence | Can candidates obtain a fair opportunity to demonstrate the required contribution? Distinguish absent evidence, absent capability and absent access. |
| Work → job value and reward | Do changed responsibilities alter the valuation or reward grounds? Separate a job change from individual performance. |
| Reward → requested contribution | Do targets, paid time and progression support the helping or cooperative work being promised? Change the target, provision or promise when they conflict. |
| Protection → work and terms | Can the proposed hours, coverage and assignments meet the qualified conditions? Return an impossible arrangement to OCE/OPS and the terms owner. |
| Voice → premises and decisions | Can participants supply information and use the required influence or challenge route before the relevant decision becomes irreversible? |
| Personnel data → further use | Is the new use justified and permitted? A shared database does not authorize use of private development or health material for selection. |

Resolve a defect at its actual owner. Invalid selection evidence needs assessment work; an unsupported service promise needs an operating decision; an uncertain health premise needs the qualified supplier. Negotiation can settle discretionary terms. It cannot manufacture a missing clinical conclusion or waive a binding protection merely by obtaining agreement.

#### HRM.1:4.5 - Choose a feasible combination and make its commitments effective

Compare the remaining options using the relevant contribution, participant outcomes, costs, uncertainty and reversibility. State material conflicts instead of claiming automatic mutual benefit. An employee can reasonably prefer an outside trajectory; the employer can decline a discretionary proposal it cannot supply. A supported bounded arrangement, refusal, formal determination or exit may follow.

Use HRM.5 for particular continuation or transition arrangements and HRM.4 for required consultation, negotiation or contested decisions. Make clear which proposal requires agreement, which requires an authorized determination, and which follows a binding obligation. Obtain the real act and its effective date through the applicable process and ADM. Ensure that OCE and OPS supply the work, access, coverage and conditions on which that act relies. Communicate the resulting terms and how a problem can be raised.

#### HRM.1:4.6 - Observe the whole and reopen the defeated premise

Examine actual provision, contribution and participant outcomes separately. Check whether mentoring occurred in available time, whether the protected schedule was respected, whether the selected person could obtain the promised tools, and whether reward followed the established terms. Satisfaction, retention, absence and output can answer different questions and should not be collapsed into one success score.

A consequential observation can reveal a mechanism without establishing a causal effect. Return a changed condition to the smallest adequate owner and follow its consumers. Preserve unrelated valid results. A changed pay date can leave capability evidence intact; changed task content can require new assessment while an existing appointment remains effective. Recurring contradictions may justify redesigning the configuration rather than correcting another isolated case.

### HRM.1:5 - Archetypal Grounding

A six-person service proposes advisory work after successful report automation. Each person's thirty available work hours are already allocated to delivery. The proposal adds three hours of mentoring for each of two experienced advisers while leaving all delivery commitments unchanged. HRM.2 can justify their advisory capability and HRM.3 can establish an appropriate reward, yet the combination still demands sixty-six hours from the advisers' sixty-hour provision.

The owner considers reducing delivery, obtaining six hours of adequate external help, or changing the mentoring promise. Suppose the service can obtain that external help with the required subject access and permitted information. OCE qualifies the provision; OPS revises allocation; HRM confirms the terms and obtains participants' responses. The complete arrangement now has a plausible obtaining way. Its success still needs actual provision and use.

Change the condition: a qualified assessment shows that the service's repeated late coverage violates a required working condition. Paying more for the same hours does not solve it. Reconsider the coverage, service window or obtaining arrangement. Conversely, if the only change is a mistaken payroll date, use ADM to repair that obligation; retain the work and assessment results.

### HRM.1:6 - Bias-Annotation

Management records can omit unpaid and invisible work, and silence can reflect an unusable voice route. Obtain participant evidence and observe actual conditions. Do not infer that every difference in interest is opposition to change. The framework's employment-centered examples require adaptation for other participation relations.

### HRM.1:7 - Conformance Checklist

- Does the chosen option specify actual work and attainable participation under current commitments?
- Are the consequential assessment, reward, voice and protection grounds adequate for their particular uses?
- Has the construction resolved incompatible time, provision or terms rather than documenting them separately?
- Can the authorized acts and promised work become effective together?
- Does each material change return to its affected grounds while preserving adequate results?

These questions inspect the construction. An adequate description does not establish that the work or protection conditions actually obtain.

### HRM.1:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

**Policy bundle as proof of coherence.** Test the dependencies in the actual work. **Productivity as health evidence.** Obtain the separate professional result. **Training as the universal repair.** Compare changed work and competent provision before selecting human acquisition. **Consensus as the completion condition.** Preserve legitimate disagreement and use the applicable decision or exit process.

### HRM.1:9 - Consequences

The result exposes failures between individually adequate practices and makes selective revision possible. It requires obtaining information and negotiating or determining real terms; some initially attractive promises will shrink or stop. It can reveal an unavailable qualified supplier instead of producing a defensible arrangement immediately.

### HRM.1:10 - Architectural Rationale

The governed object is the HR configuration across practices and participants. General composition alone does not identify the domain dependencies: what selection can infer, how reward affects requested help, or which protection condition must reach an operating owner. Particular personal transitions remain with HRM.5. Keeping the two scales distinct permits local work without losing the whole.

### HRM.1:11 - SoTA-Echoing

Boxall and Purcell's plural account connects work organization, employment practices and differing interests; this Method adopts that field basis and develops an explicit construction and return. The bounded Li and colleagues study cautions against assuming that an HR bundle benefits every dimension of wellbeing. Its cross-sectional 2009 data do not estimate this Method's effect. WHO's distinction between work-condition prevention and individual support informs the receiving protection branch; the complete clinical or safety contribution remains external. At comparable scope, a qualified HR service can supply the same needed configuration. Use it when its result and joins are sufficient; this Method adds a reusable explanation, not presumed superiority.

### HRM.1:12 - Relations

HRM.2–HRM.5 supply the distinct personnel results. STR supplies direction; OPS work and commitments; OCE.8 whole obtaining alternatives; ADM effective acts and provision. HCD and C.40.JD supply selected capability development. Qualified institutional and health/safety suppliers constrain the feasible arrangement. HRM.Reference:2 explains why these contributions are connected without making their entire supplying disciplines parts of HRM.

### HRM.1:End

## HRM.2 - Substantiate a personnel assessment and selection

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### HRM.2:1 - Problem frame

**Use this when** recruitment, promotion, allocation or another personnel choice relies on a claim about a person's suitability for actual work. Begin with the decision and the contribution it needs, not an available test or candidate score. The first useful result is a warranted comparison and recommendation, or the exact uncertainty that prevents it. The object is the assessment and selection inference; an appointment remains a separate act.

Use a sufficient established procedure directly when its work, population, support conditions and decision remain applicable. A formative learning conversation need not become a selection exercise.

### HRM.2:2 - Problem

Evidence gathered for one purpose can acquire an unjustified second meaning. Course completion becomes mastery, mastery becomes suitability for a different job, a supervisor rating becomes general potential, and an AI score becomes an appointment recommendation. The procedure can consistently rank people while measuring the wrong contribution or conditions they will never face at work.

### HRM.2:3 - Forces

| Force | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Consequential choice and incomplete evidence | State what the evidence supports and obtain only the missing contribution that can change the decision. |
| Comparable treatment and attainable demonstration | Comparable opportunity may require a qualified accommodation rather than identical irrelevant conditions. |
| Actual assisted work and independent capability | Test the required configuration; add unaided performance only for a justified work condition. |
| Speed and validity | A fast, inexpensive procedure is useful only for an inference it can support. |

### HRM.2:4 - Solution

#### HRM.2:4.1 - Define the decision and investigate the work

Name what is being decided, who may decide, the candidate population, applicable eligibility conditions and the proposed terms. Separate an entry requirement from something learnable after appointment with available support. Recover the actual work through documents, observation and knowledgeable participants. Include consequential uncommon situations, coordination and recovery where they matter; frequency alone does not establish importance. Identify which variations in work, location, tools and support the role must cover.

Translate this analysis into claims needed for the decision: for example, the ability to turn a client's ambiguous concern into a bounded advisory question using the provided agent, explain the resulting recommendation, and recognize when a required professional conclusion is unavailable. Distinguish this from the obsolete ability to type a report unaided. If adequate work analysis is missing, obtain it before selecting the instrument. OPM's job-analysis practice and qualified industrial/organizational assessment are useful suppliers; the reader must still specify the receiving decision.

For an internal talent or promotion opportunity, establish who can be considered, how nomination or application works, and which exclusions have an applicable basis. Exclusive, inclusive and blended arrangements make different participation promises. Consider whether a supported direct application or another access route is needed; do not treat a manager's nomination as evidence of capability. More places do not by themselves establish that people experience inclusion or that the selection is fair.

For recruitment, make the proposed role and terms sufficiently real to offer. Choose channels capable of reaching the relevant population, communicate material requirements and conditions accurately, and provide an accessible application route. HRM.1/HRM.3 may need to change an unobtainable offer. A small response to an unattractive offer is not evidence that no capable people exist.

#### HRM.2:4.2 - Build the evidence argument before scoring candidates

For each consequential claim, choose evidence that could support it and explain the inferential step. A representative work sample may demonstrate a defined operation; a structured interview can investigate particular prior behavior or reasoning; a qualified test can support a specified construct or predictive claim. None is universally best. Specify the conditions, relevant comparison and decision use before knowing who benefits from the rule.

| Claim needed | Possible evidence | What must be established before use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Performance of a representative advisory task | Work sample with permitted tools and an explained scoring basis | The task samples consequential work, scoring concerns that work, and the support corresponds to the job |
| A contribution across a wider class of cases | Relevant history or a professionally supported assessment | The evidence can generalize to the proposed work, population and conditions |
| Eligibility or a required qualification | The applicable verified fact | The rule actually requires it and the fact concerns this person and use |
| Performance after an obtainable learning period | Appropriate evidence and a credible learning/provision arrangement | The recommendation distinguishes present capability from the conditional future contribution |

Ask a qualified assessment supplier for the validity argument for this decision, population and conditions; the consistency of measurement; relevant fairness and bias findings; limitations; permissible administration and change conditions. A vendor's reference customer or general accuracy figure is insufficient when the inference changes. The supplier can perform technical validation; HR must obtain enough of its result to understand which use is warranted. If the result is unavailable, use an adequate alternative or leave the dependent comparison unresolved.

A work sample's resemblance to the job alone does not establish every predictive claim. Likewise, correlation with old supervisor ratings is not automatically evidence of the new contribution if those ratings reflect obsolete tasks or unequal opportunities. Keep the intended outcome and the evidence for it explicit. Use existing HCD or OPS.22 results only within the claim each supports; a good operating result may depend on team or agent contributions not controlled by the individual.

#### HRM.2:4.3 - Make the assessment usable for the actual candidates

Specify administration, instructions, resources, time conditions, evidence handling and scoring. Ensure assessors can apply the criteria, and resolve material disagreements by examining the work and scoring grounds. Preserve applicable access, accommodation, privacy and representation requirements. An accommodation that removes an irrelevant barrier can improve the inference; a change that alters the assessed contribution needs its effect examined by the qualified supplier.

Tell candidates the material conditions and permitted use of assistance. If the job uses an agent, assess the relevant assisted contribution. If an outage or independently required operation matters, assess that condition for its own reason. Do not conceal a test of unaided performance inside a nominally assisted task, or interpret access to a more capable private agent as the person's general mastery without justification.

AI assessment needs an identified model/procedure and evidence relevant to its use. Material changes in scoring or inputs can make prior scores incomparable. Reuse of data from other purposes, including private developmental or health material, requires its own grounds and permissions. A competent agent may perform assessment or checking; the required independence, competence and institutional authority determine the allocation.

#### HRM.2:4.4 - Compare candidates under the justified rule

Apply the supported procedure to the eligible population. Separate missing evidence from unfavorable evidence and record material uncertainty. A person denied a relevant opportunity should not silently receive the same interpretation as one who demonstrated failure. Seek a proportionate clarification or another valid opportunity when it can change the choice and is permitted.

Distinguish minimum conditions from compensable preferences. A required professional permission cannot be compensated by a high interview score. Among candidates who meet binding conditions, use the explained comparison relevant to the work. Do not construct weights retrospectively to select a favored person. When candidates are not distinguishable on the evidence, use the applicable tie or further-evidence procedure and state the limit; artificial numerical precision supplies no missing information.

Construct the recommendation with the proposed work and terms, evidence and uncertainty, applicable conditions, and any learning or support on which it depends. The authorized decision maker may accept it, request a defined missing contribution or make another supported choice within the actual rules. Communicate the decision and available correction or challenge route through HRM.4. Use ADM and the relevant organizational process to make an appointment or assignment effective; selection itself does not provide access, remuneration or authority.

#### HRM.2:4.5 - Revisit the inference when its grounds change

Follow the receiving work and appropriate assessment evidence. A failure can concern the selection inference, missing promised support, a changed task or an operating arrangement. Investigate before treating it as proof that the person was unsuitable. Reopen the relevant validation or procedure when the work, population, tool configuration, scoring, criterion or applicable rule changes materially. Preserve results that still support their narrower uses.

### HRM.2:5 - Archetypal Grounding

A reporting service needs two advisers. Its old test ranks unaided spreadsheet speed. Work analysis now shows that agents prepare the tables; advisers must frame client questions, identify unsupported conclusions and negotiate an actionable response. The owner constructs two representative cases with the provided agent and criteria covering those operations. A qualified assessor examines the work coverage, scoring and limits. One case contains missing client evidence; a warranted refusal to recommend is a successful response, not an incomplete answer.

Candidate A produces a polished recommendation but misses that absence. Candidate B identifies it, obtains the permitted clarification and supplies a usable bounded answer. Under the established critical condition, fluency does not compensate for the unsupported recommendation. The comparison favors B for this role, subject to the remaining eligibility and employment conditions. It does not establish B's general intelligence or unaided spreadsheet mastery.

Now the proposed service must continue during agent outages. The owner may obtain fallback provision or require an additional human operation. Only after that choice should the assessment change. A blanket ban on tools would have tested a different job; a blanket promise that tools remove all human requirements would have missed the changed dependency.

### HRM.2:6 - Bias-Annotation

The incumbent population, historical ratings and recruitment channels can encode unequal opportunities. A subgroup difference is a reason for the appropriate professional and institutional inquiry, not by itself proof of a particular measurement mechanism. Candidate acceptability, psychometric validity and legal permissibility remain distinct. Use qualified grounds for each relevant claim.

### HRM.2:7 - Conformance Checklist

- Is the personnel decision connected to actual work and a specified population and support configuration?
- Does each material inference have appropriate evidence, scope and limits?
- Are administration, access and scoring sufficient for the intended comparison?
- Are missing evidence, unmet requirements and preference differences distinguished?
- Are recommendation, authorized act, actual provision and further challenge kept separate?
- Can a change reopen the affected inference without rewriting unrelated findings?

Recognizing a plausible work sample does not assure its validity. That conclusion requires the evidence appropriate to the actual claim.

### HRM.2:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

**Completion equals competence.** Recover what was actually demonstrated. **Potential as an unexplained score.** Name the contribution and the inference. **Vendor accuracy equals local validity.** Obtain the relevant population, work and use grounds. **The highest total overrides every requirement.** Keep binding conditions separate. **AI implies mandatory human rescoring.** Select a checker for actual competence, independence or institutional necessity.

### HRM.2:9 - Consequences

The comparison becomes usable for its defined decision, and unsuitable instruments can be rejected before affecting candidates. Some decisions remain uncertain or require professional validation. A supported rejection or no-selection result can be preferable to a confident appointment based on irrelevant evidence. Administrative and participation work still remain after selection.

### HRM.2:10 - Architectural Rationale

The Method owns the personnel inference joining work evidence to a consequential choice. It consumes job analysis, learning and operating results without equating them. The distinction allows one observation to retain its valid use while a proposed promotion or appointment remains unsupported.

### HRM.2:11 - SoTA-Echoing

SIOP's 2018 Principles and OPM's job-analysis/work-sample practice ground work relevance and appropriate use. SIOP's January 2023 AI-assessment guidance adds attention to changed scoring, data sources and the applicability of validation; it concerns AI used to assess people and expressly does not cover all employee–AI collaboration. This Method's treatment of actual assisted work is a construction from work relevance and current obtaining alternatives, not a claim that the 2023 document evaluates 2026 agents. A sufficient qualified assessment service is a valid alternative to local procedure development. Its narrower technical result still needs the actual personnel decision and effective act.

### HRM.2:12 - Relations

HRM.1 supplies the connected work and terms; HRM.3 supplies reward terms; HRM.4 supplies voice and challenge; HRM.5 constructs the resulting transition. OPS.22 supplies a warranted operating finding, HCD capability evidence, and qualified assessment suppliers the necessary validation. ADM and OCE establish the effective personnel relation and usable work.

### HRM.2:End


## HRM.3 - Design and revise remuneration and progression

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### HRM.3:1 - Problem frame

**Use this when** remuneration, benefits or progression need a justified design or revision: job demands have changed, external rates differ, pay relationships are unexplained, or reward undermines the contribution being requested. Begin by identifying the actual terms and which ground is unsettled. The first useful result is an explained, affordable and institutionally supportable reward arrangement with a usable transition.

The object is the reward arrangement and its grounds. If a current entitlement is clear and only payment is wrong, use ADM directly. An operating appraisal alone does not determine a wage obligation.

### HRM.3:2 - Problem

Different questions hide under “fair pay.” Job value concerns the demands of the work. Market positioning concerns an external comparison and the offer needed to obtain participation. Individual progression concerns a rule applied to a person. Variable reward concerns a defined result or contribution under stated conditions. Payment fulfills an obligation. Treating all five as one score obscures both unjustified differences and legitimate distinctions.

### HRM.3:3 - Forces

| Force | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Internal relationships and external alternatives | Explain both; a market premium does not itself settle equal-value or other institutional questions. |
| Contribution and controllability | Reward evidence must concern the contribution and conditions the rule actually names. |
| Incentive and cooperation | A local target can penalize helping, quality or protection that the work also requires. |
| Change and continuing obligations | A new design needs a lawful, affordable transition; a proposal does not rewrite existing entitlement. |

### HRM.3:4 - Solution

#### HRM.3:4.1 - Recover the reward question and existing commitments

Identify the affected jobs, people and time periods; the current terms; applicable agreements, minimums, equality and consultation requirements; the authority to alter them; and the available budget and payment capacity. Obtain qualified interpretation where an act depends on uncertain institutional grounds. Separate an owed amount from a prospective design choice.

Explain the contribution the arrangement is intended to support and the people for whom it must be obtainable. An employer may seek scarce capability, predictable service, progression, shared learning or some combination. Employees can value money, time, security, flexibility and opportunities differently. Obtain their relevant responses instead of assuming a universal preference ranking. Non-financial conditions can matter to an offer, but do not erase an existing monetary or protective obligation.

#### HRM.3:4.2 - Establish the different bases of comparison

For **job value**, obtain current information about actual work: skill and knowledge demands, responsibilities, effort, conditions and other factors justified by the selected evaluation approach. Include less visible coordination, care or helping demands when relevant. Compare jobs through a consistent, explained method with competent evaluators and participant information. Challenge factors, descriptions or weights that omit material work or reproduce an irrelevant hierarchy. Receive the rationale and limits of the evaluation, not only a grade label. A person's exceptional output should not silently alter the description of the job everyone in that grade is expected to do.

For **market position**, select comparators that genuinely concern the role, labor market, location, relation and period at issue. Examine what the reported amount includes and the evidence's coverage. A headline salary for a differently scoped role is not a reliable comparator. Decide how external rates will inform the offer alongside internal relationships, obligations and affordability. If a premium is warranted, make its basis, scope and reconsideration conditions intelligible. Obtain qualified compensation or market analysis where the comparison cannot otherwise support the decision.

For **individual progression**, specify the rule and evidence: for example, an agreed service condition, demonstrated capability in specified work, a changed responsibility or a defined contribution. These are different possible grounds, not interchangeable proxies. Distinguish eligibility to progress, assessment of the relevant condition, the authorized decision and its effective date. Use HRM.2 where the progression rests on a personnel assessment.

For **variable reward**, establish what outcome or contribution qualifies, the period, attribution, thresholds or other rule, uncertainty and limits of participant control. An OPS.22 finding becomes a reward input only when its interpretation fits that rule. Output from an agent or a team does not automatically identify the individual contribution. Do not reward apparent speed by creating an incentive to conceal defects, move work to colleagues or exceed protected hours.

#### HRM.3:4.3 - Construct a coherent reward arrangement

Combine the bases into terms people can understand: base pay or bands, justified supplements, progression, variable components, benefits and relevant non-financial conditions. Explain which component answers which question. A grade can organize job values while a separately justified premium responds to a market condition. A progression rule can use demonstrated capability while an allowance pays for a specified condition. One label need not pretend to explain every amount.

Compare the complete costs and cash demands with MA and, where applicable, FIN. Include transition obligations, administration and effects on other groups. A budget limit can require changed work, a smaller undertaking, phased discretionary changes or a different obtaining choice. It does not make an amount already owed disappear. Take an infeasible arrangement back to HRM.1 rather than presenting an unfunded promise as motivation.

Examine the behaviors the combined terms make attractive. If advisers must mentor, the workload and reward need to allow that work. If a sales measure is used, examine its connection to acceptable service and continuing commitments. If benefits or flexible arrangements are offered, verify who can actually use them. Ask what would happen if participants followed the rule carefully; do not rely on unpaid goodwill to repair a defective design.

#### HRM.3:4.4 - Test comparisons and provide challenge

Apply the proposed arrangement to materially different jobs and cases, including a changed job, a new entrant, an established contributor, a person with an applicable adjustment and a relevant boundary case. Trace a difference to its stated basis. The purpose is not to force every amount to be equal; it is to identify unexplained, unsupported or impermissible differences and inconsistent treatment under the chosen rule.

Where a difference raises an equality or other institutional question, obtain the applicable qualified judgement and remedy. A statistical gap, a market story and a job-evaluation result have different evidential roles. Do not use one as a universal exoneration or verdict. Use HRM.4 for required participation, negotiation or contest. Allow correction of the work description and evidence, not merely appeals against an opaque final score.

#### HRM.3:4.5 - Give the arrangement effect and follow its consequences

Obtain the required decision or agreement and specify the transition: who is affected, what changes, when, which prior commitments continue, and what information or action the person and providers need. Make the entitlement, effective date and payment instruction usable through ADM. Reconcile actual provision with the terms. A correct policy file and a payroll implementation can diverge.

Observe whether the arrangement obtains the intended participation and contribution under its conditions. Distinguish recruitment response, retention, helping behavior, burden, pay distribution and fulfillment of obligations. Reopen the relevant basis when jobs, markets, rules or consequences change. Do not infer that a person lacks motivation because an offer is unattractive, or change a progression rule retrospectively because its authorized result is inconvenient.

### HRM.3:5 - Archetypal Grounding

A service changes from routine reporting to advice and paid mentoring. A qualified job evaluation finds greater responsibility in the advisory work. The employer constructs an illustrative annual band of 60–72 units and a progression rule based on specified advisory capability; these numbers demonstrate the reasoning, not market or legal benchmarks. A market comparison supports considering a separate 4-unit supplement for a scarce specialty. The employer must still establish that the resulting terms and differences meet its applicable obligations and budget.

An experienced adviser receives 68 base units and mentors new colleagues. A proposed bonus based solely on billable hours would penalize those agreed mentoring hours. The owner changes the target to the attainable delivery contribution after the allocated mentoring time and specifies how both contributions are evidenced. Paying a larger bonus on an impossible target would not solve the incompatibility.

One employee challenges the omission of substantial coordination demands from another job's description. HRM.4 obtains a competent examination; the valuation is reconsidered from the corrected work information. If, instead, the description and rule are sound but payroll omitted an effective supplement, ADM corrects the payment. Those are different repairs.

### HRM.3:6 - Bias-Annotation

Job descriptions and market categories can omit work disproportionately performed by particular groups. Past pay is not an independent valuation of present work. Include appropriate participant information and professionally warranted comparisons. A favorable average can conceal unequal access to a benefit or a progression opportunity.

### HRM.3:7 - Conformance Checklist

- Can each material pay difference be traced to an applicable obligation or an explained job, market, progression or variable-reward ground?
- Do actual job information and comparison populations support those grounds?
- Are helping work, protection and other commitments compatible with the incentives and time allocation?
- Is the complete arrangement affordable without erasing owed terms?
- Are required participation, decision, effective date, payment and challenge obtainable?
- Does observation distinguish the intended contribution from distribution, burden and fulfillment?

Recognizing a coherent structure does not establish local legality, market adequacy or causal incentive effects; obtain the corresponding evidence.

### HRM.3:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

**Appraisal determines everything.** Apply the actual reward rule and distinguish job value from individual work. **The market settles fairness.** Examine the institutional and internal comparison separately. **Grade equals complete pay explanation.** Expose supplements and other terms. **Culture compensates for unpaid work.** Reconcile the promised work and terms. **Automation authorizes a pay reduction.** Obtain a supported prospective decision under the applicable obligations.

### HRM.3:9 - Consequences

The arrangement becomes explainable and revisable by its grounds. It can expose a need for professional valuation, institutional interpretation or changed work rather than a motivational campaign. Greater transparency can reveal genuine conflicts that require negotiation or determination. The method does not promise that every participant will prefer the result.

### HRM.3:10 - Architectural Rationale

Reward connects work, contribution, alternatives and institutional terms. It is distinct from measuring performance and fulfilling payment, while depending on both. This separation permits a pay correction without reassessment and a job revaluation without rewriting a person's past performance.

### HRM.3:11 - SoTA-Echoing

ILO's 2026 treatment of gender-neutral job evaluation supplies the equal-value comparison of job demands and correction of omissions. CIPD's reward account supplies the broader combination of base pay, progression, variable reward, benefits and internal/external positioning. This Method adapts those distinct contributions into one construction with explicit transitions and returns. Neither source supplies every jurisdiction's rules or a universal optimal pay formula. Qualified local compensation provision can be the sufficient alternative; the recipient still needs its terms, grounds and implementation conditions.

### HRM.3:12 - Relations

HRM.1 resolves wider work/practice incompatibilities; HRM.2 supports an assessment-dependent progression claim; HRM.4 provides participation and challenge; HRM.5 applies terms to a particular transition. OPS.22 supplies operating findings at their scope. MA and FIN supply economic and funding grounds; ADM realizes obligations and payments. Professional pay-equity, market and institutional suppliers retain their own judgement.

### HRM.3:End

## HRM.4 - Obtain employee voice and handle a contested personnel decision

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### HRM.4:1 - Problem frame

**Use this when** affected people need to influence a work or personnel decision, raise a concern, negotiate terms, or challenge an application that requires a competent response. Begin by recovering the issue in their terms and identifying the result and process it needs. The first useful result is an obtainable route to influence, determination or remedy, followed through to its actual use.

The object is the participation or contested-decision process. Use a sufficient routine correction directly. Do not make a formal grievance the price of obtaining a clear payment correction or preventive protection.

### HRM.4:2 - Problem

A survey can collect opinions without granting influence. Consultation can occur after every meaningful choice is closed. A grievance can be sent to the person whose action is challenged without an adequate alternative. A polite discussion can leave authority, evidence and remedy unresolved. Treating these as communication or motivation failures prevents the necessary institutional work.

### HRM.4:3 - Forces

| Force | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Openness and protection | People need usable channels and appropriate information boundaries; blanket promises of secrecy can be false. |
| Timely resolution and adequate inquiry | Use a proportionate process that preserves applicable rights and decision requirements. |
| Management authority and independent interests | Authority can determine a matter without making every objection invalid or requiring consensus. |
| Informal resolution and institutional duties | A useful conversation can supplement, but cannot displace, a required process or external route. |

### HRM.4:4 - Solution

#### HRM.4:4.1 - Identify the question, urgency and applicable route

Obtain the concern or proposed contribution without requiring the participant to use HR terminology. Establish what is being sought: information, correction, influence over a proposal, negotiated terms, investigation of conduct, determination of an entitlement, appeal or a protective response. Several questions can arise from the same incident and require coordinated but different acts.

Identify the actual relationship, jurisdiction, agreements, representation and decision rules. Obtain a qualified process answer when these are uncertain: the eligible participants, authority, required steps and timing, information rights, independence and routes outside the organization. Do not turn one country's example into a universal procedure. Where a credible issue requires urgent protection or preservation, obtain the applicable response while the merits remain unresolved. Neither delay nor a provisional protective measure establishes the final conclusion.

Select an obtainable route. A person must know how to reach it, what response to expect and where to go when the usual manager is involved in the complaint or cannot provide the necessary independence. Respect applicable external reporting, representative and appeal routes. Do not promise protection from every consequence or absolute confidentiality that the process cannot provide; explain the actual safeguards, limits and permitted disclosures.

#### HRM.4:4.2 - Make influence possible before the relevant choice closes

For consultation, state the proposed change, its reasons, the decisions still open, who decides and when participant input can change them. Supply information needed to form a useful view, subject to justified limits; explain a consequential limit rather than using it to conceal all grounds. Obtain input through appropriate direct or representative means, including access for those affected by schedules, location or other barriers.

Interpret contributions for the premises they challenge or alternatives they supply. A worker's account of interruption can change a capacity estimate. A concern about an automated promotion measure can expose an invalid inference. Distinguish objections to the purpose, proposed means, terms and implementation. Answer the substance and explain the resulting decision or why a proposal was not adopted. The useful result is actual opportunity and response, not agreement with management or a high survey response rate.

For negotiation, establish what each party may commit and which terms are discretionary. Develop feasible alternatives and reconcile the whole offer. Use the applicable collective bargaining or individual agreement process. A willing representative without the necessary mandate cannot bind the group; a negotiated preference cannot erase a binding protection. LDR can help construct joint contributions while the institutional process retains its own requirements.

#### HRM.4:4.3 - Obtain an adequate inquiry for a contested matter

State the issue to be determined, relevant rule, competent decision maker and needed independence. Determine what information can establish the facts and what each affected party must have an opportunity to explain or challenge. Use a proportionate investigator or other qualified supplier where the subject or conflict requires it. A manager, specialist or agent may contribute according to actual competence, independence and permitted authority.

Distinguish allegation, observation, interpretation, missing information and supported finding. Seek material evidence from relevant sources, preserve its context and examine plausible alternatives. Provide the required notice, opportunity to respond, representation and access under the applicable process. Protect unrelated private information. Do not assume that an algorithmic record is a complete account or that a lack of a formal complaint establishes absence of harm.

The necessary inquiry varies. A pay-date error may be established from a current appointment and record. A disputed job valuation needs the work and evaluation grounds. A conduct allegation may need a different inquiry, privacy and protective arrangement. A medical cause belongs with a clinician, not an adversarial HR credibility test. If the evidence cannot determine the issue under the applicable standard, state the unresolved question and warranted next act rather than manufacture certainty.

#### HRM.4:4.4 - Determine, explain and provide the remedy

The authorized decision maker applies the applicable rule to supported findings, considers the required alternatives and consequences, and issues the determination through the proper process. Keep the factual conclusion, its institutional effect and any discretionary response distinguishable. A correction can be owed even when no one acted with bad intent; an adverse outcome does not alone prove misconduct.

Explain the decision, its grounds and effect to the appropriate recipients, with permitted disclosure and the applicable challenge or appeal route. Obtain required independence for reconsideration. The appeal question may concern facts, procedure, interpretation or remedy; choose the contribution that can actually reconsider it. A second summary of the first decision is not an independent examination merely because a different person or agent wrote it.

Translate the remedy into work. Assign correction of pay to ADM, reassessment to HRM.2, revaluation to HRM.3, changed work or support to OCE/OPS and HRM.5. Specify the owner, effective conditions and how provision can be checked. A determination without an obtainable remedy can leave the original harm unchanged.

#### HRM.4:4.5 - Examine recurrence without suppressing voice

Check whether the participation opportunity, determination and remedy actually occurred. Obtain relevant participant response and examine recurrence or adverse treatment under the applicable protections. Where a concern reveals a shared defective premise, return it to HRM.1 or the owning Method. Do not treat fewer complaints as proof of improvement: access may have deteriorated. Likewise, one substantiated error does not justify discarding every adequate decision from the same practice.

### HRM.4:5 - Archetypal Grounding

A company proposes using community contributions in promotion. Employees say that evening participation favors people with free time and that private learning discussions were never intended as selection evidence. Consultation occurs while the promotion design is still open. The owner distinguishes the proposed contribution from the proposed evidence and terms. HRM.2 develops a work-relevant demonstration during obtainable paid time; private developmental material remains outside the selection use unless separately justified and permitted. The response changes the design rather than merely explaining it more enthusiastically.

Later, an employee challenges a promotion application because their work evidence was omitted. The competent process examines the omission and its effect under the existing criteria. If the omission could change the comparison, a qualified reassessment supplies the corrected recommendation; the authorized decision and its remedy follow. This does not require reopening the entire culture programme.

A different concern reports threatening conduct by the usual manager. That route cannot rely solely on asking the same manager to resolve it informally. Obtain the applicable alternative and protective response, then the competent inquiry. The example selects a process; it does not supply one universal legal procedure.

### HRM.4:6 - Bias-Annotation

Power, dependency, language, access and fear of consequences can influence what is reported. Representatives and managers can each miss some participants' concerns. Obtain appropriate direct and representative evidence without treating every account as already established fact. A corporate agent must not obtain private conversations simply because they might make investigation easier.

### HRM.4:7 - Conformance Checklist

- Is the desired result distinguished from the channel: information, influence, negotiation, determination, appeal or remedy?
- Is the selected process applicable and actually reachable, with required representation, authority and independence?
- Can participant input still change the relevant decision?
- Does inquiry distinguish facts, interpretations and uncertainty and provide the required opportunity to respond?
- Are the decision, challenge route and remedy usable and appropriately communicated?
- Does follow-through examine actual provision and recurring conditions rather than only complaint counts?

The presence of a grievance policy is recognition evidence only. Assurance requires its applicability and actual performance for the matter at hand.

### HRM.4:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

**Survey equals voice.** Specify the influence and response. **Consultation after commitment.** Recover the choices still open or explain that a different process is required. **Objection equals disloyalty.** Investigate the challenged premise. **Dialogue replaces procedure.** Retain applicable rights, external routes and competent determination. **Another reader equals independence.** Establish the relationship and conditions that make reconsideration independent.

### HRM.4:9 - Consequences

Participants gain a usable route to influence or correction; the organization can discover flawed premises before they spread. The process can reveal real conflicts and require remedies, not merely improve perceived engagement. It needs competent provision and may be constrained by unavailable evidence or external proceedings. Agreement is not guaranteed.

### HRM.4:10 - Architectural Rationale

Employee relations produces institutional influence and warranted responses to contested personnel acts. Leadership, community work and psychological support can contribute, but none supplies the missing decision powers or procedure by itself. Keeping the act distinct makes its result usable by assessment, reward and participation Methods.

### HRM.4:11 - SoTA-Echoing

The bounded Acas Code example for Great Britain distinguishes investigation, notice, response, decision and appeal. This Method adopts their distinct functions as a useful comparator while requiring the actual jurisdictional process; it does not generalize the Code's legal force. The plural HR field account retains differing interests and power. An adequate existing employee-relations service can supply the required process with less local construction. An engagement programme or informal coaching is insufficient when the result needed is a competent determination or remedy.

### HRM.4:12 - Relations

HRM.1 receives challenged configuration premises. HRM.2 and HRM.3 supply the substantive assessment or reward grounds; HRM.5 supplies particular participation consequences. ADM and work owners realize remedies. LDR can support negotiation and joint action; CB supports community participation. Qualified institutional and subject suppliers determine the process and professional questions that remain outside this Method.

### HRM.4:End


## HRM.5 - Arrange continuing participation and work transitions

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### HRM.5:1 - Problem frame

**Use this when** a person's current work arrangement needs to continue differently, pause, resume, move or end. The trigger can be an opportunity after success, a changed personal purpose, functional difficulty, a qualified restriction, absence, a retention proposal or an intended exit. Begin with the person's actual relationship, circumstances, response and existing commitments. The first useful result is an obtainable arrangement, or the exact missing professional or institutional result that prevents it.

The object is the person's continuing participation relationship and proposed transition. Independent personal development can remain outside HR. A sufficient routine leave or transfer instruction can be used directly. A complaint or poor output is not a prerequisite for protective action, access to medical assessment or the resulting work response.

### HRM.5:2 - Problem

A career conversation can promise work that does not exist. Retention can be pursued against the person's purpose. A return can be recorded while the tasks and hours remain incompatible with a qualified restriction. Severe exhaustion can be treated as deficient motivation. These failures arise when the desired outcome is named without constructing the work, support, terms and decision that could make it attainable.

### HRM.5:3 - Forces

| Force | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Personal purpose and organizational need | Examine their actual intersection; a useful exit can be a supported result. |
| Protection and discretionary offers | A binding adjustment or restriction is not a retention bargaining chip. |
| Privacy and usable work information | Receive the functional conditions needed by the work owner without unnecessary clinical disclosure. |
| A nominal transition and actual provision | Verify the work, access, time and support, not only the personnel record. |
| Continuity and changed conditions | Preserve current obligations while establishing the next arrangement and its return conditions. |

### HRM.5:4 - Solution

#### HRM.5:4.1 - Recover the relationship and the reason for change

Establish the current work, terms and obligations; what has changed or become possible; the person's relevant aims and response; and who can supply or authorize a change. Ask what the person can actually do and obtain under current conditions. Distinguish a preference for different work, missing capability, inaccessible opportunity, unavailable support, a health-related limitation and an institutional dispute. They can coexist, but require different contributions.

Choose the branch by its grounds. A discretionary career proposal seeks a mutually workable next contribution. An accommodation, sickness-related absence or return can be governed by particular duties and qualified constraints. An employer-initiated termination requires its own applicable grounds and procedure. Do not recast all branches as a negotiation to retain talent. Where the current arrangement is adequate and the person wants no change, sufficient continuation is a legitimate result.

#### HRM.5:4.2 - Receive functional difficulty and obtain medical or safety help

Take a serious complaint or observable functional difficulty as a reason to obtain the relevant qualified contribution, even when work output remains satisfactory. A person reporting that they have no strength for ordinary activity needs access to medical assessment, not an instruction to demonstrate more commitment. Establish how the applicable healthcare, occupational-health or other competent service can be reached and what HR can properly arrange. Where urgent medical help is indicated, use the appropriate urgent route; a personnel process does not replace it.

Apply available and required interim support, leave and work-change arrangements under their actual grounds. Do not require a final diagnosis before every temporary protective response that the governing conditions already permit or require. Equally, do not prescribe a disease, duration of rest, exercise programme or treatment as an HR remedy. The clinical supplier investigates causes and determines its own recommendations. Psychological support can be part of suitable help while a needed medical inquiry remains necessary.

For a work-related question, give the qualified supplier an accurate description of relevant tasks, demands, hours, exposures, tools and possible adaptations, with permitted information from the person. Ask for the usable functional conditions, their scope, uncertainty and review point, and any contribution that remains unresolved. Determine separately what the employer must or may do under the applicable institutional rules. A clinician's conclusion, a right to an adjustment and an authorized work assignment have different functions.

Receive and disclose only information needed and permitted for the work decision. The work owner may need limits on hours, demands or particular tasks and a review condition. The underlying clinical history can remain with the appropriate professional holder. Set access and purpose for agents as well as people. Do not use confidential health or personal-assistant material to infer promotion suitability merely because it is technically accessible.

#### HRM.5:4.3 - Construct a feasible continuation, absence or return

With the person and responsible work owners, identify attainable assignments and conditions. Consider changes to tasks, sequence, hours, location, tools, support, coordination and coverage where they address the actual constraint. Use OCE for the organizational change and OPS for workload, service and capacity consequences. Compare the entire arrangement: moving one task can transfer a burden to a colleague or leave a service promise unsupported.

Join the work to its personnel terms: applicable pay, leave, benefits, permissions, duration or reconsideration condition, and the required agreement or determination. Use qualified institutional guidance for accommodation and absence duties. Some adaptations are required; others are discretionary choices among feasible options. A person's agreement does not waive a binding protection. If no proposed option is feasible, identify which condition prevents it and obtain the required further contribution or determination; do not invent a right, deny one from convenience, or impose an unsupported return.

A return arrangement needs actual work that fits the current conditions. Specify who supplies it, how access and support become available, how colleagues receive the information necessary for coordination, and how the person can report difficulty. A staged or limited arrangement can be appropriate when its grounds support it; there is no universal schedule. Attendance, a successful task and absence of a complaint do not independently establish recovery.

#### HRM.5:4.4 - Construct development and career movement from actual opportunity

For a desired next contribution, examine what the person wants to become able to do and which work or opportunity could use it. This can follow success rather than failure. Compare an internal move, a changed role, a temporary assignment, external practice, obtained help, further learning or an independent trajectory. The organization is not obliged by this Method to fund every preference, and the person's purpose is not exhausted by the current employer's need.

If human acquisition is selected, use HCD and C.40.JD to connect the way, holder and provision. Establish attainable practice, competent help, feedback and receiving work, including the time and resources actually offered. Separate evidence of learning from a promise of promotion. HRM.2 supplies a later personnel inference; HRM.3 supplies progression terms. A developmental portfolio should not silently change into employer selection data.

When adequate AI or another provider can supply the work, reconsider the acquisition choice. The person may still choose the capability for another purpose; the employer may select a different contribution. Develop the actual shared arrangement or acknowledge the absence of one. A larger course catalogue cannot resolve a disagreement about which future work or terms either party will undertake.

#### HRM.5:4.5 - Handle retention and exit as actual choices and obligations

For retention, understand the reason the person is considering leaving and which changes they regard as meaningful. Construct an offer the organization can actually supply, including work, time, reward and support. Obtain the person's response without treating refusal as evidence of deficient capability or loyalty. Do not promise a future role whose existence or authority remains unresolved. If no acceptable arrangement exists, an orderly exit can preserve the person's trajectory and the organization's remaining commitments.

For a chosen or required exit, identify the applicable notice, consultation, decision, representation, payment and other obligations through the competent institutional process. Distinguish resignation, expiry, restructuring and other grounds; they do not share one universal procedure. Preserve routes for contest where applicable. Arrange the permitted transfer of work, knowledge, access and responsibilities with OCE/OPS and ADM. Do not depend on unlimited unpaid handover or continuing access to private material. A handover duty, a learning contribution and future voluntary help need their own grounds.

An employer-initiated action cannot be supported merely by labeling a person a poor cultural fit or an unsuccessful return. Obtain the relevant facts, professional conditions and applicable process. HRM.4 handles a contested decision; this Method makes the warranted transition and its provision obtainable.

#### HRM.5:4.6 - Make the arrangement effective and check actual participation

Obtain the required agreement or authorized act, effective time and instructions. Assign provision to capable owners: work, access, pay, leave, equipment, coverage, help and any qualified review. The person should know what is expected, what they can obtain, which information is shared and how to reopen a problem. A personal agent may help them understand or negotiate; a corporate agent may implement a permitted act. Keep their mandates and data boundaries distinct.

Check the receiving work and terms against the arrangement. Are reduced hours respected in assignments and messages? Does the promised mentor have time? Did leave and pay take effect? Can the returning person obtain the allowed tasks? Do not measure success solely as attendance, retention or output. Preserve the professional review condition, participant response and appropriate evidence of actual provision.

When circumstances, qualified advice or opportunities change, reopen the affected part with its owner. A changed medical restriction can require new work and terms without invalidating earlier good output or owed pay. A new service arrangement can remove a development opportunity without erasing the acquired capability. Recurring failures in several individual arrangements return to HRM.1 for a configuration change; they are not automatically individual motivation problems.

### HRM.5:5 - Archetypal Grounding

An employee's AI-assisted reports remain accurate, but they report profound exhaustion and inability to manage ordinary activity. HR facilitates the applicable medical access and available interim absence or work-change arrangements. It does not diagnose “adrenal fatigue,” interpret the statement as resistance or prescribe prolonged bed rest. The clinical inquiry remains with its qualified supplier.

Suppose the received professional result supports a period away from work and later a limited schedule, with a specified review condition. Under the applicable rules, the authorized owner grants the relevant absence and terms. The work owner reallocates deadlines and coverage; ADM implements the leave and payment instruction. On return, tasks fit the limitation and messages do not create hidden out-of-hours work. HR checks actual provision and obtains the relevant response without distributing the medical history. The constructed example assumes these professional and institutional results; it does not establish that this arrangement is appropriate for every exhausted person.

At review, the qualified restriction changes. Only the affected work, support and terms are reconsidered. Existing pay obligations and good report evidence remain. If a new workload again exceeds supported conditions, the team-level prevention question returns to HRM.1 even if this employee makes no further complaint.

In a different case, a successful engineer wants to develop an adjacent advisory practice. The employer can offer a bounded assignment and competent help but cannot promise a permanent role. The parties establish that actual opportunity, its time and later decision conditions. If the engineer instead wants work the employer will not supply, they may pursue it independently or leave. Retaining the person at any cost is not the Method's required result.

### HRM.5:6 - Bias-Annotation

The employer's preference for continuity can dominate the person's aims, and apparent agreement can conceal dependence or lack of an accessible alternative. Obtain the person's response and preserve applicable protections. Avoid equating disability, absence or a need for accommodation with inability to contribute generally. Conversely, a single successful task does not settle a functional or health question.

### HRM.5:7 - Conformance Checklist

- Is the proposed transition grounded in the actual relation, circumstances, aims and obligations?
- Are clinical or safety questions obtained from qualified suppliers, with serious functional difficulty taken seriously before output failure?
- Are required constraints distinguished from discretionary preferences and supplied through actual work and terms?
- Does the arrangement have obtainable tasks, support, coverage, access and effective provision?
- Are private information and personal/corporate agent mandates bounded by the actual purpose and permissions?
- Can change reopen the affected conditions, and can a supported exit remain a legitimate result?

A plausible agreement or return plan is recognition evidence. Assurance concerns its applicable grounds and whether the work, support and terms actually obtain.

### HRM.5:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

**Exhaustion becomes a motivation problem.** Obtain the medical receiving contribution and appropriate work response. **Return equals attendance.** Check attainable tasks and conditions. **Retention equals success.** Preserve independent aims and a warranted exit. **Development equals an enrollment.** Obtain practice and receiving work. **A restriction is written but not implemented.** Change assignments, provision and effective terms. **Consent grants unlimited medical access.** Establish the actual permitted purpose and minimum needed information.

### HRM.5:9 - Consequences

The person's proposed continuation or transition becomes a concrete arrangement rather than an aspiration. It can require changing service commitments, obtaining external help or recognizing that no currently feasible option exists. Privacy limits some convenient information sharing. Medical assessment and institutional determination can remain necessary dependencies. The Method promises neither recovery on a schedule nor universal retention.

### HRM.5:10 - Architectural Rationale

Career movement, supported continuation, absence, return and exit share the construction of a particular participation arrangement: recover grounds, obtain feasible work and terms, give them effect and reconsider changed conditions. They differ in their grounds and powers. That common operation justifies one Method while preserving the clinical, protective, contractual and discretionary branches. Population-wide work configuration remains with HRM.1.

### HRM.5:11 - SoTA-Echoing

WHO's account distinguishes prevention through work conditions, individual support, accommodation and return to work. This Method develops the HR receiving and implementation operations; it does not claim coverage of the full WHO guideline or every occupational hazard. The 2025 integrated-talent proposal supplies a serious alternative that can support personal trajectories outside the employer, while its proposed benefits remain unvalidated. A sufficient existing absence, accommodation or career service can provide the necessary contribution directly. The framework adds the connection to actual work, terms and selective return; it supplies no clinical treatment protocol or universal employment procedure.

### HRM.5:12 - Relations

HRM.1 handles wider incompatibilities; HRM.2 supports assessment-dependent movement; HRM.3 supplies reward terms; HRM.4 supplies voice, negotiation or contest. OCE and OPS provide work changes and coverage; ADM makes terms and provision effective. HCD, C.40.JD and the relevant professional practice supply selected development. Qualified clinical, safety and institutional suppliers retain the judgements on which protection, absence and return depend.

### HRM.5:End
