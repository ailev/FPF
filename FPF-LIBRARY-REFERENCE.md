# FPF Library Architectural Reference

**DocID:** `Library` within **FPF Library**.

**Architecture and connected use of the Library's pattern languages**

- **Author:** Anatoly Levenchuk, with AI-assisted development
- **Date:** 24 October 2026
- **Use:** Understand the Library's architecture, select the appropriate level of explanation and preserve the relations between contributions from different fields.
- **Full publications:** [FPF Core](FPF-Spec.md), the [domain-framework catalogue](DPFs/) and the publications linked below.

FPF Library gives people and AI agents a connected language for reasoning about work, constructing useful results and developing better ways to obtain them. As a methodology of open-ended development, it can serve as a canon for a shared culture of thinking and acting. Its common distinctions and general Methods meet specialized knowledge in domain frameworks. A reader can use one sufficient contribution directly or follow a larger question across several publications.

This Reference explains how that language is organized and why its boundaries matter. It develops the shared account across individual frameworks: what the larger wholes are, how their contributions connect, which alternatives explain the arrangement and what changes when a relied-on premise changes. The full patterns supply the Methods, cases and source grounds. For practical search and reading, use [Using FPF Library](USING-FPF.md); the [FPF Library overview](./) gives the short introduction.

## Library.Reference:1 - One connected language, several kinds of whole

The Library becomes useful when a question crosses boundaries that an isolated answer leaves unresolved. A sound model may be unusable with the available observations. A feasible design may lack an authorized commitment. A helpful AI answer may leave the person unable to make the next judgement independently. In each case, the missing contribution has to be identified and connected to the work that consumes it.

The common language makes these differences expressible. Core distinguishes a System, a Method, its description, performed Work, capability, evidence and authority. The domain languages explain what those distinctions require in particular practices. A mathematical consequence, a corporate decision and an acquired bodily skill therefore retain different construction and qualification conditions while participating in one undertaking.

FPF Library is an organized collection of related framework publications with this connected public account. Its unity of description comes from shared meanings, general Methods, explicit dependencies and developed returns between contributions. That does not establish one discipline above every field in the collection. A Library-wide explanation can be necessary even when the explained publications describe several independently constituted disciplines.

Within the Library, a Suite makes a stronger subject claim: it describes a composite discipline through constituent DPFs and their connections. Engineering, Management, Foundational Thinking and Human Capability Engineering each have an account of that whole. A DPF outside those Suites keeps its own subject; its Methods can supply contributions to the same work. Neither arrangement determines how many publications a particular task needs.

This distinction follows [FPF E.4's ecosystem architecture](FPF-Spec.md#e4---fpf-ecosystem-architecture-framework-families-products-and-dpf-suites) and [C.20's construction of a discipline (FPF C.20:4)](FPF-Spec.md#c204---solution---recover-the-whole-from-direct-construction). The requirement for a shared explanation, including alternatives, whole conditions and worked connections, comes from [FPF E.11.PFP:4.7](FPF-Spec.md#e11pfp47---explain-the-methods-at-each-selected-scale).

### Library.Reference:1.1 - Long mantras and connected reasoning

A language of patterns needs more than useful separate Methods. Its reader also needs to understand why particular questions belong together, how their answers constrain one another, which alternatives remain possible and what a changed premise reopens. A **long mantra** gives a memorable traversal of that connected reasoning. The short wording helps hold it in attention; the full explanation supplies its reasons, subject knowledge and limits.

The system-thinking long mantra is one important instance. FPF A.1.STM connects the common project target and use with functioning, construction, ways of obtaining the system, capable creators and their cooperation. These questions connect large areas of engineering and organizational knowledge. The mantra shows where material laws, production techniques and cooperation arrangements are needed and why the supplied answers must fit together. [Engineering.Reference:0.5](DPFs/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/ENGINEERING-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md#engineeringreference05---the-system-thinking-long-mantra-across-engineering-contributions) explains the connection across that Suite.

Other developed accounts retain different subjects. The connections below are examples of full reasoning already available in the Library, not a requirement to give every framework the same formula.

The internal FPF Architectural Reference, FPF.Reference:16.10, explains how the common control construction relates the independently usable Core Methods. Here the question is how their results connect to the subject languages in this Library. Development is also a receiving use of the common control account. FPF C.43:4.8 explains how to choose an intervention from the attained repertoire, trace its effect through related Methods and provision, and use the resulting capability to form a further attainable problem. C.40.OD supplies the whole development construction; C.40.DC constructs directions, C.40.JD connects the Method, developing agent and conditions of use, and C.40.NP develops the continuation after a result. They preserve development of knowledge and possibilities beyond correction of current performance.

A development recommender uses these contributions to form advice rather than merely rank learning materials. Apply PSDA.14 in the Decision Design Principles Framework to obtain a grounded recommendation, and PSDA.15 to connect it to feasible observation and reconsideration. The actual choice, work and capability in receiving use determine what changed. Technical control, economic comparison and developmental recommendation can therefore share a connected reasoning Method while retaining their particular means and conclusions.

These accounts can meet in one undertaking without collapsing into one Method. A strategic decision can change the proposed system's use; an engineering limit can reopen a strategy; changing an offer can alter the knowledge and cooperation needed to fulfil it. Follow the shared subject or premise and bring its changed conditions to the reasoning that uses them. Shared words alone do not establish a dependency.

The teaching order makes a connection understandable one part at a time. Actual reasoning can begin at a later question, develop answers together, discover an incompatibility or return to an earlier assumption. The analogy with simultaneous equations captures this mutual constraint; the answers can be incompatible or non-unique. FPF A.22.CGUS develops the alternatives and conditions when that structure needs to be made explicit; its requirements for a formal claim are additional to using an ordinary reminder.

The general explanation of a mantra stays with the pattern or framework that supplies it. A Framework Architectural Reference connects the language's Methods; a Suite Reference explains the additional connections across its constituent fields; this Library Reference explains connections and boundaries across publications. README pages make the important entry visible. These levels should lead to the full reasoning rather than repeat several competing definitions or hide a missing Method behind a navigation link. FPF E.11.PFP:4.7.2 explains this publication arrangement.

#### Library.Reference:1.1.1 - Rhythmic coordination across different practices

The control construction also connects work whose timing must remain coordinated while individual actions vary. Apply FPF C.43:4.5.1 to establish the required temporal relations, fit preparation and recovery around shared means, and repair the particular relation that was lost. The following subject Methods explain different realizations; the common Method is available in Core without requiring any of these DPFs.

In the Embodied Rhythmics Principles Framework, RHY.11:4.4.1 and RHY.11:5.4 explain how an accent can change its place while a supporting pulse and subsequent action continue. RHY.10 distinguishes phase, tempo, grouping and the delivery of a cue. A delayed accent and an accent that delays the next pulse require different corrections. The shared result is deliberately coupled in time while its actions retain their required variation. If the human performer cannot realize the combination, RHY.12 develops that coordination; a notation or successful machine schedule does not supply the person's skill.

In Control Architecture Design, CAD.2 specifies event order, behavior between updates and reset conditions; CAD.5 relates sampling phase, information age and observation gaps to the model used for control. CAD.4 checks their shared implementation resources. A changed event time can change which information a command uses, so it returns to these constructions and the dependent feasibility or error calculation. The correspondence with rhythmics concerns the required temporal relations and their construction, not an imported model of human timing.

In Operations Management, OPS.13:4.5 selects periodic review, an event signal or their combination from the time and work needed for a useful response. OPS.10.2 supplies joint resource occupations, preparation and recovery, and the revision of the remaining schedule. An exception may need action before the next regular review; providing that response must also account for the work it interrupts and what must continue afterwards. The common choice is which temporal relations to preserve and which to change.

These connections preserve different outcomes. RHY develops an embodied performance, CAD an engineered control arrangement, and OPS workable organizational commitments and performance. The same timing requirement can reveal a missing relation in another field, while each field supplies the means and evidence that make its realization credible.

### Library.Reference:1.2 - Holonic thinking across different kinds of whole

Holonic thinking connects a whole with the parts and relations that constitute it, and with its possible contribution to a larger whole. It helps answer a practical question: **what must be connected, retained or changed for the whole to do what the work requires?** System thinking is one important application. The same question also arises when constructing a method, understanding performed work, assembling knowledge or developing a discipline.

Begin with the whole's needed contribution and conditions of use, or with an existing whole whose construction needs explanation. Recover or propose its parts and the relations through which their contributions combine. Then bring the result back to the parts. A requirement on the whole can change the choice of a part or its connection; a limit found in a part can require another whole construction or a narrower use. The useful result is a reason for retaining or changing that construction. Naming the whole and listing its parts leaves this reasoning unfinished.

The construction rule depends on the kind of whole. [FPF A.1](FPF-Spec.md#a1---recognize-a-whole-with-parts-uholon-and-admitted-holon-kinds) supplies the common holon criterion; the subject patterns explain what constitutes each kind.

| Whole being considered | What the holonic reasoning connects | Full contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| System | Constituents and their organization with functioning and use; the whole's required contribution with choices about its construction. | A.1 and A.1.STM, with the relevant system-composition Methods. |
| Method | Reusable ways of doing through their prerequisites, results, joins and alternatives; the combined way with the result it must obtain or preserve. | A.3.1 and B.1.5; B.1.5.RS for replacement and B.1.5.EW for constituent Methods in ongoing work. |
| Performed Work | Actual performance and its admitted sub-occurrences, including their time, performers and relations; the whole history with what the receiving work needs to establish. | A.15.1. |
| Episteme | Claims and their interpretation with the premises and inferential relations required by a conclusion. | C.2.1 and B.1.3. |
| Discipline | Knowledge that explains and constrains reusable practice with practice results that can test and revise that knowledge. | C.20. |

These are applications to different kinds of whole, not a closed classification of thinking. A system's components, a Method's constituents and a Work occurrence's parts have different identity and composition conditions. Reasoning across them preserves those differences.

The distinction is especially useful between **methodological thinking** and reasoning about **performed work**. Methodological thinking asks how a way of doing is selected, constructed, combined or changed. Its holonic application concerns a Method whole. An account of performed work asks what actually happened and how its parts belong to that occurrence. A reusable laboratory Method can permit a retry; a particular test can contain an interruption and two attempts. The permitted branch and the performed attempts answer different questions. A five-minute log window alone identifies neither a submethod nor an admitted Work part. Plans and descriptions of future work retain their prospective meaning.

The connections between kinds make the shared account useful. Knowledge supplies grounds and limits for constructing or selecting a Method. Capable Systems perform Work that enacts Methods. Results of that Work can support claims used to revise a Method or a system design. The relevant discipline can organize this continuing relation between knowledge and practice. Each connection needs its own explanation: enactment, production, evidential use and parthood are different relations.

For example, a laboratory Method compares two readings and repeats a reading when their difference exceeds a tolerance. If the receiving scientific claim requires independent measurement paths, two readings from the same dependent path cannot supply that independence. The required conclusion changes the Method and the provision of apparatus. A better account of the Work history helps establish what was performed, but does not make the method or apparatus adequate by itself. The whole's requirement thus changes the parts through a specific reason.

To develop an explanation at this scale, use [C.39.WA](FPF-Spec.md#c39wa---develop-an-account-of-the-whole-subject). Recover the question and its subject, construct the relevant grounds of unity, and show how they govern the choice and change of parts. Connect worked cases to that explicit construction and vary them where its premises or branches differ. When a local result must answer an encompassing request, [C.39.QR](FPF-Spec.md#c39qr---return-results-to-the-original-question) supplies the return: identify the actual relation, justify the inference, compare what is now available with the original use and obtain the missing contribution. These Methods organize the reasoning; the subject patterns above supply the relevant conditions of composition and identity.

#### Library.Reference:1.2.1 - Related forms of thinking retain their questions

The holonic aspect of system thinking concerns Systems, the holonic aspect of methodological thinking concerns Methods, and the holonic aspect of reasoning about performed work concerns Work. These distinctions help explain the common contribution. The familiar names also have wider uses. A.1.STM connects the proposed system with its intended use, creators, Methods and cooperation; OPS.23 connects work with capacity, commitments, service, money and people's conditions. Neither account becomes complete by identifying one holon kind.

Management and strategic reasoning connect several of these subjects. C.43 relates observations, choices and actions under the conditions of the same continuation. STR.18 relates worthwhile contributions, their obtaining ways, feasible means and commitments. Their holonic questions can concern systems, methods, work and knowledge together. “Strategic” and “operational” name different questions here; the words alone place neither the thinker nor the object at a particular system level.

Mathematical thinking also has several contributions. A proof account or theory can be considered as a connected whole of claims. A reusable proof Method can compose submethods. Mathematical thinking additionally constructs objects, operations, representations, witnesses and countermodels, and develops conjectures. A mathematical object that a claim concerns is different from the claim-bearing Episteme. [MATH.Reference:1–3](DPFs/Foundational%20Thinking%20DPF%20Suite/MATHEMATICAL-PRACTICE-DPF.md#mathreference1---problem-frame---construct-mathematics-for-the-question) develops that broader repertoire.

This distinction changes the next action. An adequate proof of existence may settle one mathematical question while a later use requires a way to obtain a witness. MATH.12 examines what construction the proof supplies; another mathematical contribution may be needed. Calling the proof an Episteme does not produce that contribution. A constructive account of mathematical work preserves the distinction between existence, a witness-producing operation and its execution; it need not exclude classical reasoning.

The synthesis connects these different contributions through what one supplies and another needs. It does not establish that all their originating disciplines are parts of one further discipline. C.20 supplies the stronger construction when that claim matters.

#### Library.Reference:1.2.2 - Use and translate the terms through their subject

In introductory Russian, «холоническое мышление о системах, методах, выполненных работах, знании и дисциплинах» makes the subjects explicit. The corresponding English phrase is “holonic thinking about systems, methods, performed work, knowledge and disciplines.” Use a familiar shorter adjective where the surrounding explanation preserves that subject and its operation.

Existing usages can differ. English “systematic” can mean methodical organization; it is not a substitute for FPF's “system thinking.” Richmond's [Operational Thinking](https://thesystemsthinker.com/operational-thinking/) concerns how actual processes work and their causal organization. The French historical expression *pensée opératoire* concerns logical operations in [Piaget's account](https://www.unige.ch/piaget/piaget1947PIN_03). Neither expression should be translated into the Work-holon application merely from its word form.

[E.10's lexical guidance](FPF-Spec.md#e1099---thinking-terms-and-the-whole-being-considered) applies the distinction in authoring. Preserve the source meaning, make the intended subject and relation clear, and use the full subject Method when a decision depends on its construction. The shared explanation gives an entry into that work; recognition of a holonic question and assurance that a particular whole satisfies its conditions remain separate results.

### Library.Reference:1.3 - A canon for shared human–AI culture

The Library articulates a culture of thinking and acting oriented toward the open-ended development of people, AI agents and their joint practice. It can serve as that culture’s **canon**: a common, revisable body of explanations, methods, worked cases and grounds for judgement. Participants can use this repertoire to choose and perform work, examine its results and develop better ways of continuing. The canon makes the shared practice recoverable and revisable across different situations.

People and AI agents can both sustain and renew this culture through their work. An AI contribution may be assistance with one operation or participation as a teammate responsible for an agreed contribution. The working arrangement determines who can perform, decide, check and change what. Shared use therefore combines different capabilities and responsibilities; each participant needs access to the contributions relevant to its work. No individual has to internalize the whole Library. [C.32.MWA.PP](FPF-Spec.md#c32mwapp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-provision-arrangements) explains how to construct and compare such arrangements.

Acquiring and sustaining the practice requires different means for different participants. People may need explanation, repeated practice, feedback and support for participation. AI agents need suitable capabilities, access to the relevant material, execution support and continuity of attention and task context. Giving an agent a text can enable a contribution; its subsequent work shows whether it actually used the method and obtained a useful result. [C.36.RP and C.36.MC](#libraryreference917---how-does-a-useful-episode-become-a-renewable-cultural-contribution) connect the shared repertoire to these different ways of obtaining, reconstructing and renewing practice.

For example, a person and an AI teammate can use the same engineering methods while dividing model construction, criticism and decisions according to capability and authority. A changed requirement calls for recovering the affected assumptions and revising their connected work. The person may need to learn a missing judgement; the agent may need better access to evidence or a changed execution procedure. A useful result can then expose a further attainable problem or a better method. [The connected development account](#libraryreference91---open-ended-development-through-connected-contributions) explains that continuation and the distinctions between assistance, learning, cultural renewal and development. A publication supplies the canon; actual use, retention and renewal establish the culture in practice.

## Library.Reference:2 - What the names refer to

The same word can name a field, a framework about it or a file. Keep the subject of the claim recoverable before relying on its name.

**FPF Library** names this publication collection; **FPF Core** names its transdisciplinary framework. The Core document retains `FPF` as its DocID, so an address such as `FPF C.39` identifies a Core pattern. Cite **FPF Library** for the collection; name the particular framework and pattern when the claim relies on them.

| Object | What it contributes to this architecture | What a change affects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domain | A subject area used to frame a question or organize material. | A revised boundary changes what the designation covers; it does not by itself constitute a discipline. |
| Discipline | A whole of knowledge and reusable practice, with actual constituent relations and a way those contributions constrain and revise one another. | Changes to knowledge, Methods or institutions can preserve the field when its essential connections and continuity conditions remain. |
| Principle framework | An account of recurring situations, problems, forces, Methods, cases, consequences and their relations, expressed as a pattern language. | A revised account can improve the description or change its field promise. |
| Framework edition | An identified body of framework claims about its subject under its reference scheme. In FPF terms, an episteme. | A change in the claims or effective reference scheme identifies another episteme; its continuity and compatibility with earlier editions require their own grounds. |
| DPF Suite | A larger domain framework describing a composite discipline through constituent DPFs and any justified whole-level patterns. | A constituent or connection changes the described whole only under the relevant discipline conditions; editorial inclusion alone supplies no parthood. |
| Product series | A continuing collection of editions under declared admission and reidentification rules. | Admission, retirement and continuity concern the publishing series, separately from the described discipline. |
| Publication carrier and access route | A file, site, rendering or service through which a reader obtains the content. | Availability, layout or retrieval can change while the described Method remains the same. |

These distinctions are developed in [FPF C.20:4.6](FPF-Spec.md#c2046---keep-neighboring-objects-with-their-subject-patterns), [FPF E.4:4.1](FPF-Spec.md#e441---place-support-units-and-adjacent-products-deliberately) and [FPF E.4:4.2](FPF-Spec.md#e442---describe-a-composite-discipline-with-a-dpf-suite). They prevent a practical error: moving a file, giving a publication a name or admitting an edition cannot supply a missing methodological contribution.

A pattern is also distinct from the Method it explains. The text may help a reader perform, criticize or adapt a way of working. The occurrence of that work and its actual result need their own basis. [FPF A.3.1](FPF-Spec.md#a31---umethod-reusable-way-of-doing-with-explicit-applicability) and [FPF A.3.2](FPF-Spec.md#a32---umethoddescription-description-episteme-for-a-way-of-doing) retain that distinction; [FPF A.15.1](FPF-Spec.md#a151---uwork-dated-performed-work-occurrence) supplies the account of performed Work.

## Library.Reference:3 - Why general Methods belong in Core

[FPF Core](FPF-Spec.md) contains both common conceptual distinctions and developed transdisciplinary Methods. Its Kernel is the subset that defines universal meta-concepts and the rules for their meanings and relations. Core also explains how to investigate a question, construct and combine Methods, compare arrangements, qualify claims, make decisions and continue development. The Kernel is not the whole of Core.

The placement question turns on what an operation requires. Constructing a Method from interacting contributions, comparing its performance and provision, or deciding which questions a checklist must answer can apply across fields without importing one field's substantive mechanism. These general constructions belong with their shared conditions in Core. Repeated domain copies add a duty to keep the same general construction consistent.

For example, [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](FPF-Spec.md#c32mwapp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-provision-arrangements) constructs and compares complete performance and provision arrangements. [FPF A.15.8.MS](FPF-Spec.md#a158ms---configure-methodological-support-for-work) configures the methodological support that actual work needs. [FPF C.41 and its connected patterns](FPF-Spec.md#c41---select-checklist-questions-for-a-work-result) distinguish selecting checklist questions, making their reusable expression, fitting an occasion, obtaining grounded answers, reconciling shared uses and qualifying the checking means. These remain several Methods with different results, rather than one compulsory checklist process. [FPF E.8.ML](FPF-Spec.md#e8ml---construct-a-pattern-language-for-connected-methods) explains the construction of a pattern language for connected Methods.

Wide applicability alone does not justify Core placement. Mathematical Modeling constructs correspondences, transformations and mathematical consequences under mathematical premises. Research Method Practice reconstructs practice and investigates competing accounts under inquiry-specific evidence conditions. Organization Change Engineering constructs contribution, information, assignment and authority arrangements in an organization whose work continues. The common idea of improving a method does not teach those specialized operations.

The architecture therefore keeps a general construction once and gives its specialized realization an explicit supplier. The test is whether the full operation and its conditions survive a change of subject, and what domain knowledge still has to be supplied. An abstract object, a broad title or use by many professions cannot settle that test. [FPF E.4.PFAD](FPF-Spec.md#e4pfad---principle-framework-architecture-decision) compares the actual contributions and their organization; [FPF E.4.CM](FPF-Spec.md#e4cm---develop-connected-methods-as-framework-contributions) distinguishes an already explained connection from a missing reusable Method.

## Library.Reference:4 - Why these Suites form useful wholes

A Suite describes a field whose knowledge and reusable practices form a larger whole. Its constituent languages remain usable independently, but the Suite also explains the consequential connections: what one contribution constrains, what result another consumes, how failure returns to an affected premise and which substitutions preserve the whole. The four Suites below cover selected portions of their fields, not every practice associated with their broad names. Their subjects and knowledge-practice connections, rather than the current number of files, establish the boundaries.

### Library.Reference:4.1 - Engineering connects knowledge to functioning systems

The [Engineering Suite](DPFs/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/) is organized around designing, realizing, changing and sustaining functioning systems. This edition directly assembles Systems Engineering, Maintenance Engineering and Management, Knowledge-Corpus Access Engineering, Semantic Integration Engineering and Control Architecture Design. Management describes the organizational specialization, including OCE, and Human Capability Engineering describes the human specialization in §4.4. Their separate Suite editions retain their engineering subjects while making the corresponding specialized knowledge and practices available in full.

Direct constituents and specialized contributions can constrain the same realization. An attainable operator capability can change automation. A maintenance condition can change a design. A delayed observation or actuator limit can change the feasible control arrangement and reopen a system design choice; [CAD](DPFs/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/CONTROL-ARCHITECTURE-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) connects those conditions. The meanings carried across an information interface must remain usable in the receiving operation. Organizational authority and protected practice time can determine whether a new way of working becomes possible while existing obligations continue. Qualification and later use can reopen the knowledge or design on which those arrangements depended.

Engineering includes discovering and reformulating the problem, not only executing a settled specification. A particular task can finish with an adequate analysis, design or justified no-change recommendation; a claim that a system actually changed requires that result. Similarly, HCD's engineering contribution concerns constructed and qualified capability development. The fact that all thinking and learning have physical bearers does not make every intellectual or learning activity engineering.

The [Engineering Reference](DPFs/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/ENGINEERING-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md) explains selected combinations and the external contributions they need. An asset valuation, a research finding or a management commitment can be indispensable without bringing its whole discipline into Engineering.

### Library.Reference:4.2 - Management connects direction to jointly supportable commitments

The [Management Suite](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/) concerns directing and sustaining joint activity through worthwhile direction, legitimate and feasible commitments, provision, performance and warranted reconsideration. Its thirteen constituent languages are Strategy, Operations Management, Organization Administration, Corporate Governance, Management Accounting, Corporate Finance, Marketing, Customization and Productization Management, Engineering Asset Management, Leadership, Community Management, Organization Change Engineering and Human Resource Management.

A customer promise constrains operating work. Capacity and existing obligations constrain the promise. Resource and monetary accounts connect that work to funding choices. Authority determines which proposed act can become effective. Participation conditions determine which contributions can actually be relied on. A locally sound answer can therefore fail in the whole: the budget, schedule and promise may depend on the same unavailable resource or on an agreement that has not been made.

[Customization and Productization Management (CUST)](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/CUSTOMIZATION-AND-PRODUCTIZATION-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) connects decisions about client differences to the provision and continuing obligations they require. Practitioners may use a sufficient existing offering, retain a supported local answer, construct or change a repeatable offering, or decline the request. CUST.1 supplies the complete connected Method; CUST.2–CUST.8 can also be applied directly. A common technical component does not by itself establish a repeatable offer: permitted use, ordinary execution, support and existing client obligations can require a different construction. These management questions receive full engineering, implementation and accounting contributions without duplicating their supplying frameworks.


[Human Resource Management (HRM)](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-RESOURCE-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) connects actual work and participation terms through justified assessment and selection, reward, employee voice, protection and work transitions. Adequate output can coexist with conditions requiring preventive change; a personnel recommendation still needs its effective act and actual provision. HRM obtains qualified professional results for those decisions while Human Capability Development, Community Management, OCE, OPS and ADM retain their own contributions. The relation explains its Management membership without putting all people, culture or development work inside HR.

The composite contribution is a jointly supportable management arrangement, with a return from actual performance to the premise that needs reconsideration. The same construction accommodates a business, public service, temporary collaboration or voluntary community. Corporate Finance and Corporate Governance retain their corporate conditions; membership of the Suite does not impose those conditions on every undertaking.

For an organization as the target system, management specializes the construction and sustained functioning of organized joint work: authority, interests, commitments and resources have their own substantive conditions. A changed allocation or operating commitment can change the organization's state without redesigning its architecture. Management is therefore not separated from engineering by an alleged absence of system change. It also receives independently useful results from other disciplines. A voluntary population or network is not automatically one organization or capability holder; the relevant whole and authority must be established for that use.

Organization Change Engineering belongs directly to Management: organizational constructions constrain feasible commitments, and changed commitments can require another arrangement. Maintenance Engineering and Management belongs directly to Engineering: failure and condition knowledge, maintenance policy, provision and intervention connect to sustained functioning; later functioning can reopen them. Management practitioners can apply the full maintenance Methods themselves or obtain their results. This preserves the technical and management connections inside MNT without making its complete field an additional Management constituent. Each complete language has one home in its respective Suite.

### Library.Reference:4.3 - Foundational Thinking connects formal constructions and their interpretations

The [Foundational Thinking Suite](DPFs/Foundational%20Thinking%20DPF%20Suite/) connects Mathematical Thinking, Mathematical Modeling, Physical Thinking, Computational Thinking and Notational Engineering. These Methods can produce new objects, explanations, correspondences, algorithms and languages, as well as use existing ones. A subject difficulty can call for a new formal construction; a formal construction can open a new subject question.

A physical account constrains admissible model relations. Mathematical construction derives consequences. An algorithm must obtain the required answer within its resource and interaction conditions. Modelling connects that answer back to the subject question; notation keeps the distinctions operable and their changes recoverable. The whole makes a consequence jointly interpretable and revisable while retaining its separate mathematical, physical, computational and expressive grounds.

An observational mismatch can reopen an idealization; computing the same model more accurately does not repair that premise. A numerical error can require an algorithmic correction without invalidating the physical account. These different returns are part of the field's construction. The [Foundational Thinking Reference](DPFs/Foundational%20Thinking%20DPF%20Suite/FOUNDATIONAL-THINKING-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md) explains such connections.

The name does not designate all thinking or a universal learning sequence. A pure mathematical result can be complete without a physical application. Explanation Design, Research Method Practice and Narrativization have other subjects and remain independent suppliers. Their use of mathematics or notation does not absorb their full fields into this Suite.

### Library.Reference:4.4 - Human Capability Engineering changes the human holder's capabilities

The [Human Capability Engineering Suite](DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/) concerns forming, changing, restoring and maintaining what a human holder can do. Human designates the target of the change, not necessarily its performer. A human teacher, an organization or suitable AI assistance may contribute; the resulting capability claim still concerns the person. This Suite connects the full Human Capability Development and Somatic Movement Modeling languages.

HCD connects later Work, the person's starting evidence, programme alternatives, practice, provision and qualification. SOM supplies the bodily distinctions, regulation and coordination needed for its movement-learning branch. Their results constrain one another: a programme cannot supply a missing coordination by listing it, and a local movement exercise does not establish performance of the whole task. A dancer may have adequate strength, a stationary axis and a remembered figure while lacking coordination during rotation. The two languages connect the missing bodily contribution to whole-task practice, variation, transfer and retention, rather than treating separate successful exercises as a complete result.

This is a capability-engineering synthesis with limited present coverage. It does not claim all medicine, psychology, personality change or personal management, and it does not imply that these questions are reducible to teaching. When physiological, clinical, motivational or other mechanisms are necessary, their substantive Methods and qualified provision must be obtained. The current two DPFs are the available part of the field, not a reason to close its boundary around the files already written.

The qualification Capability distinguishes this target from the human–system interaction studied in ergonomics. A bounded capability change also differs from open-ended development. The [Human Capability Engineering Reference (HCE)](DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-ENGINEERING-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md) explains the field, alternatives, available constructions and limits. An assisted answer can satisfy an immediate need without changing the person; acquiring a capability can finish while a wider open-ended development undertaking continues.

## Library.Reference:6 - Several relations, not one hierarchy

The Library uses several structures at once. Their relations answer different questions.

| Relation | Architectural consequence | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discipline parthood | A constituent contributes to the continuing practice-and-knowledge assembly described by a Suite. | Maintenance knowledge and intervention practice participate in Engineering's account of sustained functioning. |
| Method composition | Constituent Methods together obtain the result of a larger Method under their joint conditions. | A way of answering a decision question can combine Methods for obtaining observations, interpreting them and comparing alternatives. |
| Specialization | An inherited construction is developed under additional subject conditions. | Somatic Movement Modeling gives bodily sensing and coordination constructions within human capability development. |
| Supplying use | One Method's result is consumed by another operation under stated conditions. | An engineering choice receives a financial constraint without absorbing Corporate Finance. |
| Framework dependency | A framework relies on claims or Methods in an identified supplying edition. | A domain language uses Core's distinction between capability, assignment and performed Work. |
| Publication assembly | Selected complete descriptions are included with ways to find them and links back to their sources. | OCE has its full home in the Management Suite and MNT in the Engineering Suite; uses from elsewhere return to those same complete descriptions. |

Discipline parthood is established for the field, Method parthood for the way of working, and Work containment for performed activity. A project's participants and timetable do not establish any of these other relations. [FPF C.32.MWA](FPF-Spec.md#c32mwa---synthesize-an-architecture-account-of-methods-and-their-use) helps recover the Method, Work, capability and provision structures when they do not correspond one-for-one.

The directory tree supplies an unambiguous home for full descriptions. It does not turn all semantic relations into a tree. A claim of multiple disciplinary parthood needs grounds for each assembly; repeated usefulness is insufficient. Nor does a graph with several parents automatically form a mathematical lattice. A formal model requires its selected elements, relation and mathematical properties, as [FPF E.11.PFP:4.7](FPF-Spec.md#e11pfp47---explain-the-methods-at-each-selected-scale) explains.

OCE's organizational construction participates in the Management whole, a specialization of engineering. A management undertaking can also use MNT's full policy or programme Method while maintenance remains a constituent of the broader Engineering edition. In that use the manager can perform the Method: the supplying discipline need not be represented by a different person. Corporate Finance can similarly supply a constraint to an engineering project without that use establishing its whole field as an Engineering constituent. The subject and the relation of the complete field to the whole decide composition; a useful connection alone does not.

The transformed object also differs from the performer and recipient. Replacing a teacher with an AI assistant can leave the human-learning target unchanged. Replacing the learner with a robot changes the target and can require different learning and control Methods. Methods with the same input and output kinds can require different operations for those targets. Replacing an instrument can change motor demands and attainable musical material; changing listeners or cultural selection can change what is retained for other reasons. The complete MDPE.20 account preserves these distinct mechanisms. The general constructions remain in Core; the bodies that depend on a particular target and mechanism remain with their substantive fields.

### Library.Reference:6.1 - Connect structures through the result they must obtain

A change can leave the organization chart intact while making its service unavailable. A controller can retain the same components while a faster command sequence defeats its limits. Use the connected account here when a proposed change crosses structures whose parts or relations do not correspond one-for-one. If one understood relation and its subject Method already settle the question, use that answer directly.

Begin with what must become possible, for whom and under which conditions. Work backward to the contributions that could obtain it, then forward through their connections to the receiving result. The backward inquiry identifies necessary contributions; the forward inquiry asks whether they can work together. A list of available parts is insufficient when their inputs disagree, their results arrive too late or their combined demand exceeds a shared resource. A failed connection can require another constituent, another interface or provider, or a revised result agreed with its owner.

Several accounts can contribute to this reasoning. A **system composition** identifies constituents of a particular whole and the interactions through which it functions. A **functional account** groups contributions by what they do and relates the results they supply. A **Method composition** connects reusable ways of working under the whole Method's applicability and construction conditions. An account of **Work** concerns particular performances: what they constitute, what precedes or overlaps what, and what occupies the same means. The descriptions of those objects have their own structure. Their paragraphs, boxes and rows can be aligned without the described parts being identical.

Connect the accounts through the relation that carries the consequence. A tracking calculation can be allocated to a processor; the processor can be a constituent of a controller; an execution consumes processor time; the calculated command is used to act on a plant. Replacing the processor can preserve the calculation while invalidating its timing premise. Changing the meaning or permitted rate of commands can instead require a different calculation on the same processor. The needed change follows the premise and its dependents.

Composition makes a stronger claim than use or dependence. A constituent Method contributes to a reusable whole under its construction rule. Another Method can consume its result without becoming that whole's constituent. Likewise, a supplier can make delivery possible without being part of the receiving organization. Developing a Method changes a way of working; performing that development does not make it a higher constituent level of every Method it changes. Provision, development and delivery can nevertheless compete for the same person's time. Preserve that shared demand even when their Method boundaries remain separate.

Control adds another connection: information must support a choice that can influence the relevant action. In organizational work, identify who may change the commitment and how that choice reaches capable performers and available means. A reporting line, a feedback arrow and a relation of organizational parthood can coexist, but each supports a different inference. [FPF C.43](FPF-Spec.md#c43---connect-observation-choice-and-action-in-control) explains the observation–choice–action connection, including its organizational use.

An abstraction keeps the distinctions needed for a selected answer and omits others. A two-box controller drawing may explain which contribution supplies a command while being insufficient to decide whether that command can be tracked. A more detailed account can recover that omitted condition without introducing another system constituent. Model scope concerns what is included; detail concerns the distinctions retained within it. A temporal scale, update period or planning horizon can change the answer again: a slower update changes a timing condition, while a longer planning horizon changes the interval considered. Neither change by itself grants authority or establishes a containing system.

Bring the findings back to the whole. Test a replacement against all receiving uses it can affect; retain independent uses whose premises still hold. If the old whole cannot carry the required behavior or composition claim, reconsider its boundary and identity rather than preserving it for a neat hierarchy. [FPF B.1.5.RS](FPF-Spec.md#b15rs---evaluate-a-constituent-method-replacement-in-its-encompassing-uses) supplies the constituent-replacement construction. [FPF C.30.ASV:4.5a](FPF-Spec.md#c30asv45a---select-structures-for-a-method-and-its-uses) helps choose the Method structures that can change the decision; [FPF C.32.MWA](FPF-Spec.md#c32mwa---synthesize-an-architecture-account-of-methods-and-their-use) develops their connected architecture account, alternatives and moved burdens. Where the unresolved question is how the Methods will be performed and supported together, [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](FPF-Spec.md#c32mwapp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-provision-arrangements) constructs and compares those arrangements.

These are different uses of one constructive return, not a universal calculation. A control design needs its error dynamics and physical limits; a service arrangement needs usable completion, qualified capacity and commitments. The two cases below show how their subject Methods determine different changes from the same whole-to-contributions reasoning. A construction explains what would hold under its premises. Actual systemhood, Method composition, performed Work, authority or disciplinary membership needs the corresponding subject grounds; the explanation alone supplies none of those claims. In particular, a proposed larger discipline returns to [FPF C.20](FPF-Spec.md#c20---composition-of-udiscipline-discipline-cal) for its knowledge–practice couplings and continuing whole.

### Library.Reference:6.2 - Interpret levels, layers and scales through their relations

A level is relative to an identified relation and subject. Nested system constituents can support system-composition levels; constituent Methods can support Method-composition levels. Classification, refinement, abstraction, authority and time answer other questions even when a source also calls their positions levels. The depth of a composition depends on the selected whole. A taxonomic child is a narrower kind, whereas a constituent supplies a contribution to a whole.

An account of functional layers groups contributions by what they do and explains how their results connect. Planning and tracking can be functional layers in a control design. Delivery, support and development can be functional groupings in an operating arrangement. Several modules or people can contribute to one layer, and one module or person can contribute to several layers. Use a layered account when this grouping helps explain the required result; it does not require a matching organization of physical components or teams.

This gives a qualified answer to “are management levels functional layers?” A source can be grouping functions, composing organizational units, allocating decision rights or comparing horizons. Delivery, support and development performed by one team can be described functionally. If that team is a constituent of a firm under the applicable system-composition conditions, its system relation remains available as well. A manager's authority and planning horizon require their own account. The word *management* chooses none of these relations in advance.

“Method level” similarly needs its local meaning. It can concern a constituent, a narrower family, a refinement or the order in which an explanation introduces topics. For refinement, first determine whether the variation remains within one Method's identity. When two Methods are identified, [FPF A.3.1:4.8](FPF-Spec.md#a3148---method-relations-composition-and-work-enactment) asks which semantics are retained, what is narrowed or strengthened and what action or result changes. More detailed description alone does not settle this comparison. Teaching one topic before another can make an explanation usable without making the earlier topic a constituent or execution stage of every later Method.

Use a qualified expression when it changes the reader's inference. The following English and Russian phrases illustrate that practice; they are explanatory choices, not new kinds or a rule for replacing source vocabulary.

### Library.Reference:6.3 - A faster command can defeat an unchanged controller

Consider a constructed sampled controller with plant `x[k+1] = x[k] + u[k]` and tracking law `u[k] = (r[j] - x[k])/2`. The planner's command `r[j]` is held for `N` fast steps. The state is fully observed; this model has no disturbance, computation delay or unmodeled saturation. Commands satisfy `|r[j]| ≤ 8.9`; consecutive command changes have magnitude at most `δ`. Required sampled limits are `|x[k]| ≤ 10` and `|u[k]| ≤ 0.6`.

Planning and tracking are two functional contributions that could run on one processor. A separate system composition could include a controller, plant and their constituents. The functional grouping alone does not establish two physical Systems or two system-composition levels. The current question is whether the command and tracking contributions are compatible under the sampled limits.

Let `e[j] = x[jN] - r[j]` immediately after a command is selected. During the held-command interval the error halves at each fast step. At the next command change,

```text
e[j+1] = 2^(-N) e[j] - (r[j+1] - r[j]).
```

If the initial error satisfies `|e[0]| ≤ E`, the interval `|e| ≤ E` at command changes is invariant for `E = δ/(1 - 2^(-N))`: the next error is at most `2^(-N) E + δ = E`. Within each interval the error decreases. Hence `|u| ≤ E/2`, and `|x| ≤ |r| + E` is a sufficient state bound. This is the consequential join: the planner's jump and update conditions determine the tracking demand.

With `N = 4` and `δ = 1`, `E = 16/15`. The input magnitude is at most `8/15 < 0.6`, and the sufficient state bound is `8.9 + 16/15 = 299/30 < 10`. Under the stated initial and model conditions, every command sequence within this interface respects the sampled limits. Holding the current command is allowed by those constraints; the calculation does not establish attainment of every further planner objective.

Now require commands every two fast steps while retaining permitted unit jumps. The plant, processor and two functional contributions are unchanged. The error bound becomes `E = 4/3`, so the earlier sufficient input argument fails. There is also an actual violating trajectory: take `r[0] = 0` and `x[0] = 16/15`, admitted by the earlier design. After two steps, `x[2] = 4/15`. The permitted command `r[1] = -1` then gives `u[2] = -19/30`, whose magnitude exceeds `0.6`. The failure is in the changed temporal and command interface, not in the number of component boxes.

One repair keeps `N = 2` and the command-magnitude bound `8.9`, but reduces the jump bound to `δ = 0.9`. Require initial conditions `|e[0]| ≤ 1.2` and `|x[0]| ≤ 10`. The error envelope is invariant because `1.2/4 + 0.9 = 1.2`, so `|u| ≤ 0.6`. Check the state separately: `x[k+1] = (x[k] + r[j])/2` is a convex combination. From `|x[k]| ≤ 10` and `|r[j]| ≤ 8.9`, it follows that `|x[k+1]| ≤ 9.45 ≤ 10`. Thus the state set is invariant as well.

The coarser bound `|r| + E` would have suggested reducing the command magnitude to `8.8`. The separate state invariant shows that this extra restriction is unnecessary in the stipulated model. The revised interface still sacrifices the original unit-jump demand, and a transition from an operating controller must establish both new initial conditions. Accept this candidate only if the intended task permits that smaller jump. Retaining `N = 4` instead sacrifices the faster response. A timed command segment, another tracking law or stronger actuation changes other premises and requires another derivation.

[CAD.1–CAD.4 in Control Architecture Design](DPFs/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/CONTROL-ARCHITECTURE-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) develop the corresponding architecture, interface, feasibility and co-design questions. This sampled calculation leaves real-plant adequacy, continuous-time constraints, shared-processor scheduling and the planner's further objectives to their required constructions. Keeping only two boxes loses the recurrence and cannot decide this change; retaining the recurrence, command conditions and initial state is enough for the demonstrated sampled-limit comparison. Processor detail becomes relevant if computation or delay changes that premise.

### Library.Reference:6.4 - The same organization can require different performance and provision

Consider a prospective diagnostic service with two accepted reports due by hour six of an eight-hour day. Each report needs two hours of analyst preparation followed by one hour of an authorized expert's judgement and acceptance. The analyst has eight available hours. The expert has four allocable hours with no further fixed calendar restriction in this constructed case. The protocol in use is qualified, and the expert is authorized to accept both reports.

The expert must also perform one hour of support that keeps the service usable and one hour of development of the reusable diagnostic protocol. A feasible schedule gives the analyst preparation at hours `0–2` and `2–4`, the expert support at `0–1`, protocol development at `1–2`, and reviews at `2–3` and `4–5`. Both reports arrive before hour six; expert occupancy totals four hours.

Delivery, support and development are different functional contributions made by the same person. Their three rows establish no three organizational units or management ranks. The reusable report Method also differs from the two planned performances and the separate protocol-development work. Developing a protocol changes a Method used in delivery; that development need not be a constituent of each report-producing Method. Yet all three kinds of contribution draw on the same expert capacity. Their distinct boundaries must remain connected in the service account.

Change the case class: additional evidence now makes each qualified review take `1.5` expert-hours. Keep the reporting promise, preparation and authority unchanged. Expert demand becomes `3 + 1 + 1 = 5` hours against four available. A total of analyst and expert labour would hide the shortage: spare analyst time cannot supply the qualified expert judgement. An unchanged organization chart therefore carries an unavailable promise.

One bounded alternative defers the hour of protocol development. This works only if the current protocol remains qualified and the development commitment permits a one-day delay. Keep analyst preparation at `0–2` and `2–4`; schedule support at `0–1` and reviews at `2–3.5` and `4–5.5`. Both reports meet the deadline and expert occupancy is four hours. Today's service is obtained by moving development into another period. This is not a sustainable recurring arrangement by arithmetic alone. If the protocol must change before today's reports can be accepted, deferral fails at that dependency despite the hours fitting.

## Library.Reference:8 - A worked connection across the Library

The following constructed case illustrates how these contributions fit together.

Three laboratories want a more dependable diagnostic measurement practice. An existing calculation works for familiar specimens but gives unexplained disagreements for a new class. The groups also want practitioners to handle further unfamiliar cases. They are considering better source access, an external specialist service, a shared support office and local provision coordinated by their leads.

### Library.Reference:8.1 - Different contributions to one useful result

The intended results already reveal several subjects. A functioning measurement arrangement is an engineering result. An explanation of the discrepancy is a knowledge result. A practitioner's ability to recognize and investigate a new case is a capability result. A feasible agreement about contributions is a management result. None can be inferred merely from completion of another.

[Decision Design (PSDA)](DPFs/DECISION-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) helps construct the question and compare alternatives without assuming at the outset that the answer is an office or a course. [Research Method Practice (RMP)](DPFs/RESEARCH-METHOD-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) supplies inquiry when existing knowledge cannot explain the disagreement. If the issue is a model premise, [Foundational Thinking](DPFs/Foundational%20Thinking%20DPF%20Suite/) connects physical explanation, model correspondence and calculation. A faster or more accurate computation of the same inadequate model does not resolve that premise.

The resulting explanation changes what must be obtained. If a usable method already exists and the only defect is access, [Implementation Facilitation (IS)](DPFs/IMPLEMENTATION-FACILITATION-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) can supply the missing help. If the desired result is each practitioner's independent judgement, external answers alone are insufficient; [Human Capability Development (HCD)](DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) supplies the relevant learning and capability qualification. When organizational contributions or authority must change, [Organization Change Engineering (OCE)](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) constructs that realization while ongoing obligations remain in view.

The office alternatives concern how these transformations will be performed and supported. [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](FPF-Spec.md#c32mwapp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-provision-arrangements) compares their complete performance and provision. A common office for several masteries is a serious alternative when the same contribution can serve them without erasing their different criteria, and the gain from sharing exceeds the extra coordination and interpretation burden. Specialized development support has a case when it supplies methodological inquiry that each workplace lacks while receiving subject expertise from the laboratories. Local leadership, distributed specialists or external provision can be sufficient when those arrangements supply the whole needed contribution under attainable conditions.

[STR.16](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str16---construct-a-feasible-portfolio-with-shared-or-branch-specific-support) compares shared and branch-specific support while preserving each branch's purpose. [LDR.2](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/LEADERSHIP-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ldr2---agree-feasible-joint-contributions) obtains responses to a feasible joint proposal. Where independent groups require a common mandate, [CB.13](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/COMMUNITY-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#cb13---establish-a-workable-mandate-for-shared-decisions) supplies that contribution; the participants retain powers they have not delegated.

Suppose one qualified specialist has eight available hours in the relevant week and each of two distinct activities requires five hours of that specialist's time, including preparation and return. Each activity is individually feasible; the pair needs ten hours. Shared administration does not supply the missing two hours. Different timing, a qualified substitute or a smaller promise can make a different arrangement feasible. The choice depends on which change preserves the required result, not on the number of offices or participants. This is a condition on the whole combination, not a defect in either local Method.

### Library.Reference:8.2 - What a changed condition sends back

If the new specimen class defeats an assumption in the model, the return is to that assumption and its dependent conclusions. If the model remains adequate but the specialist withdraws, the return is to provision and commitments. If the service delivers good answers but practitioners still cannot investigate unfamiliar cases, the missing result is their capability; repeating the service's success does not answer that question. If the scientific result is adequate but its explanation cannot be used at the workplace, [Explanation Design (EXD)](DPFs/EXPLANATION-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) supplies the recipient-relative construction.

These branches show why the Library retains different languages. Research retains its independently useful knowledge result and its own field boundary when this engineering work uses it. The existing Methods preserve the changed premise, the receiving action and the relevant return.

Once the practice works adequately, one branch can finish. The new understanding may reveal a harder but attainable problem, such as recognizing an unfamiliar failure before it affects a measurement. [FPF C.40.NP](FPF-Spec.md#c40np---continue-from-a-result-to-new-attainable-problems) develops that continuation. If several independent practitioners need to reconstruct and adapt the method, [FPF C.36.RP](FPF-Spec.md#c36rp---sustain-and-renew-shared-ways-of-working) supplies cultural retention and renewal, with [Community Management (CB)](DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/COMMUNITY-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) where participation or common work needs organizing. Neither a permanent office nor an endlessly open project is required by that continuation.

The same architecture can serve a question that has no laboratory or system-realization goal. A mathematical investigation may finish with a warranted construction; a narrative may finish with a source-faithful account suited to its purpose.

## Library.Reference:9 - Open-ended development belongs across these fields

Open-ended development expands knowledge, understanding and reproducible ways of obtaining results, then uses that changed starting position to open further worthwhile and attainable problems. The novelty can be new to a person, organization or community without being new to humanity. An adequate knowledge contribution can matter before all prospective applications have been realized.

The general connection is developed in [FPF C.40.OD](FPF-Spec.md#c40od---construct-and-continue-open-ended-development), [FPF C.40.JD](FPF-Spec.md#c40jd---develop-a-way-its-holder-and-its-provision-together) and [FPF C.40.NP](FPF-Spec.md#c40np---continue-from-a-result-to-new-attainable-problems). They connect a consequential difficulty, problematisation, a useful direction, construction of a way of working, preparation of a capable performer and the means it needs, application and the next possibility. They keep the branch that supplies an immediate result distinct from the branch that develops a performer's capability and the branch that develops the Method itself.

That construction is transdisciplinary. It can concern a person, organization, AI system or distributed cultural carriers while requiring different mechanisms in each case. HCD explains human practice, feedback and transfer. OCE explains organizational realization and authority. RMP explains research. Community Management explains participation where a common undertaking exists. An executable learning system also needs its own challenge representations, learning and transfer mechanisms; the general development account is not their implementation.

The general construction remains incomplete for a concrete use until its needed subject mechanisms are supplied. Keeping those mechanisms in their domain languages preserves their distinct premises, evidence and revision. Putting the general connection inside one domain framework would make a common Method depend on that field. Keeping only scattered citations would leave the reader to invent how a sufficient result opens the next problem. The selected architecture develops the common connection in Core and retains its subject-specific suppliers.

Repeated delivery, a new version or a higher current score alone does not establish this continuation. The useful claim concerns the growth actually obtained, the problems it enables and the conditions supporting further development. A bounded service or project can end while its result supports that wider practice.

### Library.Reference:9.1 - Open-ended development through connected contributions

Development can begin with a faint difficulty in ordinary work, a possibility that changes what is worth attempting, or a newly noticed limit after success. The problem, the way forward and the means of development can all need construction. The Methods connected by this account help people conduct that work, join its necessary contributions and continue from the position actually reached. It also retains independently useful opportunity inquiry and advice: a bounded useful result can finish such an undertaking without pretending that development itself is complete.

The developed subject, the provider of help and the chooser of a commitment need not be the same. Self-development is the branch in which the subject itself makes the relevant contribution to conducting its own development. An externally supplied improvement does not establish that contribution. A technical or non-agent subject can develop under another performer's work without being assigned agency by analogy.

#### Library.Reference:9.1.1 - One connected practice and independently useful contributions

The connected question is how a worthwhile change becomes attainable and actual, and how the changed position becomes a basis for further development. C.40.OD supplies the construction linking current work, problematisation, direction and Method construction, performer preparation, provision, consequential work and further inquiry. STR.16 handles a different recurring difficulty: independently worthwhile undertakings whose shared means and support must fit together. The opportunity and advice branches below supply useful results within that practice and remain usable independently.

**Connected formulation.** Notice what matters in the current situation; make the unresolved question worth answering; construct a reachable change with its means; qualify the contributions together; obtain the real commitment and provision; perform the work and distinguish the result from what was learned; use the changed position to find the next worthwhile attainable problem; improve the way development is done when that change is worth its cost. Enter at an adequate existing result, preserve independent work, and return to the particular missing contribution when a connection fails.

This formulation helps retain the joins explained by the patterns. Enter through the contribution whose inputs are available in the present work. A local performance can skip unnecessary learning because a qualified performer exists. A known adequate Method can avoid fresh research. A genuine inquiry can continue for its own scientific result without waiting for a sale. A branch can end, rest or consolidate while the subject retains a way to notice and pursue its next development. Endless activity and ever-larger obligations are not the meaning of open continuation.

The practical result of one complete use is a meaningful bounded start, actual work through qualified Methods, an intelligible account of the changed contribution and a reasoned next development question. Where a company or provider needs strategic descriptions, programmes, work arrangements and learning material, those are expressions of the relevant construction and separate instructional work; their existence is not evidence of the capability they describe.

#### Library.Reference:9.1.12 - Qualify the combination as well as its ingredients

Joining results requires three judgements: each result is adequately qualified; it fits the receiving subject, configuration, use and horizon; and the proposed combination meets its shared conditions. Identify the next responsible receiver for a missing result. In C.40.JF's constructed eight-week case, directions A and B each require the same specialist for two hours every week; only two hours are available after existing obligations. Each direction fits alone. Together they need four hours and fail the joint condition under the no-overtime and urgent-cover protections. Doing A and then B takes sixteen weeks, outside the eight-week horizon. Shared support would be another configuration whose combined demand needs qualification. #### Library.Reference:9.1.13 - Architectural rationale

The connected repertoire supplies three scales of result without treating them as interchangeable. A worthwhile opportunity or responsible advice can be enough for a bounded inquiry. A development undertaking needs the further joins by which necessary contributions become usable together, work occurs and a new position is obtained. A provider or organization with several independent ends needs a portfolio whose common demand and support can be chosen coherently. The shorter result remains useful without claiming the larger one.

The opportunity and advising Methods retain opportunity construction and advice because their results are reused in the larger practice and can be sought independently. C.40.OD supplies the connected development construction; its subject-specific learning, engineering, inquiry, cultural and organization work use their actual professional suppliers. STR.16 supplies the joining construction for independent purposes, shared support, resource competition and real commitments. Retaining a possibility in C.40.OP does not allocate it, and comparing a human programme in HCD.2 does not construct an organization's portfolio of research, products and development undertakings.

Using Decision Design alone remains a serious simpler alternative for a recommendation question with adequate inputs. HCD, OCE, Strategy or another professional Method can likewise finish their own question directly. A list of those suppliers is insufficient when the reader still has to invent how their outputs become one feasible development undertaking or how the first success opens a different worthwhile question. The connected Methods make that joining work explicit without absorbing the supplying disciplines.

The connected repertoire is broader than one composite Method. A framework may offer several independently reusable ways whose results constrain one another; its publication Parts are not Method constituents or a mandatory action order. A supplied domain result is a premise under qualified conditions, not the domain's entire Method.

The opportunity, advice and continuing-practice contributions are independently useful. This architectural reference connects the complete Core patterns and domain-framework publications so that a practitioner can discover and use their joins while retaining precise PatternIDs. A separate publication becomes preferable when a branch has an independently useful whole and the revised distribution serves its use, learning and change better. Reconsider the organization when changed uses or contributions make another arrangement more useful.

A useful narrower profile can inherit applicable guidance and explain its changed situation, contribution, conditions and evidence needs. More than one level of narrowing is possible, and a contribution can serve several profiles. State whether the actual relation is bounded-use projection, specialization, composition, reuse or result dependence.

## Library.Reference:10 - What justifies another pattern, DPF, Suite or LPF

The Library's boundaries follow useful constructions and their subjects. A new topic or an awkward folder location is a reason to inspect the architecture, not a sufficient reason for another framework.

[FPF E.4.DPF](FPF-Spec.md#e4dpf---domain-and-local-principle-frameworks-whether-and-how-to-author-and-publish) and [FPF E.4.PFAD](FPF-Spec.md#e4pfad---principle-framework-architecture-decision) compare the recurring problems, available full Methods, their connections and the independently useful result. That comparison can retain an adequate supplier, improve its explanation, add a missing Method, regroup a developed field or identify another field. Existing elementary operations do not prevent a new composition; a useful summary does not establish one.

| Proposed contribution | What gives it the corresponding boundary |
| --- | --- |
| A new or revised pattern | A recurring problem and reproducible construction with its own useful result, conditions and cases; an existing owner is preferable when the addition completes that owner's promised result. |
| A DPF | A coherent field account of its entities, relations and operations; how the practice obtains, tests and uses knowledge; and how its Methods are constructed, selected, connected and revised. Where the practice creates new units, representations or operations, the account explains that construction. Recurring problems and independently useful results justify the boundary. |
| A DPF Suite | A composite discipline established through C.20, constituent DPFs and developed whole connections; more patterns or more cited suppliers do not supply that construction. |
| An LPF | Guidance for a bounded local practice, such as an organization, project, tool arrangement or practitioner setting, using applicable Core and domain contributions under its actual local conditions. |
| An Architectural Reference | A needed public account of an existing whole, its architecture, alternatives, connections and returns. The underlying Methods remain developed at their substantive owners. |
| A pedagogical companion or tool | Learning support or an operational means of access, calculation or checking with its own intended use and limits. |

An LPF can select local responsibilities, tools and working arrangements without turning those choices into universal rules. A domain framework can receive an existing Method or split into better field descriptions; novelty of every operation is not the test of its identity. A proposed Suite must explain the larger field's actual knowledge-practice coupling and its continuity through permissible changes.

The consequential comparison is what each boundary changes in obtaining, understanding, adapting and maintaining a useful result. Shared content can reduce duplicate explanation while increasing reliance on access and compatible sources. Independent variants can preserve necessary differences at the cost of maintaining them. A new framework is warranted when its field and useful whole justify that burden, not merely when a name is available.

## Library.Reference:12 - The Library and its wider ecosystem

FPF Library is the project's publication collection. The wider FPF ecosystem also includes other authors' domain and local frameworks, teaching materials, tools, services and the people and organizations that use and maintain them. A common conceptual dependency does not put all of these products inside the Library or give them one publication or maintenance responsibility.

This distinction lets the public Methods remain complete without making every useful learning arrangement part of the Library. A pedagogical companion can sequence practice, give feedback or adapt material for an audience. Tooling can retrieve a passage, compute a result or perform a declared check. Those contributions need their own capabilities and evidence; the fact that they cite FPF Library publications does not establish them. The Library's Reference explains its architecture and connected language, while learning programmes and operational tools retain their own purposes.

Public architectural reasons belong where they help readers understand and adapt the language: why a boundary exists, which serious alternative it excludes, what a dependency supplies and which condition would reopen the choice. The resulting architecture offers common meanings and reusable general Methods, specialized constructive knowledge and explicit ways to connect them.
