# Development DPF Suite Reference

> Find the contribution that lets development continue, and the Methods for obtaining it.

**Reference edition, 12 October 2026.** The [Suite account](README.md) states the collection, membership and identity rules. Each answer returns to the full supplying publications and their conditions of use.

Use this Reference while arranging development of yourself, a collective, an organization or another system, providing help, or supporting continued practice among independent carriers. It supplies practical entries to complete Methods, not a fixed process or an obligatory office. Open a known sufficient pattern directly. The supplying pattern's entry, conditions, worked case and return still govern its use.

Addresses `Development.Reference:n` identify sections of this Reference, not patterns. Each section includes its subordinate headings through the next heading of the same or higher level. The final boundary is `Development.Reference:End`.

## Development.Reference:1 - What result are we trying to obtain?

A request for a development office can name a proposed arrangement before anyone has established its useful result. Recover that result first. Does a person need to perform some current work, become able to perform it, organize their own further development, or help others become able to continue? Does a provider need a viable offer, a company need a different capability, or a community need useful participation? These results can depend on each other without being the same.

[DOCA.1 and DOCA.1.CHR](DEVELOPMENT-AND-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#doca1---bound-the-development-opportunity-inquiry) help bound the inquiry and characterize the subject and intended change. [DOCA.7](DEVELOPMENT-AND-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#doca7---bound-the-development-direction-advising-engagement) bounds help when the adviser, recipient, sponsor and choice owner differ. Their result can be a better bounded inquiry or a qualified stop, not a compulsory programme.

Use [Human Capability Development](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Development%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) when a person must acquire the ability; use an adequate available service when supplied performance is sufficient. Neither route alone establishes self-development. Continue to the connected undertaking only when the receiving work needs that larger result.

## Development.Reference:2 - Where does development begin, and what happens after success?

Development need not start with a chosen practice or an explicit problem. Recurrent workarounds, a surprising success, an avoided task or a narrowing of attainable possibilities can justify inquiry. The development language's [DOCA.1–4](DEVELOPMENT-AND-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#doca1---bound-the-development-opportunity-inquiry) connects such inquiry to a worthwhile problem, possible directions and the support they need. An adequate established answer can end that inquiry; creating novelty is not a condition of useful work.

[DOCA.9](DEVELOPMENT-AND-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#doca9---construct-and-sustain-a-connected-development-undertaking) connects the proposed transition, its realization and continuation from the changed position. Its worked case shows why successful delivery of the current result is different from the subject becoming able to organize a further development problem. The next problem may be harder because earlier limitations have been removed, but escalation without a worthwhile attainable gain is not development discipline.

For an unknown way to obtain the result, follow [C.39](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c39---find-and-develop-a-way-to-obtain-a-result) and the Development framework's direct C.40 returns. For improvement of the means of development, follow its E.23.CDI return rather than treating every Method edit as development. A sufficient contribution ends the local inquiry; continuing development opens the next substantive question.

## Development.Reference:3 - How do the different transformations form one undertaking?

Ordinary work, inquiry, changed subject capability, construction of products and Methods, support provision and cultural continuation must supply one another where the intended result depends on them.

Use [DOCA.9:4](DEVELOPMENT-AND-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#doca94---solution). It develops the typical connections and their conditional returns, including an adequate external contribution that changes which work the undertaking must perform itself. The result is a proposed connected undertaking with identifiable receiving contributions and conditions, not an already operating organization. Its worked case provides the construction at a size that can be inspected and adapted.

An actual transformation-flow network claim uses the distinct flow and network rules in [E.18](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#e18---transformation-flow-structure) and [E.18.NET](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#e18net---network-of-transformation-flow-structures). Methods, performed work, receiving dependencies and shared resources remain different relations. If only one adequate contribution is needed, there is no obligation to model a whole network. If a connection cannot be constructed from the supplied Methods, return that missing contribution to its actual practice.

## Development.Reference:4 - Who should perform and support the Methods?

Compare complete arrangements, not office names. The same development work might be performed through ordinary managers and workplaces, a specialist development office, a common service for several masteries, a federation, an external provider or an extensive AI arrangement. Methods, capability, resources and powers must be connected without assuming that their structures coincide.

[ME.6](../Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/METHOD-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#me6---compare-method-architecture-alternatives-and-simultaneous-enactment-conflicts) constructs these alternatives from the supplied practice and receiving result. It distinguishes the subject's necessary own contribution from substitutable provision, exposes joint demand and authority, and compares shared and specialized support. Its examples include a company without a new office, voluntary external participants and qualified AI. Current [Organization Change Engineering](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) supplies the actual organizational design and realization where an organization is the governed subject.

A tunnel-building company may need technical performance, learning and continued development. That does not decide that one office should own all three. A specialist development provider can obtain technical expertise from elsewhere while preserving its own strategic remit. Conversely, a common service can share genuinely common provision without treating every mastery as interchangeable. The Method compares these conditions and their whole costs.

Stop when the current or selected arrangement is sufficient for the next use. Return to ME.6 when the work, capacity, provider, mandate or means change enough to alter that answer. A Method architecture does not itself appoint staff or grant powers.

## Development.Reference:5 - Which directions can we undertake together?

Research, products, people, infrastructure and community work can be independently worthwhile while competing for the same real performers. Ranking each direction separately can promise incompatible work or charge the same common provision several times.

[DOCA.10](DEVELOPMENT-AND-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#doca10---construct-a-feasible-development-portfolio-with-shared-or-branch-specific-support) constructs shared versus branch-specific support, joint demand and feasible combinations. It retains scientific and editorial ends and decisions, and shows when an existing Strategy result already suffices. The [Strategy DPF](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Development%20DPF%20Suite/STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) supplies direction, offer, programme and bounded commitment Methods; [Research Method Practice](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Development%20DPF%20Suite/RESEARCH-METHOD-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) supplies inquiry and its warranted stop.

For a development provider, this means comparing its own development, a shared Method library and independent scientific contributions, B2C/B2B provision, client projects and people/AI/community work alongside current obligations. One real performer has one capacity in the comparison, even when that person fills several roles. Independent mathematical inquiry with no material shared dependency need not acquire a portfolio or office merely to use a development Method. Stop with the sufficient bounded decision; reopen the affected combination when a shared condition changes.

## Development.Reference:6 - How does a proposal become feasible joint work?

An accepted strategy does not supply all the contributions needed to enact it. Nor does telling people about the strategy resolve objections or make incompatible promises feasible.

[Leadership](LEADERSHIP-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) supplies four connected Methods. LDR.1 obtains and continues a missing leadership contribution. LDR.2 negotiates complete feasible contributions. LDR.3 constructs a useful answer through substantive discussion. LDR.4 prepares a proposal through decision, commitments, a mutually understood start and first useful work. This last contribution carries propitka: trusted judgement and examination of consequences can change the proposal before commitment, and first use tests what the agreement actually provided.

Read [LDR.4](LEADERSHIP-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ldr4---prepare-a-proposal-for-decision-and-first-use) when that whole transition is missing. Open one of the other Methods directly when only its contribution is needed. The independent-community case makes voluntary contribution and refusal visible; no common employer is presumed. An authority or commitment must obtain in the actual setting. Ordinary sufficient coordination needs no special preparation campaign.

## Development.Reference:7 - What help, learning and cultural continuation are needed?

These are different questions with different sufficient answers.

| Missing contribution | Useful entry and result | Ordinary boundary or return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Help through which an interpretable practice becomes workable | [Practice Implementation Support](PRACTICE-IMPLEMENTATION-SUPPORT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md), IS.1–5: a bounded help relationship, diagnosis, support strategy, introduction/adaptation and continuation or end | An unknown development direction returns to DOCA; an unknown subject Method returns to its field or Method Engineering. |
| A person must become capable, rather than receive a service | [Human Capability Development](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Development%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md): learning purpose, prepared practice, feedback and transfer | Competent provider performance is not evidence of the recipient's learning; adequate existing competence may need only access or support. |
| Useful participation, relationships or joint contributions | [Community Management](COMMUNITY-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md), starting at CB.1 or the known missing Method | Do not create a community when independent use of an available resource is enough. Employment does not by itself establish community participation or voluntary mandate. |
| A practice must continue among further carriers, including independent people, collectives or AI | [C.36](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c36---cultural-evolution-and-cultural-evolution-engineering) and [C.36.RP](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c36rp---sustain-and-renew-shared-ways-of-working), with the subject practice's actual sources | Transmission or participation counts do not establish reconstruction, use or capacity to continue. Follow the direct cultural Method even when there is no community organizer. |

For a culture of development, combine the actual cultural continuation contribution with DOCA.9's development result. Reproducing a training activity or one mastered Method does not show that further carriers can notice a consequential difficulty or possibility, construct a worthwhile development problem, and organize the next attainable transition. A local helping undertaking can nevertheless finish legitimately before that larger culture claim is established.

## Development.Reference:8 - Can these Methods produce a coherent project document package?

Use these Methods to construct the content needed for a coherent project package. For example, a development provider may need a connected concept, strategy, project architecture and programmes for its own development and capacity to help clients. Scientific inquiry, a shared Method library, B2C/B2B provision and community contributions can have independent ends while sharing means.

[DOCA.9:4](DEVELOPMENT-AND-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-PRACTICE-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#doca94---solution) constructs the development undertaking and keeps its intended changes and receiving results connected. Sections 4.4–4.9 connect alternatives, organization, performed contributions and further development, then give local work the Method language it needs. Use [STR.6](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Development%20DPF%20Suite/STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str6---construct-strategic-option-families-or-develop-reusable-material-for-strategic-exploration) for complete strategic alternatives, [STR.8:4.2a](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Development%20DPF%20Suite/STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str842a---explain-why-the-proposed-changes-would-work-together) to explain why changes and already available contributions work together, and [STR.11](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Development%20DPF%20Suite/STRATEGY-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#str11---select-strategy-portfolio-commitments-and-assumptions) for bounded commitments.

For organization work, use OCE's Methods for [concept alternatives](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#oce3---generate-and-compare-organization-concepts), [contribution architecture](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#oce4---design-an-organizations-contribution-architecture), [paired product and organization decisions](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#oce7---coordinate-product-or-service-and-organization-architecture-decisions), and [bounded realization](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#oce9---realize-a-bounded-organization-capability-increment). RMP supplies warranted inquiry; HCD, a human learning or development programme; CB, a community arrangement; and Practice Implementation Support, a helping undertaking. ME.6 connects the performance and provision arrangements, and DOCA.10 tests joint feasibility where shared means make that question real. Leadership supplies missing joint contributions to formulation and enactment.

The resulting documents must agree about the subjects, commitments, means and contributions on which they rely. A provider's planned service, work actually delivered and a recipient's new capability remain separate claims. Company assistance stays within the agreed client remit; scientific choice retains its independent criteria.

Stop with the content needed for the next responsible action. When a required content construction is absent from these suppliers, report the missing Method or connection. [The local realization question](#developmentreference10---how-does-the-selected-practice-become-usable-locally) concerns putting those Methods into usable local practice, not relabeling every project document an LPF.

## Development.Reference:9 - What changes when capable AI becomes inexpensive?

Recompare the whole arrangement. A qualified agent may supply inquiry, source recovery, construction, explanation, practice feedback or an authorized decision. The changed viable division of work can remove a specialist bottleneck or an unnecessary office.

Use [ME.6's AI case](../Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/METHOD-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#me656---capable-ai-changes-the-whole-arrangement-not-just-the-coachs-speed) and [C.11.DUA](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c11dua---make-advice-and-evidence-demands-worth-their-burden). Include the actual capability, access, context, powers, qualification, shared capacity and consequences of error. Remove human duplication only when the replacement supplies the contribution on which the receiving result depends. Do not retain people merely because an earlier arrangement had them; do not assume a general capability claim supplies the required local judgement or authorization.

If the intended gain is a human's mastery or self-development, AI performing all of the work can defeat that gain even when the immediate output improves. If the gain is an externally supplied result, requiring the human to learn the provider's Method may add waste. Return to the intended result, then change the performance and support arrangement. The worked costs in these Methods are prospective models, not measured universal savings.

## Development.Reference:10 - How does the selected practice become usable locally?

To realize a selected proposal, the responsible performers need usable Methods, actual means and powers, sufficient preparation, commitments and an entry into real work.

Follow [ME.6:4.9](../Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/METHOD-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#me649---give-the-selected-realization-its-exact-contribution-and-conditions) to the actual missing contribution. ME.10 makes Method material usable; OCE.9 realizes an organizational change; Practice Implementation Support provides and adapts the helping undertaking; HCD develops a person's needed capability. An adequate already available contribution ends its branch. A changed result or dependency returns to the supplying Method and the affected whole.

Use [E.4.DPF](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#e4dpf---domain-and-local-principle-frameworks-whether-and-how-to-author-and-publish) when a bounded local practice needs an LPF language. Keep local decisions and examples distinct from the general Method, retain its supplier and conditions, and revise local guidance when those conditions change. A hierarchy of projects does not imply a hierarchy of copied frameworks. A service description, programme, assignment or architecture decision remains its own kind of document.

## Development.Reference:11 - Where do the answers come from, and when should they change?

The [Suite account](README.md#membership) identifies the membership and its separate constitution rules. This Reference selects questions and direct contributions for the stated use; it does not reproduce all member content or claim that all members are necessary for every answer. Core Methods, adjacent disciplines and underlying sources are returned where they matter.

The form adapts [E.11.DSG](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#e11dsg---dpf-suite-reference): begin with a working situation, give a short answer or honest stop, and make the complete supplying Methods available as the question requires. Instructional how-to guidance is the relevant alternative when the reader needs a procedure for a selected task. Here the reader may first need to identify the missing result or supplier, and direct use of a known sufficient DPF remains available. This is a bounded basis for the Reference form; claims about the Methods' effectiveness retain their own evidence requirements. Reconsider the form when another arrangement gives these readers a more truthful or less burdensome answer.

The complete patterns retain their problem, forces, developed solution, examples, evidence limits and source choices. The office-form literature, implementation-support literature, leadership sources and role-community essays contribute at different evidence strengths. Their interpretations and limits are stated in the actual supplying patterns; a recent date, organizational label or illustrative success is not evidence of universal effectiveness.

A changed source or failed use reopens the claim and connection it can alter. A new product edition does not silently change a saved historical answer. If a cited publication is unavailable or the relevant candidate is not yet admitted, the affected answer remains provisional; an accessible title is not a substitute for its full Method. Use another sufficient direct source when available, or retain the specific missing contribution and the condition for retry.
