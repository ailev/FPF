# Domain Principle Frameworks

**Find a field's methods for the result you need.** These 33 frameworks explain domain practice through connected patterns, with conditions, worked cases and source grounds. Use them for a current task or as contributions to continuing development; applying a method once does not require a development programme.

If you know the field, choose a Suite or framework below. If you only know the difficulty, describe it in ordinary words and search the texts; [Using FPF Library](../USING-FPF.md#choose-what-to-read) explains how to find and connect useful contributions. The [Library introduction](../) gives the overall purpose and a first-use route.

Framework and method links open readable text on fpf.tools; **Markdown** links open the repository source. GitHub previews can truncate large files. Check the [displayed source edition](https://fpf.tools/status) when the version matters.

## Combine only the contributions your work needs

For example, a decision may require a research finding, a mathematical model and an explanation to its participants. Decision Design, Research Method Practice, Foundational Thinking and Explanation Design then supply different results. Read the relevant conditions and connect those results; you do not need to study every framework or use every pattern in sequence.

When the purpose is to expand an agent's capabilities and continue to further attainable problems, use [Core's connected development method (C.40.OD)](https://fpf.tools/C.40.OD) with the relevant domain methods. The particular changes needed by a person, AI, team or organization differ.

Every full framework has one home in the Library. Using its methods elsewhere does not add a second membership. An embedded Framework Architectural Reference explains its pattern language; Suite References explain the larger disciplinary assemblies. The [Library Architectural Reference](../FPF-LIBRARY-REFERENCE.md) explains the boundaries and connections across them.

## Reading and reuse

Before applying the methods, follow [Using FPF Library](../USING-FPF.md). A folder alone does not contain external frameworks its methods may need. [FPF Core](https://fpf.tools/FPF) supplies the common concepts and transdisciplinary methods.
