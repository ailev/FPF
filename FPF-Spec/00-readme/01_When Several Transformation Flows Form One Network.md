Inspect [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/17_18_Transformation Flow Structure/04_E.18.NET - Network of Transformation-Flow Structures|E.18.NET]] when the current question joins independently identified transformation-flow structures through exact direct relation occurrences. Several stages, several paths, or several valuations of one flow do not make a network.

- **Build the builder.** A compiler-source-change flow, a bootstrap-compiler build flow, an application build flow, and a deployment-and-operation flow keep their own changes, work, positions, and local state. Recover the project's exact relation that says which executable compiler is used for the application build and its exact delivery relation to deployment. A selected tool-build network can itself be a member of a selected release network; follow a finite member path rather than inventing “level 2” and “level 3” kinds. Inspect [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/17_18_Transformation Flow Structure/04_E.18.NET - Network of Transformation-Flow Structures|E.18.NET]]; if the case is only one flow with several stages or valuations, stay in [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/17_18_Transformation Flow Structure/00_E.18 - Transformation Flow Structure|E.18]].
- **Change a product and its production system.** Product-development Work changes and verifies the product definition; production-system-change Work redesigns, retools, and qualifies equipment; production Work makes units; operation supplies observations that may inform later product-development Work. Treat their transformation-flow structures as separately identified selected structures: the structures organize positions and exact relations but do not perform the Work. Keep the product and production system distinct, and recover the exact release, readiness, production, and observation relations needed by the current case. A selected product-development network and a selected production-system-change network can be members of a larger selected network without becoming one giant flow. Inspect [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/17_18_Transformation Flow Structure/04_E.18.NET - Network of Transformation-Flow Structures|E.18.NET]]; if only one architecture correspondence is current, use its direct architecture pattern instead.

When the immediate question is whether dated Work actually occurred or whether an entity was actually produced, leave the network overview and inspect the direct owners. [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_Role-Method-Work Alignment/01_A.15.01 - U.Work|A.15.1]] identifies one `U.Work` occurrence from its actual performer `U.System`, exact covering `U.RoleAssignment`, `performedUnderAssignment(W, RA)`, enacted method, temporal extent, and containing system; a log, ticket, or record is not that occurrence. After that Work and the required direct subject basis are exact, [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_Role-Method-Work Alignment/07_A.15.PROD - Production Work, Entity-Identity Inception, and Production Completion Recovery|A.15.PROD]] separately tests production-work participation, entity-identity inception, and production completion. Missing Work granularity, a direct production-participation governor, identity-specification or inception basis, completion criterion or applicability, boundary or composition facts, or required substrate is a branch-local blocker. Actual change, output or result, identifier allocation, evidence, delivery, and acceptance remain separate claims and none proves those three production claims by itself.
