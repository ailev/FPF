# Engineering DPF Suite Reference

> A methodological reference for using FPF and domain patterns to answer engineering, management, and practice-development questions.

- **Author:** Anatoly Levenchuk, with AI-assisted development and review
- **Version:** 2 September 2026
- **Status:** Eternal alpha: a published working reference, already used for cross-DPF selection and case analysis, and extended as the Suite develops.
- **Publication:** [FPF repository](https://github.com/ailev/FPF)

Begin with the decision or action that must happen next. This Reference helps engineers, engineering managers, Method engineers, practice developers, and assisting Systems find a useful first answer, combine relevant contributions, or identify the result still missing.

Use the Table of Contents below to find a situation, a working question, or a source DPF. The four worked situations show how to apply the available methods and which decisions still need evidence or a specialist result. The direct question-to-result index supports quicker lookup; each DPF's own Table of Contents gives the fuller pattern repertoire. Open the relevant pattern and apply its Problem frame, Solution, worked cases, and checklist to your case.

This is a methodological reference organized for lookup and application. Instructional Guides are separate learning products organized for explanation, exercises, and development of the learner's abilities. The examples here are selected entry points; use the source DPFs directly for other questions.

The source set includes complete releases of Method Engineering, Systems Engineering, Music and Dance Practice Engineering, and Organization Change Engineering, together with partial releases of Operations Management and Human Capability Development. The [source overview](#what-the-sources-can-supply) explains how to use these publications and their examples.

## Use this Reference

Use this Reference when several DPFs may contribute to your question, when you are unsure which DPF applies, or when a needed method or result is missing. For a question already answered by one known pattern, go directly to that pattern.

Start with a sentence such as: "We must decide which greenhouse-control arrangement to obtain" or "We need to understand what limits this engineer's work." Find the nearest question below, open the cited pattern, and apply its method to your case. Bring the result back to the decision that prompted the search. Add another pattern when its contribution changes that decision.

The examples distinguish **method guidance**, **evidence from a constructed case**, and **a result obtained in your project**. A pattern explains how to do the work; a worked example shows one application under stated conditions. Your conclusion depends on the evidence and decision authority in your own case.

When an input is missing, say which conclusion needs it and what would make it usable. Use [A.15.9](../../../_current-pattern-hosts/A.15.9-Request-and-Use-a-Bounded-Result-from-Another-Practice.md) to obtain a bounded specialist result when needed. Pause the dependent decision and continue work that does not rely on that input.

## Should we scale a partner-Dance event after one successful showcase?

A Salsa practice team has one exciting rehearsed showcase and is considering a larger festival slot. The intended use is social Dance among unfamiliar partners.

**First useful answer.** Decide the team's next development Work through [MDPE.7](../../dpf-evolutionary-stylistics-and-style-engineering/MUSIC-AND-DANCE-PRACTICE-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-BRIEF-AND-VARIANT-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/MUSIC-AND-DANCE-PRACTICE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#mdpe7---choose-the-next-music-or-dance-development-work). Specify the decision, who may make it, the materially different candidate Works, comparison criteria, constraints, and evidence. A rehearsed showcase alone does not establish unfamiliar-partner transfer, so that claim cannot yet justify the larger slot.

### Choose the next Work and its evidence

[MDPE.7 - Choose the Next Music-or-Dance Development Work](../../dpf-evolutionary-stylistics-and-style-engineering/MUSIC-AND-DANCE-PRACTICE-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-BRIEF-AND-VARIANT-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/MUSIC-AND-DANCE-PRACTICE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#mdpe7---choose-the-next-music-or-dance-development-work) explains how to choose now, probe again, reject the candidate set, or return to another current question. The useful result is the team's own choice with its grounds and revisit condition. Use the comparison to decide whether a probe is worthwhile and what it should test.

The [constructed social-Dance and live-Music development application](../../dpf-evolutionary-stylistics-and-style-engineering/MUSIC-AND-DANCE-PRACTICE-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-BRIEF-AND-VARIANT-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/MUSIC-AND-DANCE-PRACTICE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#a-constructed-social-dance-and-live-music-development-decision) shows one case that combined characterization, architecture, support-environment testing, observation, local change, and choice. The team in that example selected an unfamiliar-partner trial and deferred the larger slot. The one-month trial was selected for the conditions of that constructed case; choose the receiving team's next Work from that team's conditions.

If the team chooses observation Work, arrange the authorized probe and observe the named population under the intended partner and support conditions. Keep failures as well as successes. Use those observations for the subsequent transfer and scale decision.

## Which greenhouse-control obtaining arrangement should survive?

A small equipment company needs climate control for a new greenhouse configuration. It is comparing a ready controller, a commissioned custom controller, internal development with AI assistance, and a ready controller with an internal supervisory layer.

**First useful answer.** Use [SYSE.24](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse24---choose-how-the-project-will-obtain-a-needed-engineering-result) to compare whole arrangements for obtaining the same accepted result. Include who performs the necessary Work, the Methods and means, integration, configuration, assurance, support, capability, resource burden, access, and exit. These details make the alternatives comparable.

### Establish the comparison and the choice

Start with the crops, climate, actuators, operating conditions, and acceptance thresholds for the real greenhouse. [SYSE.1](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse1---choose-and-reopen-the-project-system-of-interest) and [SYSE.16](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse16---recover-the-systems-and-conditions-needed-for-a-use-decision) help identify the climate-control System and the users, providers, surroundings, and fallback conditions that matter to its use. Obtain the actual control thresholds from the applicable domain practice.

The [GH-2 worked application](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#app-syse-04--worked-application-obtain-climate-control-for-a-new-greenhouse-configuration) shows the four arrangements, a common comparison basis, two remaining candidates, and a protected interface-and-fallback replay. Its stated price-and-burden rule selects the ready controller plus an internal supervisory layer. This demonstrates the comparison; a procurement recommendation for your greenhouse requires your offers, configuration, evidence, and authority.

| Input that can change the choice | What to obtain for the real project |
| --- | --- |
| Control performance and protected fallback | Configuration-specific control thresholds, electrical-safety limits, permission to conduct the challenge, and fallback evidence. |
| Commercial feasibility and continuing access | Actual price and funding constraints; terms for access, updates, data, duties, remedies, and exit over the intended horizon. |
| Work allocation and capability | An organizational arrangement for human, AI, and provider contributions, with assignments, authority, and capability evidence. When internal capability must be developed, identify whose capability must change, the representative Work, and the needed development result. |
| Continued operation and maintenance | The maintenance and operating results needed for support, recovery, reliability, and any queue, capacity, or whole-service commitment. The available OPS.5 through OPS.7 methods answer narrower operating decisions. |

Use the available qualified results first. Request a missing specialist result through A.15.9 when it could reverse the decision. Run the authorized replay on the real candidate configuration and have the responsible chooser record the resulting choice.

### Continue from the chosen arrangement

Use [SYSE.3](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse3---develop-the-recursive-realization-network) for realization, [SYSE.13](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse13---establish-configuration-identity-variants-and-effectivity) for configuration identity, [SYSE.10](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse10---assess-research-model-and-trial-results-for-an-engineering-decision) for evidence use, [SYSE.4](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse4---select-an-engineering-challenge-and-qualify-evidence-use) for assurance, and [SYSE.14](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse14---make-a-release-decision-for-named-engineering-work-or-use) for release as those decisions become current. Start these decisions from the chosen arrangement and obtain the result needed for each one. The GH-2 example leaves one realization question open: can the internal integration Agent configure the supervisory layer and produce commissioning evidence before the need date?

Open [ME.1](../METHOD-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-COMPLETION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/METHOD-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#me1---choose-and-reopen-the-project-method-of-interest) if the integration, commissioning, control-development, or assurance Method itself needs engineering. Once the real arrangement and operating account are established, [OPS.5](../OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-FIRST-EDITION-CONTENT-DRR-AND-OPS-A-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ops-5) can bound starts, [OPS.6](../OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-FIRST-EDITION-CONTENT-DRR-AND-OPS-A-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ops-6) can guide evidenced continuation of an admitted case, and [OPS.7](../OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-FIRST-EDITION-CONTENT-DRR-AND-OPS-A-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ops-7) can address local priority or an existing commitment.

**Stop or continue.** Pause a decision at the first missing input that could reverse it. Obtain that input and rerun the affected comparison; preserve conclusions whose premises remain unchanged.

## What should a small engineering team develop, obtain, or delegate?

A small engineering startup wants to improve its ability to obtain engineering results and compete in its market. Candidate moves include developing current members, recruiting or assigning another performer, using providers, improving a Method or platform, and changing the allocation of Work among human, AI, robotic, and hybrid Systems.

**First useful answer.** Name one needed engineering result and the representative Work that produces it. Then apply [OCE.8](../ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-CONTENT-DRR-AND-FOUNDATION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#oce-8) to compare whole organizational arrangements for obtaining that result. The method can yield a choice, a probe, rejection of the candidates, or a statement of the evidence still needed. The startup still needs case evidence before it can select an arrangement.

### Prepare a comparison that can support a decision

Use [ME.1](../METHOD-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-COMPLETION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/METHOD-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#me1---choose-and-reopen-the-project-method-of-interest) and [ME.3](../METHOD-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-COMPLETION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/METHOD-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#me3---build-situational-method-requirements-and-fit-criteria) if the Method or its required contribution is unclear; use [ME.6](../METHOD-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-COMPLETION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/METHOD-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#me6---compare-method-architecture-alternatives-and-simultaneous-enactment-conflicts) when competing Method architectures affect the choice. For engineering-result and repertoire questions, use [SYSE.15](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse15---choose-and-refresh-the-engineering-methods-needed-by-a-project) and [SYSE.24](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse24---choose-how-the-project-will-obtain-a-needed-engineering-result). [SYSE.12](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse12---develop-an-engineering-platform-for-practitioner-work) helps investigate a platform limit; [SYSE.20](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse20---reconcile-overlapping-engineering-work-and-required-order) helps investigate conflicts among overlapping engineering Work.

Recover the existing arrangement from representative Work: contributors, their capabilities and assignments, interfaces, permissions, authority, failures, support burden, and effects on others. Keep the target result, use, situation, horizon, acceptance basis, and protected conditions comparable across the candidates. [C.38](../../../_current-pattern-hosts/C.38-Construct-Comparable-Ways-to-Obtain-One-Result.md) supplies the general whole-arrangement comparison method; OCE.8 applies it to the organizational choice.

The following are candidate families to investigate. Develop actual alternatives only where their contributions address the needed result.

| Candidate family | What the comparison needs |
| --- | --- |
| Develop current members | Identified members and representative Work they would later perform; current capability envelopes, transfer conditions, time and opportunity cost; an appropriate human-development result when people are developed. |
| Recruit or assign another performer | The proposed performer's eligibility and actual capability, assignment scope, authority, resources, integration needs, and contribution-interface evidence. |
| Obtain provider expertise | Provider capability and duty, acceptance evidence, data and knowledge custody, continued access, support, recovery, exit, and the receiving team's remaining Work. |
| Change a Method, interface, or platform | Evidence of the limiting result, the candidate change, a representative trial, and the burden moved to other contributors. |
| Allocate bounded Work to human, AI, robotic, or hybrid Systems | Capability and action-specific agency, assignments, permissions, authority, interfaces, oversight, failure return, configuration identity, security and safety limits, and observations of actual Work. |

### Make the organizational choice and carry it into Work

Use OCE.8's comparison and choice rules with the participants and authority needed for the decision. Return the compared arrangements, evidence, selected choice or probe, conditions, and revisit point. When an input is missing, return the particular claim it blocks and obtain that input.

Use [A.2.2](../../../_current-pattern-hosts/A.2.2-U.Capability-System-Ability-Envelope-and-Measures.md) for holder-specific capability claims, [E.23.CDI](../../../_current-pattern-hosts/E.23.CDI-Developing-Capability-for-a-Named-Work-Family.md) for a development question, and [A.15.8](../../../_current-pattern-hosts/A.15.8-Work-Performance-Configuration-and-Recovery-Testing.md) when the performing configuration or recovery matters. [A.13](../../../FPF-Spec.md#a13---the-agential-role--agency-spectrum) and [A.15.1](../../../_current-pattern-hosts/A.15.1-U.Work.md) supply the performer and actual-Work distinctions used by these claims. Use HCD's available methods for the human demand, diagnosis, and profile questions.

Obtain target-domain acceptance and consequence criteria for the product being engineered. Request a strategy, finance, legal, governance, safety, security, maintenance, or wider Operations result only when it can change the arrangement decision. Name the missing contribution in the request. The organization remains responsible for its choice.

After the arrangement, Work, performer permissions, and operating account are established, use [OPS.5](../OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-FIRST-EDITION-CONTENT-DRR-AND-OPS-A-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ops-5), [OPS.6](../OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-FIRST-EDITION-CONTENT-DRR-AND-OPS-A-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ops-6), and [OPS.7](../OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-FIRST-EDITION-CONTENT-DRR-AND-OPS-A-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/OPERATIONS-MANAGEMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#ops-7) for admission, exceptional-case continuation, and local priority or commitment revision respectively.

**Stop or continue.** Do not begin dependent Work while its required capability, assignment, permission, authority, or protected-condition evidence is missing. Obtain that result or choose a bounded authorized probe. The useful stopping point is a comparison and decision for which the organization is responsible, or an explicit unresolved premise that the next piece of work can address.

## What can be concluded now about Engineer-E27’s development need for ReleaseWork-R9?

Engineer-E27 is preparing for prospective 2026-Q4 ReleaseWork-R9. The question is what the engineer must be able to contribute and what, if anything, needs development.

**First useful answer.** Use [HCD.1](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd-1) to state a **prospective demand hypothesis**: ReleaseWork-R9 may require Engineer-E27 to challenge AI output, reason about configuration impacts, return evidence, coordinate specialist contributions, detect critical errors, and adapt across unfamiliar mixed physical and software variants. The case currently lacks the observations needed for a qualified current-demand conclusion, a limiting-target diagnosis, or a current capability-profile decision.

### Move from a hypothesis to an evidenced target

Use [HCD.1](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd-1) to say what the later Work could require Engineer-E27 to do under the stated configuration and conditions, with the evidence and uncertainty for that claim. Relate the claim to the decision being considered. Gather representative observations that can test the hypothesis. A qualified set of representative cases for Engineer-E27, under conditions compatible with the proposed Work, can test the hypothesis before ReleaseWork-R9 itself occurs.

Use [HCD.3](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd-3) to distinguish a human capability, misconception, or behaviour that can be changed from a demand outside the intended envelope, an unsuitable Method, tool or support failure, assignment or authority problem, organizational limit, missing specialist contribution, or an access, adaptation, or enactment problem. Choose a safe observation that discriminates the live alternatives. Return a qualified target, a non-training cause, or the evidence still needed to distinguish the alternatives. This is a development diagnosis; medical diagnosis or intervention needs the relevant qualified practice.

Use [HCD.4](HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#hcd-4) to compare current and target capability claims across the engineer's simultaneous Work. Keep the evidence for each claim, support conditions, critical-error limits, uncertainty, interfaces, trade-offs, and Engineer-E27's participation visible. A bounded profile choice uses the C.11 decision inputs required there: the decision subject, existing alternatives, comparison basis, beliefs, outcome model, choice rule, whether a further probe is worthwhile, participation, and authority.

Name the representative occurrences that supply the observations. |
| Current engineering-culture enactment, a bounded practice-change decision, retained alternatives, or culture-related evidence gaps | A compatible [SYSE.21](../SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-FIRST-EDITION-AUTHORING-CAMPAIGN/SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#syse21---deliberately-continue-and-change-systems-engineering-culture) result for Engineer-E27, the Method, Work, period, and use, or a qualified direct substitute permitted by the receiving HCD method. |

## Using several patterns together

### Keep the decision and each contribution visible

Combine patterns around the result your project needs. Use FPF for a transdisciplinary move such as making alternatives comparable or obtaining a specialist contribution. Use the applicable DPF or specialist practice for the domain method, threshold, evidence, and acceptance conditions. Identify who may make the final decision, especially when a supplier's result is one input to it.

Use Method Engineering when the way of working is itself the problem. A pattern, a whole DPF, or an instructional Guide can describe methods for different uses. Choose descriptions and representations for the use you need: lookup, explanation, comparison, or enactment support. During actual Work, a performer uses a method and produces the case results; the support arrangement makes the needed tools, materials, and access available.

### Use several views when they answer different questions

Project management, process management, and case management can describe the same Work from different viewpoints. Recover the actual Work and select each view for its intended use. Reconcile the views at the shared Work and decisions.

### Find the Suite and publication details when you need them

The Engineering DPF Suite groups related DPF products. Its [architecture decisions](../DPF-ARCHITECTURE-CAMPAIGN/CROSS-PRODUCT-ARCHITECTURE-DRR.md) describe the product boundaries; the [publication account](../ENGINEERING-DPF-SUITE-GUIDE-FIRST-EDITION-CAMPAIGN/CURRENT-REALIZATION-ACCOUNT.md) identifies available releases and the current Reference. Consult those sources for a product-scope or availability question. The Reference's selected examples cover only part of the methods available in the Suite.

## When a source or your case changes

Check the source publication's date and the cited pattern when a decision depends on exact wording or a previous result. If a newer source, changed configuration, new observation, or specialist result alters an input, reopen the affected conclusion and its dependent decisions. Retain conclusions whose evidence and applicability still hold.

For a missing input, identify the result you need, its supplying practice, and the conditions under which you can use it. Obtain that result and retry the dependent decision. If a cited source is unavailable or no longer fits the question, use an applicable direct pattern or qualified specialist source, and keep the unsupported conclusion open.

Finish the lookup when the current question has a usable answer or a specific unresolved input that determines the next work. A known DPF can be used directly at any point.
